Start & finish

Preview

The first week ends with a summit finish. After a flat start across the Pontine plain, the GPMs at Bellegra and Colle Ferro soften the legs before the 13.9 km climb from Subiaco to Monte Livata (6.9%, max 17%), whose steepest section – 10.4% on average – comes in the first 4 km. A key test for the maglia rosa contenders before the first rest day.
