Preview

A largely flat stage from Lombardy into the Veneto via Solferino and the plains around Verona. The sting is in the tail: in the Euganean Hills the short, steep muri of Passo Roverello and Castelnuovo, the latter cresting 9 km from the finish in Vo', could undo the sprinters' plans.
