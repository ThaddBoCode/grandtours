Start & finish

Preview

4,900 m of climbing on the roads of Il Lombardia: Madonna del Ghisallo, Culmine di San Pietro, Miragolo San Salvatore and Alino come in quick succession before the summit finish at Piani di Bobbio. The final climb via Valtorta is 10.1 km at 9.0% with ramps of 18% – the hardest test of the race so far, with the time trial waiting the next day.
