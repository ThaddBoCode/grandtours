Start & finish

Preview

This year's Tappa Bartali brings a new summit finish at Passo dei Due Santi in the Zum Zeri ski area. After the Garfagnana and Foce Carpinelli, the race drops into the Lunigiana before climbing again from Pontremoli; the final ascent is 8.7 km at 8.7% (max 13%) and the last 2.4 km average 11.6% – terrain for pure climbers.
