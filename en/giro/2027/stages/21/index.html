Preview

The traditional finale in the capital for the fifth year in a row: 131 km, completely flat, heading out towards Ostia before eight laps of a city circuit of around 9 km past Rome's monuments. A last bunch sprint for the stage, while the maglia rosa lifts the Trofeo Senza Fine.
