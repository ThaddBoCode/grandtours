Preview

The queen stage: 192 km and 5,700 m of climbing with the Mortirolo from Mazzo di Valtellina (12.8 km at 10.1%, the Montagna Pantani) twice and, in between, the Passo di Gavia – the Cima Coppi at 2,618 m, back for the first time since 2014 (16.5 km at 8.0%). The second Mortirolo summit is 33.3 km from the line, followed by the descent to Edolo and the uphill run to Aprica – the Giro can be turned upside down here.
