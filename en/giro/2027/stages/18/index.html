Start & finish

Preview

An all-Trentino mountain stage with 4,450 m of climbing: past Lake Garda to Passo Ballino, then a circuit of around 50 km between Ponte Arche and Spiazzo with two ascents of Passo Durone and the tough Passo Daone. The final climb from Pinzolo to Madonna di Campiglio is more regular – 12.5 km at 5.7% – but after this much climbing, attacks from the GC men are likely.
