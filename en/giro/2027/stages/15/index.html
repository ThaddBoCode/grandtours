Preview

A flat, fast 37.3 km time trial starting inside the Autodromo Nazionale Monza and finishing in Milan, via Concorezzo, Cernusco sul Naviglio and Piazzale Loreto. A day for the specialists, while the climbers must limit their losses the day after Piani di Bobbio; Filippo Ganna won the last two time trials into Milan in 2020 and 2021.
