Who wears pink in Rome? The contenders, the defending champions and the stages that matter.

Tadej Pogačar

UAE Team Emirates XRG · Born 1998
1×Giro wins · 2024
–Giro 2026

Won the Giro on his only start in 2024, by 9:56. He has named the Tour, the Worlds and the Vuelta as his 2027 goals but says he would rather ride the Giro – the Trieste start with two Slovenian stages is seen as a lure. Not confirmed: UAE will decide at the December camp.

Jonas Vingegaard

Visma–Lease a Bike · Born 1996
1×Giro wins · 2026
1.Giro 2026

Defending champion: won on his 2026 debut by 5:22 with five stage wins, completing his set of all three Grand Tours. His 2027 programme is undecided and Cyclingnews does not expect him back. A defence would make him the first back-to-back winner since Induráin in 1992–93.

🧗 Climbers⏱ Time triallists

Paul Seixas

Decathlon CMA CGM · Born 2006
–Giro wins
–Giro 2026

Fourth on his Tour debut in 2026, aged 19, and winner of Il Lombardia. He has never ridden the Giro but calls a debut there possible, with the home Worlds in Haute-Savoie his priority. Only speculated – the decision is due at Decathlon's December camp.

🧗 Climbers⏱ Time triallists

Antonio Tiberi

Team Bahrain Victorious · Born 2001
–Giro wins · Best Giro result: 5. (2024)
–Giro 2026

Fifth and best young rider in 2024. Stage 9 to Monte Livata runs on his home roads in Lazio, and he wants to start. Bahrain sports director Franco Pellizotti says Tiberi is on the team's Giro programme for now – announced, to be finalised at the December camp.

🧗 Climbers⏱ Time triallists

Felix Gall

Decathlon CMA CGM · Born 1998
–Giro wins
2.Giro 2026

Runner-up in 2026, 5:22 behind Vingegaard and the strongest climber behind him. Eurosport's Jeroen Vanbelleghem names him among the riders who will like the eight uphill finishes. No word yet on his 2027 programme – speculated.

🧗 Climbers

Remco Evenepoel

Red Bull–Bora–Hansgrohe · Born 2000
–Giro wins
–Giro 2026

Both his Giro starts ended early: in 2021 after a crash, in 2023 with Covid while in the maglia rosa. Cyclingnews says organisers hope to attract him, but pundits advise him to focus on a Tour with 71 km of time trials. Participation only speculated.

Florian Lipowitz

Red Bull–Bora–Hansgrohe · Born 2000
–Giro wins
–Giro 2026

Third at the 2025 Tour; his only Giro start, in 2024, ended after stage 5. With Evenepoel likely to lead Red Bull at the Tour, Jens Voigt thinks the Giro offers him the best chance of a first Grand Tour win. Only speculated – nothing has been decided.
