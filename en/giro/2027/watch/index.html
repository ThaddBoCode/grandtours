Spectator guide

Where to watch the Giro, roughly when the riders come by – and every stage for your calendar.

📅 Whole Giro to calendar 185 days to the Grand Départ 🕐 Start times not yet published

Tips for your day at the roadside

  1. Be early: the route is usually closed to traffic several hours before the race.
  2. The publicity caravan passes about 1½ to 2 hours before the riders – half the fun for many.
  3. On climbs the riders are slower and the bunch splits up – that's where you see the most.
  4. The finish area is the busiest. For more space, pick a spot a few kilometres earlier.
  5. In time trials riders start one at a time – you see them all, but spread over hours.

General experience. Binding schedules and road closures are published by the organiser and local authorities before the race.
