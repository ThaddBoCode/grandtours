Stage 19 / 21 · Friday, 24 May 2024

Mortegliano →Sappada

Hilly 157 km 🏆 Andrea Vendrame
Review

Vendrame solos to victory in Sappada

From a 19-rider breakaway containing four stage winners of this Giro, Andrea Vendrame attacked on the descent of the Sella Valcalda. He held off Georg Steinhauser and Pelayo Sánchez on the Cima Sappada and won by 54 seconds over Sánchez; the maglia rosa group finished almost 16 minutes down.
