Stage 2 / 21 · Saturday, 9 May 2026

Burgas →Veliko Tarnovo

Hilly 221 km 🏆 Guillermo Thomas Silva
Review

Thomas Silva wins in Veliko Tarnovo and makes history

Around ten riders crashed on a slippery road 23 km from the finish; Santiago Buitrago, Jay Vine and Marc Soler abandoned. Jonas Vingegaard countered an attack by Christian Scaroni on the final climb, but a small group sprinted for the win, taken by Guillermo Thomas Silva (XDS Astana) ahead of Florian Stork and Giulio Ciccone. Silva took the maglia rosa and maglia bianca, the first Uruguayan to win a Giro stage and wear pink.
