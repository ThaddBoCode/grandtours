Stage 10 / 21 · Tuesday, 13 October 2020

Lanciano →Tortoreto

Hilly 177 km 🏆 Peter Sagan
Review

Sagan finally wins a Giro stage

Before the start in Lanciano, Mitchelton–Scott and Team Jumbo–Visma pulled out after positive COVID-19 tests, and Michael Matthews was also gone. Peter Sagan, part of the break, attacked on the steepest part of the climb at Tortoreto and won solo for his first Giro stage, ahead of Brandon McNulty and João Almeida, who gained four bonus seconds.
