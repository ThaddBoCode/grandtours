Stage 7 / 21 · Friday, 13 May 2022

Diamante →Potenza

Hilly 196 km 🏆 Koen Bouwman
Review

Bouwman wins in Potenza and takes the maglia azzurra

A seven-man breakaway including Tom Dumoulin, Bauke Mollema and Davide Formolo fought out the hilly stage, with Koen Bouwman taking maximum points on Monte Sirino, at Viggiano and on La Sellata. Led out by Dumoulin, Bouwman won the sprint ahead of Mollema and Formolo for his first Grand Tour stage win and took the maglia azzurra; the favourites finished together almost three minutes down.
