Stage 1 / 21 · Friday, 6 May 2022

Budapest →Visegrád

Flat 195 km 🏆 Mathieu van der Poel
Review

Van der Poel wins at Visegrád and takes the first maglia rosa

Breakaway riders Mattia Bais and Filippo Tagliani were caught 13.7 km from the finish, and Lennard Kämna's late attack on the climb to Visegrád was reeled in inside the last kilometre. Mathieu van der Poel won the uphill sprint ahead of Biniam Girmay and Pello Bilbao and took the maglia rosa, the maglia ciclamino and the maglia azzurra; Girmay took the maglia bianca.
