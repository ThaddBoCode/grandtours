🏆2021

Pogačar wins his second Tour in a row

Tadej Pogačar won the Tour for the second year running, 5:20 ahead of Jonas Vingegaard and 7:03 ahead of Richard Carapaz, becoming the youngest rider to win the Tour twice. He won three stages – the Laval time trial and the summit finishes at the Col de Portet and Luz Ardiden – and, as in 2020, also took the mountains and young rider classifications. Carapaz became the first Ecuadorian to finish on the Tour podium.
