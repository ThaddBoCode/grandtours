Stage 13 / 21 · Friday, 9 July 2021

Nîmes →Carcassonne

Flat 219.9 km 🏆 Mark Cavendish
Review

Cavendish equals Merckx with his 34th stage win

About 60 km from the finish a crash on a descent brought down a group of riders, some of them into a small ravine, and forced Simon Yates, Lucas Hamilton and Roger Kluge out of the race. In a chaotic sprint in Carcassonne Michael Mørkøv led out Mark Cavendish, who won ahead of Mørkøv and Jasper Philipsen for his fourth stage of the race and his 34th in the Tour, equalling Eddy Merckx's record. Cavendish extended his lead in the points classification to 101 points.
