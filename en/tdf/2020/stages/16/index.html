Stage 16 / 21 · Tuesday, 15 September 2020

La Tour-du-Pin →Villard-de-Lans

Mountain 164 km 🏆 Lennard Kämna
Review

Kämna makes up for Puy Mary with a solo win at Villard-de-Lans

A breakaway of more than 20 riders built a lead of over 12 minutes, and Richard Carapaz's repeated accelerations on the climb to Saint-Nizier-du-Moucherotte broke it apart. Lennard Kämna attacked just before the summit and won alone, 1:27 ahead of Carapaz and 1:56 ahead of Sébastien Reichenbach. Apart from Tom Dumoulin and Nairo Quintana, the top ten finished together and the first eight places overall were unchanged.
