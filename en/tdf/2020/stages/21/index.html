Stage 21 / 21 · Sunday, 20 September 2020

Mantes-la-Jolie →Paris (Champs-Élysées)

Flat 122 km 🏆 Sam Bennett
Review

Bennett wins on the Champs-Élysées, Pogačar wins the Tour

Sam Bennett won the sprint on the Champs-Élysées ahead of Mads Pedersen and Peter Sagan to seal the green jersey. Tadej Pogačar finished safely in the bunch to become the first Slovenian winner of the Tour, 59 seconds ahead of Primož Roglič and 3:30 ahead of Richie Porte, and also won the mountains and young rider classifications. Movistar won the team classification.
