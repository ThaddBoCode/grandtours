Preview

The queen stage: 5,400 m of climbing over the Soulor, Tourmalet (from Luz-Saint-Sauveur) and Hourquette d'Ancizan, then up to the Col du Portet – at 2,215 m the highest point of the Tour. Three days from Paris, yellow is on the line.
