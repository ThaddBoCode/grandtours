Stage 21 / 21 · Sunday, 21 July 2024

Monaco →Nice

Individual time trial 33.7 km 🏆 Tadej Pogačar
Review

Pogačar wins the Monaco–Nice time trial to seal his third Tour

For the first time since 1989 the Tour ended with a time trial, and for the first time ever it did not finish in Paris. Tadej Pogačar won the 33.7 km from Monaco to Nice 1:03 ahead of Jonas Vingegaard and 1:14 ahead of Remco Evenepoel, his sixth stage win of the race. He took the overall victory by 6:17 over Vingegaard and 9:18 over Evenepoel, with Biniam Girmay in green, Richard Carapaz in polka dots and Evenepoel in white.
