Legendary places on the route

Climb · Hautes-Pyrénées

Col du Tourmalet

2,115 m 18.3 km · 7.7 %
Stage 14 · Oier Lazkano

The Tourmalet (2,115 m) is the most frequently crossed pass in the Tour de France, first climbed in 1910 with Octave Lapize leading. The Souvenir Jacques Goddet is awarded at its summit.

 Climb · Hautes-Pyrénées

Hourquette d'Ancizan

1,564 m 10.3 km · 7.8 %
Stage 14 · David Gaudu

The Hourquette d’Ancizan (1,564 m) links the Payolle valley with the Vallée d’Aure. The Tour first crossed it in 2011.
