Stage 19 / 21 · Friday, 25 July 2025

Albertville →La Plagne

Mountain 93.1 km 🏆 Thymen Arensman
Review

Arensman holds off Vingegaard and Pogačar at La Plagne

An outbreak of lumpy skin disease among cattle forced a late reroute that cut the stage to 93.1 km, still finishing at La Plagne. Thymen Arensman went clear 14 km from the finish and held off Jonas Vingegaard and Tadej Pogačar by 2 seconds for his second stage win. Florian Lipowitz gained 41 seconds on Oscar Onley to strengthen his hold on third place overall.
