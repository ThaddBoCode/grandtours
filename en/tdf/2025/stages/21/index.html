Stage 21 / 21 · Sunday, 27 July 2025

Mantes-la-Ville →Paris (Champs-Élysées)

Flat 132.3 km 🏆 Wout van Aert
Review

Van Aert drops Pogačar on Montmartre and wins in Paris

The final stage featured three ascents of the Butte Montmartre, inspired by the Paris 2024 Olympic road race, and GC times were neutralised early because of rain. Wout van Aert followed Tadej Pogačar's attack on the last climb of Montmartre, dropped him near the top and soloed to victory on the Champs-Élysées. Pogačar finished fourth to seal his fourth Tour win, with Jonathan Milan in green and Florian Lipowitz in white.
