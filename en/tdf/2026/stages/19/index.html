Stage 19 / 21 · Friday, 24 July 2026

Gap →Alpe d'Huez

Mountain 127.9 km 🏆 Tadej Pogačar
Review

Pogačar breaks Pantani's Alpe d'Huez record

Pogačar went clear alone on the climb to Alpe d'Huez, about 12 km from the finish, picked off the breakaway riders one by one, caught Carapaz and Lenny Martinez 1.7 km from the line and won solo – his fifth stage win of the race. His time beat the record for the climb set by Marco Pantani in 1995. Carapaz took the polka-dot jersey from Pogačar by one point, and del Toro stayed third overall, 24 seconds ahead of Seixas.
