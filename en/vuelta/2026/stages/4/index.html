Stage 4 / 21 · Tuesday, 25 August 2026

Andorra la Vella ↻

Mountain ↻ Loop 104.9 km 🏆 Tadej Pogačar
Review

Pogačar wins solo in Andorra after a 50 km attack

Tadej Pogačar attacked 50 km from the finish on the Col de Beixalís and won in Andorra la Vella in 2:40:25. Jarno Widar led the chasers home 2:39 behind, with Felix Gall and Oscar Onley in the same group. Primož Roglič moved up to second overall at 3:21 ahead of Enric Mas (3:32), and Onley took the white jersey.
