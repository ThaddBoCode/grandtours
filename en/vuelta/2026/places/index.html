Legendary places on the route

Climb · Andalucía

La Pandera

1,872 m
Stage 14

La Pandera (1,872 m) is the highest point of the Sierra Sur de Jaén and carries a military station on its summit. The Vuelta finished here in 2002, 2003, 2006, 2009, 2017, 2022 and 2026, starting with a win for Roberto Heras.

 Finish · Aragón

Valdelinares

1,695 m
Stage 7

Valdelinares (1,695 m) in Teruel province is the highest municipal seat in Spain; the Aramón Valdelinares ski resort lies seven kilometres away. The Vuelta finished here in 2005 with Roberto Heras, in 2014 with Winner Anacona and in 2026 with Andreas Leknessund.
