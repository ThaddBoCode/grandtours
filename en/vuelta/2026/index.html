🏆Stage 21

Enric Mas wins his first Vuelta

Enric Mas (Movistar Team) won the Vuelta in 73:52:55, 2:15 ahead of Primož Roglič and 2:44 ahead of Felix Gall. He inherited the red jersey after Pogačar's exit on stage 8, won stage 9 on the Alto de Aitana and defended his lead all the way to Granada.
