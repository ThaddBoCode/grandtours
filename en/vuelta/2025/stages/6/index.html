Stage 6 / 21 · Thursday, 28 August 2025

Olot →Pal

Mountain 170.3 km 🏆 Jay Vine
Review

Vine wins at Pal as Træen takes red and Ayuso loses seven minutes

On the first proper mountain stage, finishing at Pal in Andorra, Jay Vine attacked from a ten-man break over the Alto de la Comella and held off Torstein Træen on the final climb. Træen finished about three and a half minutes ahead of the main GC group and took the red jersey, while Juan Ayuso lost seven minutes; Vine moved into the polka-dot jersey and Giulio Pellizzari into the white jersey.
