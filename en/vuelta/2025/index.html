🏆2025

Vingegaard wins his first Vuelta by 1:16

Jonas Vingegaard won the Vuelta for the first time in his career, taking stages 2, 9 and 20 and finishing 1:16 ahead of João Almeida. He got sick during the second week and had to defend his red jersey before sealing the overall win on the Bola del Mundo, while Almeida could only take time through bonus seconds and the time trial. Tom Pidcock was third (+3:11), his first Grand Tour podium.
