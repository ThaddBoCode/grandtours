Stage 14 / 18 · Wednesday, 4 November 2020

Lugo →Ourense

Hilly 204.7 km 🏆 Tim Wellens
Review

Wellens wins in Ourense for a second time

Tim Wellens (Lotto–Soudal) took his second stage win of this Vuelta in Ourense, ahead of Michael Woods and Zdeněk Štybar (both on the same time). The general classification was unchanged: Roglič led by 39 seconds over Carapaz and 47 over Carthy.
