Stage 3 / 18 · Thursday, 22 October 2020

Lodosa →Laguna Negra de Urbión (Vinuesa)

Hilly 166.1 km 🏆 Dan Martin
Review

Dan Martin wins at Laguna Negra de Urbión

Dan Martin (Israel Start-Up Nation) won the summit finish at Laguna Negra de Urbión, with Roglič and Carapaz following on the same time. Roglič stayed in front, five seconds ahead of Martin and 13 ahead of Carapaz.
