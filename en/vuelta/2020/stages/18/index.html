Stage 18 / 18 · Sunday, 8 November 2020

Hipódromo de la Zarzuela →Madrid

Flat 124.2 km 🏆 Pascal Ackermann
Review

Ackermann wins in Madrid, Roglič seals the overall

Pascal Ackermann (Bora–Hansgrohe) won the final sprint in Madrid ahead of Sam Bennett and Max Kanter for his second stage win. Primož Roglič finished the Vuelta as overall winner in 72:46:12, ahead of Richard Carapaz (+0:24) and Hugh Carthy (+1:15), and also won the points classification.
