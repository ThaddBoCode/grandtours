Stage 8 / 21 · Saturday, 27 August 2022

La Pola Llaviana →Colláu Fancuaya

Mountain 154.5 km 🏆 Jay Vine
Review

Vine wins again at Colláu Fancuaya, Pedersen takes green

Jay Vine won his second stage from the break after dropping his companions 6.2 km out; Marc Soler was second at 43 seconds, level with Rein Taaramäe, and Thibaut Pinot fourth at 47 seconds. Vine took the polka-dot jersey, and Mads Pedersen the green jersey at the intermediate sprint. Evenepoel, Mas and Roglič finished 1:20 behind Vine, and the overall order stayed the same.
