Stage 5 / 21 · Wednesday, 30 August 2023

Morella →Burriana

Hilly 186.5 km 🏆 Kaden Groves
Review

Groves wins again, Evenepoel grabs a time bonus

Eric Fagúndez led alone for more than 110 km before being caught on the Collado de la Ibola by Eduardo Sepúlveda. Remco Evenepoel won the intermediate sprint for a six-second time bonus. In Burriana Kaden Groves took the sprint ahead of Filippo Ganna and Dries Van Gestel, his second stage win in a row.
