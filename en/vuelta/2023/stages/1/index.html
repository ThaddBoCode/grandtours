Stage 1 / 21 · Saturday, 26 August 2023

Barcelona ↻

Team time trial ↻ Loop 14.8 km 🏆 Team Picnic–PostNL
Review

dsm–firmenich win the opening team time trial in the dark

The Vuelta opened with a 14.8 km team time trial in Barcelona, run in rain and fading light. Team dsm–firmenich set the best time of 17:30 with Movistar on the same time, and Lorenzo Milesi pulled on the first red jersey. Laurens De Plus crashed and left the race, and a puncture for Jonas Vingegaard cost Jumbo-Visma time.
