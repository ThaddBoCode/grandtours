Stories

The big stories of the Vuelta and the story of every stage – updated continuously.

Headlines

★2024

Roglič wins a fourth Vuelta and equals the record of Roberto Heras

Primož Roglič (Red Bull–Bora–Hansgrohe) won the general classification by 2:36 over Ben O'Connor, with Enric Mas third at 3:13. It was his fourth Vuelta title, which equals the record of Roberto Heras. He also won three stages (4, 8 and 19) and took the red jersey back for good on stage 19.

🏆Stage 6

O'Connor strikes on stage 6 and leads until stage 19

Ben O'Connor (Decathlon–AG2R La Mondiale) won stage 6 to Yunquera from a breakaway and led Primož Roglič by 4:51 afterwards, then stayed in front of the general classification after every stage from 6 to 18. Roglič took the red jersey from him only on stage 19. O'Connor finished second overall, his best result in a grand tour.

🏆Stage 19

Roglič attacks on Moncalvillo and takes the red jersey back

Before stage 19, Roglič and O'Connor were only 5 seconds apart. Roglič won at the Alto de Moncalvillo, 46 seconds ahead of David Gaudu and Mattias Skjelmose, and led O'Connor by 1:54 afterwards. That effectively decided the general classification; Skjelmose took the white jersey.

⚠️Stage 16

Van Aert crashes on a wet descent and abandons after three stage wins

Wout van Aert had won stages 3, 7 and 10 and was far ahead in the points classification, with 291 points before he left. On stage 16 he crashed on a slippery descent, suffered a knee injury and abandoned. The green jersey passed to Kaden Groves and the polka-dot jersey to Jay Vine.

⚡2024

Groves wins three sprints and the green jersey with 226 points

Kaden Groves (Alpecin–Deceuninck) won stages 2, 14 and 17 and took the points lead after Van Aert's exit, finishing with 226 points. Roglič was second with 140. Twice Van Aert was beaten by him in a sprint, on stages 2 and 14.

🔥Stage 20

Vine takes the polka-dot jersey from team-mate Soler by two points

On stage 20 Jay Vine took the polka-dot jersey from his team-mate Marc Soler and won the mountains classification 78 points to 76. Soler was still named most combative rider and had won stage 16. UAE Team Emirates also took the team classification, 33:53 ahead of Red Bull–Bora–Hansgrohe.

•Stage 15

ProTeam Kern Pharma wins three stages, two of them by Castrillo

Pablo Castrillo (Equipo Kern Pharma) won stage 12 at Manzaneda and stage 15 at Valgrande-Pajares, each time from a breakaway, and Urko Berrade added a third win for the Spanish ProTeam on stage 18. On the Cuitu Negru Castrillo held off Aleksandr Vlasov by 12 seconds.

•Stage 1

Grand Départ in Lisbon: McNulty wins the opening time trial

The Vuelta started in Portugal: the first three stages ran from Lisbon to Oeiras, Cascais to Ourém and Lousã to Castelo Branco before the race moved on to Spain. Brandon McNulty won the 12 km time trial in 12:35, two seconds ahead of Mathias Vacek, and became the first leader of the red jersey. The race ended in Madrid with another time trial.

•2024

Defending champion Kuss only 14th, Uijtdebroeks pulls out

Sepp Kuss, the 2023 winner, started with bib 1 for Visma–Lease a Bike and finished 14th overall, 20:25 behind. His team-mate Cian Uijtdebroeks did not start stage 15.

Stage by stage

1 Sat 17 Aug · Lisbon → Oeiras · Review McNulty wins the Lisbon time trial and takes the first red jersey Brandon McNulty won the 12 km time trial from Lisbon to Oeiras in 12:35 and took the red jersey. Mathias Vacek was second, 2 seconds back, and Wout van Aert third at 3 seconds. Vacek wore the white jersey. 2 Sun 18 Aug · Cascais → Ourém · Review Groves wins the bunch sprint, Van Aert takes red through bonus seconds Kaden Groves won the bunch sprint in Ourém, just ahead of Wout van Aert, who had come up from behind on the line. Thanks to six bonus seconds for second place, Van Aert took the red jersey, 3 seconds ahead of McNulty. Groves became the leader of the points classification. 3 Mon 19 Aug · Lousã → Castelo Branco · Review Van Aert wins the sprint in Castelo Branco and defends red Wout van Aert, in the red jersey, won the sprint in Castelo Branco, launching it 200 metres from the line and holding off Kaden Groves. It was his first stage win of this Vuelta. He now led the general classification by 13 seconds over McNulty and also took the lead in the points classification. 4 Tue 20 Aug · Plasencia → Pico Villuercas · Review Roglič wins at Pico Villuercas and takes the red jersey On the first summit finish of the Spanish stages Primož Roglič beat Lennert Van Eetvelt, who was given the same time in second, in the sprint; João Almeida was third. Roglič took the red jersey with an 8-second lead over Almeida and 32 over Enric Mas. Antonio Tiberi took the white jersey. 5 Wed 21 Aug · Fuente del Maestre → Sevilla · Review Bittner beats Van Aert in the Seville sprint Pavel Bittner (dsm–firmenich PostNL) won the bunch sprint in Seville narrowly ahead of Wout van Aert and Kaden Groves. Two breakaway riders, Ibon Ruiz and Txomin Juaristi, had been in front for about 120 km. Nothing changed at the top of the general classification: Roglič still led ahead of Almeida. 6 Thu 22 Aug · Jerez de la Frontera → Yunquera · Review O'Connor solo to Yunquera: 4:51 lead and the red jersey Ben O'Connor broke away from the break, dropped Gijs Leemreize on the Puerto Martinez and won in Yunquera 4:33 ahead of Marco Frigo. His win put him in the red jersey, 4:51 ahead of Roglič and 4:59 ahead of Almeida. Florian Lipowitz was third on the stage and took the white jersey. 7 Fri 23 Aug · Archidona → Córdoba · Review Van Aert takes his second win in Córdoba Wout van Aert won the sprint from a reduced group in Córdoba ahead of Mathias Vacek and Pau Miquel, all given the same time. Pavel Sivakov had attacked shortly before the red kite. Ben O'Connor stayed in the red jersey with Roglič at 4:45; Antonio Tiberi took over the white jersey. 8 Sat 24 Aug · Úbeda → Cazorla · Review Roglič wins in Cazorla and takes 56 seconds out of O'Connor Primož Roglič attacked several times on the final climb in Cazorla, caught the last breakaway rider Oier Lazkano and won ahead of Enric Mas, the only one able to follow him. With bonuses he took 56 seconds out of race leader Ben O'Connor, cutting his deficit to 3:49. Roglič also took the polka-dot jersey. 9 Sun 25 Aug · Motril → Granada · Review Yates wins solo in Granada, O'Connor saves red Adam Yates won in Granada after a solo move from the breakaway, 1:39 ahead of Richard Carapaz. Ben O'Connor took the sprint of a ten-rider group, 3:45 behind Yates, and stayed in red ahead of Roglič (3:53); Carapaz moved up to third overall. Yates took the polka-dot jersey and Florian Lipowitz the white. 10 Tue 27 Aug · Ponteareas → Baiona · Review Van Aert completes the hat-trick in Baiona Wout van Aert won in Baiona from a five-man breakaway, finishing 3 seconds ahead of Quentin Pacher, with Marc Soler third at 2:01. It was Van Aert's third stage win. The field with Ben O'Connor in the red jersey came in about five and a half minutes later, and the general classification did not change. 11 Wed 28 Aug · Padrón ↻ · Review Dunbar springs a surprise 500 m out – O'Connor loses 37 seconds Eddie Dunbar (Jayco–AlUla) won on the Padrón loop from a leading group of 14 by attacking 500 metres from the line; Quinten Hermans was second (+0:02). In the peloton Roglič and Mas dropped red jersey Ben O'Connor, who lost 37 seconds. His lead over Roglič shrank to 3:16; Carlos Rodríguez took the white jersey. 12 Thu 29 Aug · Orense → Estación de Montaña de Manzaneda · Review Castrillo wins from the breakaway in Manzaneda Pablo Castrillo (Equipo Kern Pharma) broke clear of a ten-man breakaway on the final climb to Manzaneda and won ahead of Max Poole (+0:08) and Marc Soler (+0:16). The favourites cancelled each other out. Nothing changed at the top of the general classification: O'Connor led Roglič by 3:16. 13 Fri 30 Aug · Lugo → Puerto de Ancares · Review Woods wins at Puerto de Ancares, O'Connor loses almost two minutes Michael Woods (Israel–Premier Tech) attacked alone on the last climb and won ahead of Mauro Schmid (+0:45) and Marc Soler (+1:11). In the peloton Ben O'Connor lost 1:55 to Roglič but kept the red jersey, his lead down to 1:21. Wout van Aert took the lead of the mountains classification. 14 Sat 31 Aug · Villafranca del Bierzo → Villablino · Review Groves beats Van Aert in the Villablino sprint After the Puerto de Leitariegos, where the last breakaway riders were caught, the stage came down to a sprint: Kaden Groves held off Wout van Aert on his wheel to win his second stage, with Corbin Strong third. The general classification did not change: O'Connor led Roglič by 1:21. 15 Sun 1 Sept · Infiesto → Valgrande-Pajares · Review Castrillo wins again at Cuitu Negru, Roglič closes to 1:03 Pablo Castrillo won for the second time, attacking from a leading trio to finish 12 seconds ahead of Aleksandr Vlasov and 31 ahead of Pavel Sivakov. In the red jersey group Roglič attacked but was brought back by Enric Mas; O'Connor limited his losses but now led Roglič by only 1:03. Florian Lipowitz took back the white jersey. 16 Tue 3 Sept · Luanco → Lagos de Covadonga · Review Soler wins solo at Covadonga as Van Aert crashes out Marc Soler (UAE Team Emirates) won solo at Lagos de Covadonga, 18 seconds ahead of Filippo Zana and 23 ahead of Max Poole. Wout van Aert, part of the breakaway, crashed on a wet descent and abandoned with knee pain. Ben O'Connor was dropped on the final climb but kept the red jersey by 5 seconds over Roglič; Kaden Groves became the points leader and Jay Vine the mountains leader. 17 Wed 4 Sept · Arnuero → Santander · Review Groves wins his third stage in the rain in Santander A four-man breakaway including Xabier Isasa stayed ahead for a long time but was caught 200 metres from the line in the rain. Kaden Groves, now the points leader, won the sprint ahead of Pavel Bittner and Vito Braet. Roglič remained 5 seconds behind O'Connor in the red jersey. 18 Thu 5 Sept · Vitoria-Gasteiz → Maeztu · Review Berrade wins solo in Maeztu, Soler takes the polka-dot jersey From a leading group of 42 riders, Urko Berrade (Equipo Kern Pharma) counter-attacked after a move by Steven Kruijswijk, held off the chasers and won in Maeztu ahead of Mauro Schmid and Max Poole (both +0:04). Marc Soler took the polka-dot jersey. In the standings O'Connor stayed 5 seconds ahead of Roglič. 19 Fri 6 Sept · Logroño → Alto de Moncalvillo · Review Roglič wins on Moncalvillo and takes the red jersey Primož Roglič attacked alone on the Alto de Moncalvillo, left Enric Mas and O'Connor behind and won 46 seconds ahead of David Gaudu and Mattias Skjelmose. He took the red jersey with a 1:54 lead over O'Connor, with Mas third at 2:20. Skjelmose became the leader of the young rider classification. 20 Sat 7 Sept · Villarcayo → Picón Blanco · Review Dunbar wins for the second time, Vine takes the polka-dot jersey On the penultimate stage Eddie Dunbar left the red jersey group less than five kilometres from the top of the Picón Blanco, passed Pavel Sivakov and won ahead of Enric Mas (+0:07) and Roglič (+0:10). Roglič now led O'Connor by 2:02 and Mas by 2:11. Jay Vine took the polka-dot jersey from Marc Soler. 21 Sun 8 Sept · Distrito Telefónica → Madrid · Review Küng wins the Madrid time trial as Roglič secures a fourth overall win Stefan Küng won the 24.6 km time trial in Madrid in 26:28, 31 seconds ahead of Roglič and 42 ahead of Mattia Cattaneo. The podium did not change: Roglič won by 2:36 over Ben O'Connor and 3:13 over Enric Mas, equalling Roberto Heras' record of four Vuelta wins.
