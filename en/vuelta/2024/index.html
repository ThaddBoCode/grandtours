★2024

Roglič wins a fourth Vuelta and equals the record of Roberto Heras

Primož Roglič (Red Bull–Bora–Hansgrohe) won the general classification by 2:36 over Ben O'Connor, with Enric Mas third at 3:13. It was his fourth Vuelta title, which equals the record of Roberto Heras. He also won three stages (4, 8 and 19) and took the red jersey back for good on stage 19.
