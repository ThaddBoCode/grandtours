Stage 12 / 21 · Thursday, 29 August 2024

Orense →Estación de Montaña de Manzaneda

Hilly 137.5 km 🏆 Pablo Castrillo
Review

Castrillo wins from the breakaway in Manzaneda

Pablo Castrillo (Equipo Kern Pharma) broke clear of a ten-man breakaway on the final climb to Manzaneda and won ahead of Max Poole (+0:08) and Marc Soler (+0:16). The favourites cancelled each other out. Nothing changed at the top of the general classification: O'Connor led Roglič by 3:16.
