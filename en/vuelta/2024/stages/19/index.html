Stage 19 / 21 · Friday, 6 September 2024

Logroño →Alto de Moncalvillo

Hilly 173.5 km 🏆 Primož Roglič
Review

Roglič wins on Moncalvillo and takes the red jersey

Primož Roglič attacked alone on the Alto de Moncalvillo, left Enric Mas and O'Connor behind and won 46 seconds ahead of David Gaudu and Mattias Skjelmose. He took the red jersey with a 1:54 lead over O'Connor, with Mas third at 2:20. Skjelmose became the leader of the young rider classification.
