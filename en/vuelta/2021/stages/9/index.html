Stage 9 / 21 · Sunday, 22 August 2021

Puerto Lumbreras →Alto de Velefique

Mountain 188 km 🏆 Damiano Caruso
Review

Caruso wins at Velefique; Roglič extends his lead

Damiano Caruso dropped his breakaway companions 71 km out and held off the GC group to win solo, taking the mountains jersey. Roglič sprinted to second at 1' 05", one second ahead of Enric Mas; Mikel Landa lost five minutes, and Mas was the only rider within a minute of Roglič going into the rest day.
