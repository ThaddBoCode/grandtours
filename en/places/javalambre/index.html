Observatorio Astrofísico de Javalambre

The Javalambre Astrophysical Observatory sits on the Pico del Buitre (1,957 m) in the Sierra de Javalambre in Teruel province. The road up to it is a well-known cycling climb; the Vuelta finished here in 2019 with Ángel Madrazo and in 2023 with Sepp Kuss.
