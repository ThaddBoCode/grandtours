Roma (Fori Imperiali)

Since 2023 Rome has been the Giro's permanent final venue, with the last stage on a circuit around the Colosseum, the Via dei Fori Imperiali and the Circus Maximus. Before that the race had ended in the capital only in 1911, 1950, 2009 and 2018.
