In the headlines

Giro 2024 · Stage 19

Vendrame solos to victory in Sappada

From a 19-rider breakaway containing four stage winners of this Giro, Andrea Vendrame attacked on the descent of the Sella Valcalda. He held off Georg Steinhauser and Pelayo Sánchez on the Cima Sappada and won by 54 seconds over Sánchez; the maglia rosa group finished almost 16 minutes down.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 12

Vendrame wins in Bagno di Romagna

An 18-rider break went clear, with Geoffrey Bouchard extending his lead in the mountains classification. Andrea Vendrame beat Chris Hamilton in a two-up sprint in Bagno di Romagna, while early crashes forced out Marc Soler and former race leader Alessandro De Marchi, who suffered fractures to his collarbone, ribs and vertebrae.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 3

Magnier again: sprint victory in Sofia

The last Bulgarian stage crossed the Borovets Pass, its only climb, on the way to the capital. Paul Magnier won his second sprint ahead of Jonathan Milan and Dylan Groenewegen, strengthening his hold on the maglia ciclamino, while Guillermo Thomas Silva stayed in pink. Adam Yates and Andrea Vendrame did not start after the previous day's crash.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 19

Bouwman wins again at Castelmonte and secures the maglia azzurra

The stage crossed into Slovenia for the Kolovrat, where Koen Bouwman, Mauro Schmid, Attila Valter and Alessandro Tonelli emerged from the breakaway, later joined by Andrea Vendrame. Bouwman took the sharp final left-hander first and held off Schmid for his second stage win, with maximum points on all four climbs making the maglia azzurra mathematically his; Carapaz, Hindley and Landa remained inseparable.
