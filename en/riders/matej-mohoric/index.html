In the headlines

Tour 2023 · Stage 19

Mohorič wins the photo finish in Poligny

After a fast, nervous stage, Kasper Asgreen, Matej Mohorič and Ben O'Connor went clear on the Côte d'Ivory. O'Connor opened the sprint early and Mohorič beat Asgreen in a photo finish by the width of a rim – his third Tour stage win. In an emotional interview he paid tribute to his late teammate Gino Mäder.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 19

Mohorič double as Bahrain Victorious win the team prize

Matej Mohorič soloed to wins in Le Creusot and Libourne, the first of which completed his set of stage wins in all three Grand Tours. His teammate Dylan Teuns won in Le Grand-Bornand, and Bahrain Victorious won the team classification.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 7

Mohorič solos to Le Creusot as Roglič loses time

On the 249.1 km stage, the longest of the race, a 29-rider group including Mathieu van der Poel and Wout van Aert went clear and Matej Mohorič attacked alone on the Signal d'Uchon. He won by 1:20 over Jasper Stuyven, took the polka-dot jersey and completed his set of stage wins in all three Grand Tours. Van der Poel extended his lead to 30 seconds over van Aert, while Primož Roglič lost more than three minutes to the other favourites.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 9

Bernal attacks on the gravel and takes pink

The stage finished on gravel roads at Campo Felice, where Egan Bernal countered Aleksandr Vlasov, dropped Giulio Ciccone and won by seven seconds to take the maglia rosa and the maglia bianca, 15 seconds ahead of Remco Evenepoel. Geoffrey Bouchard took the maglia azzurra, and Matej Mohorič abandoned after a heavy crash on the descent of the Passo Godi.
