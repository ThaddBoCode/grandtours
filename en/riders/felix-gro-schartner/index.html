Vuelta 2020 · Stage 10

Roglič wins in Suances and regains the red jersey

Roglič won the flat stage from Castro Urdiales to Suances ahead of Felix Großschartner and Andrea Bagioli (both on the same time) and took the red jersey back from Carapaz. Carapaz is listed on the same time in the general classification, with Dan Martin 25 seconds back.
