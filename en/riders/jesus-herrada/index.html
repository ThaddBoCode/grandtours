In the headlines

Vuelta 2023 · Stage 11

Herrada wins from a 26-rider breakaway

A 26-rider break stayed away to the finish at La Laguna Negra, with the favourites finishing well behind. Jonathan Caicedo attacked 1.5 km out but was passed 250 m from the line by Jesús Herrada, who won ahead of Romain Grégoire and Andreas Kron. Herrada took the polka-dot jersey, while the top of the overall standings did not change.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 7

Herrada wins from the break in Cistierna

Six riders went clear early and held off the peloton, with Fred Wright starting the sprint 300 m out. Jesús Herrada won his second Vuelta stage ahead of Samuele Battistella and Wright. Sam Bennett led the peloton home 29 seconds later, and Remco Evenepoel stayed in red as the top ten did not change.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 17

Urán wins at Tentudía as Roglič abandons

Rigoberto Urán won from a 13-man break after Lawson Craddock was caught shortly before the final kilometre, holding off Quentin Pacher, with Jesús Herrada third. The win completed his set of stage wins at all three Grand Tours. Primož Roglič did not start after his crash, leaving Remco Evenepoel 2:01 ahead of Enric Mas, with Juan Ayuso moving onto the podium.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 14

Bardet wins at Pico Villuercas; Carapaz abandons

Romain Bardet emerged as the strongest of an 18-man break, dropped everyone on the final climb and won 44 seconds ahead of Jesús Herrada and Jay Vine, taking the mountains lead. Richard Carapaz abandoned, suffering in the high temperatures, and Eiking lost 20 seconds but stayed in red.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 6

Lutsenko solos to victory on Mont Aigoual

An eight-man breakaway was allowed to fight out the stage, and Kazakh champion Alexey Lutsenko dropped Neilson Powless on the Col de la Lusette, a climb new to the Tour, to win alone. Jesús Herrada was second at 55 seconds and Greg Van Avermaet third at 2:15. The favourites finished together almost three minutes down, and Adam Yates kept yellow by 3 seconds over Primož Roglič.
