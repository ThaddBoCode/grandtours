In the headlines

Vuelta 2025 · Stage 14

Soler wins solo at La Farrapona; Vingegaard takes second

Marc Soler attacked from a 24-man break ahead of the final climb and soloed to the win, UAE's seventh stage of the race. Among the favourites a headwind kept things quiet until the last kilometre, when Jai Hindley attacked; Jonas Vingegaard outsprinted João Almeida for second place and extended his lead to 48 seconds.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 20

Vine takes the polka-dot jersey from team-mate Soler by two points

On stage 20 Jay Vine took the polka-dot jersey from his team-mate Marc Soler and won the mountains classification 78 points to 76. Soler was still named most combative rider and had won stage 16. UAE Team Emirates also took the team classification, 33:53 ahead of Red Bull–Bora–Hansgrohe.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 16

Soler wins solo at Covadonga as Van Aert crashes out

Marc Soler (UAE Team Emirates) won solo at Lagos de Covadonga, 18 seconds ahead of Filippo Zana and 23 ahead of Max Poole. Wout van Aert, part of the breakaway, crashed on a wet descent and abandoned with knee pain. Ben O'Connor was dropped on the final climb but kept the red jersey by 5 seconds over Roglič; Kaden Groves became the points leader and Jay Vine the mountains leader.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 18

Berrade wins solo in Maeztu, Soler takes the polka-dot jersey

From a leading group of 42 riders, Urko Berrade (Equipo Kern Pharma) counter-attacked after a move by Steven Kruijswijk, held off the chasers and won in Maeztu ahead of Mauro Schmid and Max Poole (both +0:04). Marc Soler took the polka-dot jersey. In the standings O'Connor stayed 5 seconds ahead of Roglič.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 5

Soler wins solo in Bilbao, Molard takes red

Marc Soler bridged to a 17-man break, went clear on the second ascent of the Alto del Vivero and held off the chasers, who came within six seconds, to win in Bilbao. Daryl Impey and Fred Wright followed at four seconds; with the peloton more than five minutes down, fourth-placed Rudy Molard took the red jersey, two seconds ahead of Wright.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 2

Soler wins in Lekunberri, Roglič stays in red

Marc Soler (Movistar) won the stage from Pamplona to Lekunberri, 19 seconds ahead of Roglič and Dan Martin. Roglič stayed in the red jersey, now nine seconds ahead of Martin and eleven ahead of Carapaz, who took over the mountains lead.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 2

Mass crash in Bulgaria ends Yates' and Buitrago's Giro

On stage 2 to Veliko Tarnovo, around ten riders – several of them GC contenders – went down 23 km from the finish on a slippery road at high speed. Santiago Buitrago and UAE's Jay Vine and Marc Soler abandoned, and podium contender Adam Yates did not start stage 3.

 Vuelta 2025

UAE win seven stages and the team classification

UAE Team Emirates XRG won seven stages: the team time trial (5), Jay Vine on stages 6 and 10, Juan Ayuso on 7 and 12, João Almeida on the Angliru (13) and Marc Soler at La Farrapona (14). They also took the team classification, 23:01 ahead of Visma–Lease a Bike, second place overall for Almeida and the mountains jersey for Vine.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 10

Van Aert completes the hat-trick in Baiona

Wout van Aert won in Baiona from a five-man breakaway, finishing 3 seconds ahead of Quentin Pacher, with Marc Soler third at 2:01. It was Van Aert's third stage win. The field with Ben O'Connor in the red jersey came in about five and a half minutes later, and the general classification did not change.
