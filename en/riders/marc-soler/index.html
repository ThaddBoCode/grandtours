In the headlines

Giro 2026 · Stage 2

Mass crash in Bulgaria ends Yates' and Buitrago's Giro

On stage 2 to Veliko Tarnovo, around ten riders – several of them GC contenders – went down 23 km from the finish on a slippery road at high speed. Santiago Buitrago and UAE's Jay Vine and Marc Soler abandoned, and podium contender Adam Yates did not start stage 3.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 17

Pogačar cracks on the Col de la Loze

A day after the time trial, on the queen stage to Courchevel with more than 5,000 m of climbing, Pogačar was dropped on the Col de la Loze and needed help from his teammate Marc Soler. Vingegaard's lead grew to 7:35, while Felix Gall won the stage alone.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 12

Vendrame wins in Bagno di Romagna

An 18-rider break went clear, with Geoffrey Bouchard extending his lead in the mountains classification. Andrea Vendrame beat Chris Hamilton in a two-up sprint in Bagno di Romagna, while early crashes forced out Marc Soler and former race leader Alessandro De Marchi, who suffered fractures to his collarbone, ribs and vertebrae.
