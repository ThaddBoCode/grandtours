In the headlines

Tour 2025 · Stage 14

Evenepoel quits on the Tourmalet, Arensman wins at Superbagnères

Third-placed Remco Evenepoel was dropped early on the Tourmalet and abandoned, while Mattias Skjelmose also left after a crash. Thymen Arensman attacked from the break on the Peyresourde, 37 km from the finish, and won his first Tour stage at Superbagnères, 1:08 ahead of Tadej Pogačar, who gained 4 seconds on Vingegaard in the final metres. Florian Lipowitz moved up to third overall and took the white jersey.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 10

Ganna dominates the time trial to Massa

Filippo Ganna (Netcompany INEOS) won the 42 km individual time trial from Viareggio to Massa by 1:54 over teammate Thymen Arensman, who gained the most among the favourites and moved up to third overall. Jonas Vingegaard missed the top ten but closed to 27 seconds, so Afonso Eulálio kept the maglia rosa despite a poor time trial.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 19

Kuss wins the queen stage, Hindley moves onto the podium

On the Dolomites stage over six climbs, including the Passo Giau as the roof of the race, Sepp Kuss (Visma–Lease a Bike) won from the breakaway at Alleghe, 13 seconds ahead of Derek Gee-West and 36 ahead of Giulio Ciccone, who took the maglia azzurra. Thymen Arensman cracked and lost third place to Jai Hindley, and Jhonatan Narváez abandoned.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 19

Cattle disease outbreak cuts the last Alpine stage to 93.1 km

On the eve of stage 19, an outbreak of lumpy skin disease among cattle on the Col des Saisies forced the organisers to reroute the stage, removing two categorised climbs and shortening it to 93.1 km. Thymen Arensman won at La Plagne, his second stage win of the race after Superbagnères.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 10

Yates wins on Bastille Day, Healy takes the yellow jersey

On the first mountain stage, through the Massif Central, Simon Yates attacked twice on the Puy de Sancy and won from a 29-rider breakaway, 9 seconds ahead of Thymen Arensman. Ben Healy, third from the same break, took the yellow jersey with a 29-second lead over Tadej Pogačar, while Lenny Martinez took the polka-dot jersey.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 14

Ganna beats Pogačar in the Desenzano time trial

A week after his defeat in Perugia, Filippo Ganna won the 31.2 km time trial to Desenzano del Garda by 29 seconds over Tadej Pogačar, with Thymen Arensman third. Geraint Thomas, fourth on the day, moved up to second overall, 3:41 behind Pogačar.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 16

Hirt wins the queen stage at Aprica as Carapaz's lead shrinks

On a stage with 5250 m of climbing over the Mortirolo and Santa Cristina, Jan Hirt and Thymen Arensman caught Lennard Kämna, and Hirt went clear 7.9 km from the finish to hold off Arensman by seven seconds for his first Grand Tour stage win. Mikel Landa attacked the favourites, only Carapaz and Hindley could follow, and Hindley's four bonus seconds for third cut Carapaz's lead to three seconds.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 21

Sobrero wins in Verona, Hindley seals the Giro

Italian time trial champion Matteo Sobrero won the 17.4 km time trial in 22:24, 23 seconds ahead of Thymen Arensman and 40 ahead of Mathieu van der Poel, for his first Grand Tour stage win. Jai Hindley finished 15th to win the Giro by 1:18 over Carapaz, with Landa third; Hugh Carthy moved up to ninth, but Juan Pedro López kept the maglia bianca.
