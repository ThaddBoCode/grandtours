In the headlines

Tour 2022 · Stage 5

Clarke wins on the cobbles, Pogačar profits as Roglič crashes

Simon Clarke narrowly beat Taco van der Hoorn in the sprint of the breakaway at Arenberg. Behind, Vingegaard was slowed by a mechanical, Roglič crashed and dislocated his shoulder and Jack Haig abandoned, while Pogačar attacked with Jasper Stuyven and gained time on all his rivals. Van Aert kept yellow, 13 seconds ahead of Neilson Powless.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 3

Crashes end Roglič's Tour

Pre-race favourite Primož Roglič crashed about 10 km from the finish of stage 3, a day of crashes on which Jack Haig and Robert Gesink abandoned, Geraint Thomas dislocated his shoulder and Caleb Ewan broke his collarbone. The peloton stopped for a minute at the start of stage 4 in protest at the dangerous run-in. Roglič lost more than 30 minutes on stage 8 and did not start stage 9.

 Vuelta 2021

Roglič wins a third straight Vuelta

Primož Roglič won the Vuelta by 4' 42" over Enric Mas, with Jack Haig third at 7' 40". He is only the third rider to win the race in three successive years and took stages 1, 11, 17 and 21. His margin was the largest since Alex Zülle won by 5' 07" in 1997.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 20

Haig steps up after Landa's exit to take third; Mäder wins white

Jack Haig started as a domestique for Mikel Landa, who struggled and abandoned on stage 17; Haig then became team leader. On stage 20 he moved past López into third overall, Gino Mäder won the white jersey and finished fifth, and Bahrain Victorious took the team classification.
