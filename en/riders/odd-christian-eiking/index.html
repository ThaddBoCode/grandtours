In the headlines

Vuelta 2021 · Stage 10

Eiking holds the red jersey for seven days

On stage 10 Odd Christian Eiking finished in the breakaway and took the red jersey, the day Roglič crashed on a descent. He kept it through stage 16 and lost it only on stage 17, when he was dropped on the way to the Lagos de Covadonga and Roglič took over.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 11

Roglič beats Mas in Valdepeñas; Eiking stays in red

Magnus Cort was caught with 200 m to go and Roglič powered away from Enric Mas to win his second stage of the race (Mas at 3"). Eiking finished 11 seconds down and kept the red jersey; Philipsen (fever) and Alex Aranburu (crash) had both withdrawn before the start.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 15

Majka wins solo; Eiking keeps red before the rest day

Rafał Majka dropped Fabio Aru with 87 km to go and soloed to the finish in El Barraco, with Steven Kruijswijk second at 1' 27". The GC group stayed quiet apart from Adam Yates, who gained 15 seconds on the last climb, and Eiking kept the red jersey into the second rest day.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 12

Cort wins in Córdoba by half a wheel

Magnus Cort, led out by Jens Keukeleire, held off Andrea Bagioli in the sprint by half a wheel for his second stage win, with Michael Matthews third. The top ten was unchanged and Eiking kept the red jersey.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 13

Sénéchal wins his first Grand Tour stage

Deceuninck–Quick-Step set such a fast pace in the technical finale that sprinter Jakobsen was dropped, so lead-out man Florian Sénéchal sprinted himself and held off Matteo Trentin for his first Grand Tour stage win. Eiking stayed in the red jersey.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 14

Bardet wins at Pico Villuercas; Carapaz abandons

Romain Bardet emerged as the strongest of an 18-man break, dropped everyone on the final climb and won 44 seconds ahead of Jesús Herrada and Jay Vine, taking the mountains lead. Richard Carapaz abandoned, suffering in the high temperatures, and Eiking lost 20 seconds but stayed in red.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 16

Jakobsen wins a third stage; crash victims abandon

Fabio Jakobsen won his third stage of the race in the sprint, ahead of Jordi Meeus and Matteo Trentin. After a crash a few kilometres after the start, Giulio Ciccone, Rudy Molard and Sep Vanmarcke abandoned the race; Eiking kept the red jersey.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 17

Roglič wins solo at Covadonga and retakes red

Egan Bernal attacked 61 km out and Roglič followed immediately, then dropped him with 7.5 km to go and soloed to his third stage win. Eiking was dropped, so Roglič took the red jersey with a 2' 22" lead over Mas; teammate Sepp Kuss led home the chase group at 1' 35", denying the contenders the second-place bonus.
