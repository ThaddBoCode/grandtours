In the headlines

Tour 2023 · Stage 3

Philipsen opens his account in Bayonne

After a two-man breakaway by Neilson Powless and Laurent Pichon, Jasper Philipsen won the sprint into Bayonne ahead of Phil Bauhaus and Caleb Ewan following a strong lead-out by Mathieu van der Poel. The commissaires reviewed his sprint duel with Wout van Aert but let the result stand. Adam Yates kept yellow and Powless added to his mountains lead.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 4

Philipsen doubles up on the Nogaro motor circuit

On a slow stage with no breakaway until around 100 km in, Philipsen won at the Circuit Paul Armagnac by a quarter of a wheel from Caleb Ewan. Crashes in the finale brought down Fabio Jakobsen among others, and Mathieu van der Poel was relegated for impeding Biniam Girmay. Philipsen took the green jersey from Victor Lafay.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 13

Kwiatkowski conquers the Grand Colombier on Bastille Day

On the Bastille Day summit finish Michał Kwiatkowski went clear from the breakaway 11 km from the finish and won 47 seconds ahead of Maxim Van Gils. Pogačar was third, four seconds ahead of Vingegaard, and with the bonus seconds cut the gap to 9 seconds. Caleb Ewan abandoned during the stage.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 5

Démare wins in Messina and takes the maglia ciclamino

Alpecin–Fenix raised the pace on the Portella Mandrazzi, dropping Caleb Ewan and Mark Cavendish for good, while Arnaud Démare fought his way back after the descent. Démare then beat Fernando Gaviria and Giacomo Nizzolo in the sprint – his first Grand Tour stage win in two years – and took the maglia ciclamino.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 6

Démare pips Ewan with a bike throw in Scalea

Diego Rosa rode alone in front for around 140 km before being caught 27.6 km from the finish. Arnaud Démare came out of Caleb Ewan's slipstream and beat him with a bike throw for his second consecutive stage win, with Mark Cavendish third; Juan Pedro López kept the maglia rosa.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 12

Oldani takes his first professional win in Genoa

On the race's longest stage, 204 km to Genoa, which returned to the Passo del Bocco for the first time since Wouter Weylandt's fatal crash on its descent in 2011, a 25-man breakaway was allowed to fight for the win. Stefano Oldani, Lorenzo Rota and Gijs Leemreize went clear and Oldani beat Rota in the sprint for his first professional victory; Juan Pedro López stayed in pink, while Caleb Ewan had left the race before the start.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 3

Crashes end Roglič's Tour

Pre-race favourite Primož Roglič crashed about 10 km from the finish of stage 3, a day of crashes on which Jack Haig and Robert Gesink abandoned, Geraint Thomas dislocated his shoulder and Caleb Ewan broke his collarbone. The peloton stopped for a minute at the start of stage 4 in protest at the dangerous run-in. Roglič lost more than 30 minutes on stage 8 and did not start stage 9.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 5

Ewan wins in Cattolica, Landa crashes out

Caleb Ewan came from behind to beat Giacomo Nizzolo and Elia Viviani in Cattolica, with Nizzolo taking the maglia ciclamino. In a crash-strewn finale Mikel Landa went down heavily after Joe Dombrowski hit a marshal and had to abandon with a broken collarbone and broken ribs; Pavel Sivakov also crashed and later left the race.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 7

Ewan's second win, in Termoli

Fernando Gaviria made a surprise attack in the final kilometre, but Caleb Ewan closed the gap and came past to win ahead of Davide Cimolai and Tim Merlier. Ewan took the maglia ciclamino; Domenico Pozzovivo had not started after crashes the day before, and Attila Valter kept the maglia rosa.
