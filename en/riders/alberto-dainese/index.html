In the headlines

Giro 2023 · Stage 17

Dainese wins the bunch sprint in Caorle

Alberto Dainese won the bunch sprint in Caorle ahead of Jonathan Milan and Michael Matthews. Geraint Thomas kept the maglia rosa, 18 seconds ahead of João Almeida.

 Vuelta 2023 · Stage 19

Dainese wins the sprint in Íscar ahead of Ganna

Four escapees were never more than three minutes clear and were caught 20 km from the finish, after which an attack by Samuele Battistella was also reeled in. A crash at the flamme rouge brought down green jersey Kaden Groves. Alberto Dainese won the sprint ahead of fellow Italian Filippo Ganna, and the overall standings did not change.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 11

Dainese wins in Reggio Emilia, the first Italian stage win

On a pan-flat stage, Dries De Bondt's solo attack was caught 1.3 km from the line. Alberto Dainese came round Fernando Gaviria and Simone Consonni to win his first Grand Tour stage, the first by an Italian at this Giro, while Richard Carapaz took three bonus seconds and moved up to second overall.

 Vuelta 2023 · Stage 21

Groves takes green with stage wins 4, 5 and 21

Kaden Groves won the sprints in Tarragona and Burriana and finished the Vuelta with the win in Madrid. He wore the green jersey from stage 4 to the end and won the points classification with 315 points, 79 ahead of Remco Evenepoel. The other sprint wins went to Geoffrey Soupe (stage 7), Juan Sebastián Molano (12) and Alberto Dainese (19).

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 5

Philipsen wins in Albacete; Elissonde takes red

Jasper Philipsen won the sprint ahead of Jakobsen and Alberto Dainese, after a big crash 11 km from the finish had caught Romain Bardet and Taaramäe among others. Kenny Elissonde took the red jersey, five seconds ahead of Roglič.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 8

Jakobsen wins again and takes back green

Despite Astana pushing the pace for echelons 38 km out, which briefly split the bunch into three groups, the stage ended in a mass sprint: Fabio Jakobsen won ahead of Alberto Dainese and Philipsen. He took the green jersey back from Philipsen, while Roglič kept the red jersey.
