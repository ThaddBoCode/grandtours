In the headlines

Giro 2023 · Stage 8

Healy solos 50 km to his first Grand Tour stage win

Ben Healy attacked about 50 km from the finish and won alone in Fossombrone, 1:49 ahead of Derek Gee, Filippo Zana and Warren Barguil, for his first Grand Tour stage win. Behind, Remco Evenepoel lost time to Primož Roglič and Geraint Thomas, while Andreas Leknessund kept pink by eight seconds over him.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 18

Zana beats Pinot at Zoldo Alto

Filippo Zana beat Thibaut Pinot at Zoldo Alto, with Warren Barguil third at 50 seconds, and Pinot took back the maglia azzurra. Primož Roglič and Geraint Thomas finished 21 seconds ahead of João Almeida, so Roglič moved up to second overall, 29 seconds behind Thomas.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 11

Vingegaard breaks Pogačar on the Granon and takes yellow

Jumbo–Visma attacked Pogačar repeatedly on the Télégraphe and the Galibier, where Warren Barguil was first over the top. On the Col du Granon Vingegaard went clear 4.5 km from the finish and won alone, 59 seconds ahead of Nairo Quintana, while Pogačar lost 2:51. Vingegaard took yellow, 2:16 ahead of Romain Bardet and 2:22 ahead of Pogačar; Mathieu van der Poel abandoned.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 13

Pedersen wins from a three-man group in Saint-Étienne

Warren Barguil did not start after a positive COVID-19 test. Mads Pedersen attacked his breakaway companions 10.6 km from the finish, only Fred Wright and Hugo Houle could go with him, and he won the sprint for his first Grand Tour stage. The peloton finished almost six minutes down, with no changes at the top of the general classification.
