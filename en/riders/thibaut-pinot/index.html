In the headlines

Tour 2023 · Stage 20

Pinot's farewell on the Petit Ballon

In his last Tour before retirement, Thibaut Pinot attacked from the breakaway on stage 20 and crossed the Petit Ballon alone, on a climb lined by thousands of cheering fans. He was caught on the Col du Platzerwasel and Pogačar won in Le Markstein ahead of Felix Gall and Vingegaard, while Pinot received the day's combativity award.

 Giro 2023

Pinot wins the maglia azzurra and finishes fifth

Thibaut Pinot won the mountains classification with 237 points ahead of Derek Gee and finished fifth overall. He was runner-up on two stages: at Crans-Montana behind Einer Rubio and at Zoldo Alto behind Filippo Zana.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 18

Zana beats Pinot at Zoldo Alto

Filippo Zana beat Thibaut Pinot at Zoldo Alto, with Warren Barguil third at 50 seconds, and Pinot took back the maglia azzurra. Primož Roglič and Geraint Thomas finished 21 seconds ahead of João Almeida, so Roglič moved up to second overall, 29 seconds behind Thomas.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 8

Peters wins in Loudenvielle as Pinot's hopes fade

Nans Peters dropped Ilnur Zakarin on the descent of the Port de Balès and held on over the Peyresourde to win from a 13-man break, 47 seconds ahead of Toms Skujiņš and Carlos Verona. Behind, Tadej Pogačar attacked on the Peyresourde and gained around 40 seconds on the yellow-jersey group. Thibaut Pinot, suffering from back pain, lost almost 19 minutes, while Adam Yates kept yellow by 3 seconds.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 13

Bad weather cuts stage 13 to 74.6 km

Stage 13, originally 207 km long and then rerouted to 199 km, was shortened to 74.6 km before the start because of bad weather, with the start moved from Borgofranco d'Ivrea to Le Châble in Switzerland. Einer Rubio won at Crans-Montana ahead of Thibaut Pinot and Jefferson Alexander Cepeda.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 3

Matthews claims the uphill sprint in Melfi

Michael Matthews won the uphill sprint in Melfi ahead of Mads Pedersen and Kaden Groves. Evenepoel stayed in pink, Primož Roglič moved up to third overall at 44 seconds, and Thibaut Pinot took the maglia azzurra.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 21

Cavendish wins in Rome, Roglič wins the Giro

Mark Cavendish won the final sprint in Rome ahead of Alex Kirsch and Filippo Fiorelli. Primož Roglič secured overall victory by 14 seconds over Geraint Thomas, becoming the first Slovenian to win the Giro; Jonathan Milan won the maglia ciclamino, Thibaut Pinot the maglia azzurra and João Almeida the maglia bianca.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 9

Jungels solos to victory in Châtel

Bob Jungels attacked from the big breakaway on the Col de la Croix with 64 km to go and held on alone for his first Tour stage win, 22 seconds ahead of Jonathan Castroviejo and 26 ahead of Carlos Verona; Thibaut Pinot, who had chased hard, was passed in the final kilometre. Simon Geschke took the polka-dot jersey, and Pogačar kept yellow before the rest day.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 8

Vine wins again at Colláu Fancuaya, Pedersen takes green

Jay Vine won his second stage from the break after dropping his companions 6.2 km out; Marc Soler was second at 43 seconds, level with Rein Taaramäe, and Thibaut Pinot fourth at 47 seconds. Vine took the polka-dot jersey, and Mads Pedersen the green jersey at the intermediate sprint. Evenepoel, Mas and Roglič finished 1:20 behind Vine, and the overall order stayed the same.
