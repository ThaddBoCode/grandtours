In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 16

Evenepoel wins the time trial, Lipowitz crashes out

Evenepoel won the race's only individual time trial, over 26.1 km, 28 seconds ahead of Pogačar and over a minute ahead of Mattias Skjelmose – his third Tour time-trial win and his second stage win in a row. Florian Lipowitz crashed on a descent, broke his collarbone and abandoned. Seixas was fourth, five seconds faster than del Toro, cutting the gap in the fight for third overall to 20 seconds; Pogačar now led Evenepoel by 4:32.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 14

Evenepoel quits on the Tourmalet, Arensman wins at Superbagnères

Third-placed Remco Evenepoel was dropped early on the Tourmalet and abandoned, while Mattias Skjelmose also left after a crash. Thymen Arensman attacked from the break on the Peyresourde, 37 km from the finish, and won his first Tour stage at Superbagnères, 1:08 ahead of Tadej Pogačar, who gained 4 seconds on Vingegaard in the final metres. Florian Lipowitz moved up to third overall and took the white jersey.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 19

Roglič attacks on Moncalvillo and takes the red jersey back

Before stage 19, Roglič and O'Connor were only 5 seconds apart. Roglič won at the Alto de Moncalvillo, 46 seconds ahead of David Gaudu and Mattias Skjelmose, and led O'Connor by 1:54 afterwards. That effectively decided the general classification; Skjelmose took the white jersey.
