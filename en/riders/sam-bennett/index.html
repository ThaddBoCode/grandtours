In the headlines

Tour 2021 · Stage 13

Cavendish equals Merckx's record of 34 stage wins

Mark Cavendish, a late replacement for the injured Sam Bennett at Deceuninck–Quick-Step, won in Fougères for his first Tour stage win since 2016 and added further wins in Châteauroux, Valence and Carcassonne. His fourth victory was his 34th Tour stage win, equalling Eddy Merckx's record. He wore the green jersey from stage 4 to Paris and won the points classification for the second time.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 21

Bennett beats Sagan to the green jersey

Sam Bennett won his first Tour stage on Île de Ré, took the green jersey that day and kept it to Paris, where he also won on the Champs-Élysées. Peter Sagan, chasing a record eighth points title, was relegated in Poitiers for shouldering Wout van Aert and could not close the gap – it was the first Tour he finished without winning the points classification.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 3

Ewan weaves through to win in Sisteron

Jérôme Cousin rode alone at the front for over 100 km and was caught 16 km from the finish, while Anthony Perez, on course for the polka-dot jersey, crashed into his team car on a descent and had to abandon. Caleb Ewan came from far back and passed Sam Bennett on the line. Peter Sagan took the green jersey; Alaphilippe stayed in yellow.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 5

Van Aert wins in Privas, Alaphilippe penalised out of yellow

With no breakaway on a mostly downhill stage, Wout van Aert came off Cees Bol's wheel to win the slightly uphill sprint – his first Grand Tour stage win – ahead of Bol and Sam Bennett, who took the green jersey. After the stage Julian Alaphilippe was given a 20-second penalty for an illegal feed inside the last 20 km, and Adam Yates took the yellow jersey, 3 seconds ahead of Primož Roglič.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 7

Van Aert wins again as crosswinds catch out Pogačar

Bora–Hansgrohe set a fierce pace from the start to drop Sam Bennett and other sprinters, and later INEOS Grenadiers used the crosswinds to split the peloton. Wout van Aert won his second stage in three days ahead of Edvald Boasson Hagen and Bryan Coquard. Tadej Pogačar, Mikel Landa, Richie Porte and Bauke Mollema were caught behind and lost 1:21; Peter Sagan took back green and Egan Bernal the white jersey.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 10

Bennett wins his first Tour stage and takes green

On the island-to-island stage from Oléron to Ré, crashes hit the peloton: Sam Bewley abandoned with a broken wrist, and Pogačar's teammate Davide Formolo fractured his collarbone and left the race after the stage. Led out by Michael Mørkøv, Sam Bennett held off Caleb Ewan and Peter Sagan for his first Tour de France stage win and took the green jersey. Primož Roglič stayed in yellow.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 11

Ewan wins a photo finish in Poitiers, Sagan relegated

After Mathieu Ladagnous's solo breakaway was caught, four riders threw their bikes at the line and Caleb Ewan won his second stage of the Tour. Peter Sagan, initially second, was relegated for shouldering Wout van Aert in the sprint, so Sam Bennett moved up to second and extended his green-jersey lead to 68 points. Ion Izagirre abandoned after a crash 30 km from the finish.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 14

Kragh Andersen escapes in the finale in Lyon

Bora–Hansgrohe drove the pace on the Col du Béal to drop Sam Bennett and other sprinters. In a finale full of attacks, Søren Kragh Andersen went clear 3 km from the finish and won alone by 15 seconds; Luka Mezgec won the sprint for second ahead of Simone Consonni and Peter Sagan. Roglič kept yellow and Bennett the green jersey.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 19

Kragh Andersen doubles up in Champagnole

After Rémi Cavagna's early solo attack was neutralised, a 12-rider group including Sam Bennett, Peter Sagan and Matteo Trentin went clear in the finale. Søren Kragh Andersen attacked 16 km from the finish and won alone by 53 seconds ahead of Luka Mezgec and Jasper Stuyven – his second stage win of this Tour. The general classification remained unchanged before the time trial.
