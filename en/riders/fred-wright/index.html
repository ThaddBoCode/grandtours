In the headlines

Tour 2022 · Stage 8

Van Aert takes his second stage win in Lausanne

Before the start, Pogačar's teammate Vegard Stake Laengen left the race with COVID-19. Fred Wright, the last of the breakaway, was caught 3.5 km from the finish on the climb to Lausanne, where van Aert won the uphill sprint ahead of Michael Matthews and Pogačar. Pogačar's 4 bonus seconds increased his lead over Vingegaard to 39 seconds.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 13

Pedersen wins from a three-man group in Saint-Étienne

Warren Barguil did not start after a positive COVID-19 test. Mads Pedersen attacked his breakaway companions 10.6 km from the finish, only Fred Wright and Hugo Houle could go with him, and he won the sprint for his first Grand Tour stage. The peloton finished almost six minutes down, with no changes at the top of the general classification.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 19

Laporte takes the first French stage win of this Tour

Protesters on the road briefly neutralised the race early on, and Enric Mas had left with COVID-19 before the start. After Alexis Gougeard, Fred Wright and Jasper Stuyven had been reeled in, Christophe Laporte jumped from the peloton in the final two kilometres and held off the sprinters in Cahors by one second, with Jasper Philipsen second. It was the first stage win by a French rider in this Tour.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 16

Roglič crashes out of second place

In the finale of stage 16, Primož Roglič, the three-time defending champion, collided with Fred Wright in a five-man sprint and crashed heavily, crossing the line with cuts on his right side. He was second overall, 1:26 behind Remco Evenepoel, but did not start stage 17. Evenepoel then led Enric Mas by 2:01.

 Vuelta 2022

Pedersen: three stage wins and the green jersey

Mads Pedersen won stages 13, 16 and 19 and took the green jersey from Sam Bennett on stage 8, keeping it to Madrid. He finished the points classification with 409 points, ahead of Fred Wright (186) and Enric Mas (138). Bennett had won stages 2 and 3 in the Netherlands and left with COVID-19 before stage 10.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 5

Soler wins solo in Bilbao, Molard takes red

Marc Soler bridged to a 17-man break, went clear on the second ascent of the Alto del Vivero and held off the chasers, who came within six seconds, to win in Bilbao. Daryl Impey and Fred Wright followed at four seconds; with the peloton more than five minutes down, fourth-placed Rudy Molard took the red jersey, two seconds ahead of Wright.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 7

Herrada wins from the break in Cistierna

Six riders went clear early and held off the peloton, with Fred Wright starting the sprint 300 m out. Jesús Herrada won his second Vuelta stage ahead of Samuele Battistella and Wright. Sam Bennett led the peloton home 29 seconds later, and Remco Evenepoel stayed in red as the top ten did not change.
