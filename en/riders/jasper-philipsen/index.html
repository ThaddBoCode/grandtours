In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 17

Philipsen wins in Voiron as the green battle tightens

Pedersen bridged to a large breakaway and won the intermediate sprint, after which a series of attacks followed. In the sprint of a reduced group, Mathieu van der Poel led out Jasper Philipsen, who won ahead of Mauro Schmid and Olav Kooij. Pedersen finished only eighth and his lead in the points classification shrank to seven points.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 3

Crashes knock out Philipsen and Almeida

Jasper Philipsen, winner of stage 1 and the first yellow jersey, crashed in the intermediate sprint on stage 3 and abandoned with a broken collarbone and broken ribs. Pogačar's teammate João Almeida broke a rib in a crash on stage 7 and abandoned two days later, while Filippo Ganna and Stefan Bissegger had already left on stage 1 with concussions.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 1

Philipsen takes first yellow after Visma split the race in Lille

Visma–Lease a Bike split the peloton in the crosswinds 17 km from the finish, and Jasper Philipsen won the sprint of the reduced front group ahead of Biniam Girmay to take the first yellow jersey. Pogačar and Vingegaard made the split, while Remco Evenepoel and Primož Roglič lost 39 seconds. Filippo Ganna and Stefan Bissegger abandoned after crashes with concussions.

 Vuelta 2025

Pedersen wins the green jersey while Philipsen takes three sprints

Mads Pedersen of Lidl–Trek led the points classification from stage 4 to the end and won it with 277 points, ahead of Jonas Vingegaard (197) and Jasper Philipsen (135). He collected many high placings in the sprints and at the intermediate sprints, and won stage 15 from a breakaway. Philipsen took the bunch sprints on stages 1, 8 and 19.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 8

Philipsen sprints to his second win in Zaragoza

On the flat stage to Zaragoza, with no categorised climbs, Jasper Philipsen won the expected bunch sprint for his second stage of this Vuelta. Philipsen came around Elia Viviani, who was later relegated for deviating too much during the sprint.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 19

Philipsen takes a third stage win in Guijuelo

On the transition stage from Rueda to Guijuelo, Jasper Philipsen won the bunch sprint comfortably ahead of Mads Pedersen and Orluis Aular, his third stage of the race after stages 1 and 8. Earlier, Visma–Lease a Bike had delivered Jonas Vingegaard to the front at the intermediate sprint, where he took four bonus seconds and stretched his lead to 44 seconds.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 10

Philipsen finally gets his win in Saint-Amand-Montrond

After a quiet stage from Orléans, Jasper Philipsen, led out by Mathieu van der Poel, won the bunch sprint for his first stage win of this Tour. Green jersey Biniam Girmay was second and Pascal Ackermann third. There were no changes at the top of the classifications.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 13

Philipsen wins a contested sprint in Pau

With Primož Roglič a non-starter and Juan Ayuso abandoning during the stage, crosswinds again briefly split the peloton before the race came back together for a sprint in Pau. Jasper Philipsen won ahead of Wout van Aert, Pascal Ackermann and Biniam Girmay, after a heavy crash 700 m from the line had held up many riders. Rivals criticised Philipsen's sprint, and Ackermann refused to shake his hand.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 16

Philipsen's third win in Nîmes as Girmay crashes

Thomas Gachignard rode alone at the front for much of the second half but was caught 25 km from Nîmes. Green jersey Biniam Girmay crashed 1.5 km from the finish, and Jasper Philipsen, led out by Robbe Ghys and Mathieu van der Poel, won ahead of Phil Bauhaus and Alexander Kristoff. It was Philipsen's third stage win, matching Girmay's tally.
