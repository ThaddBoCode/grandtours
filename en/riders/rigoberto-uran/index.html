In the headlines

Giro 2023 · Stage 10

Cort wins in Viareggio as Thomas takes over the maglia rosa

Nine riders, including Evenepoel, Domenico Pozzovivo, Stefan Küng and Rigoberto Urán, did not start after the rest day, so Geraint Thomas rode in pink, two seconds ahead of Primož Roglič. Magnus Cort beat Derek Gee and Alessandro De Marchi, 51 seconds clear of the bunch, while Aleksandr Vlasov abandoned and Jay Vine crashed and lost more than ten minutes.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 17

Urán wins at Tentudía as Roglič abandons

Rigoberto Urán won from a 13-man break after Lawson Craddock was caught shortly before the final kilometre, holding off Quentin Pacher, with Jesús Herrada third. The win completed his set of stage wins at all three Grand Tours. Primož Roglič did not start after his crash, leaving Remco Evenepoel 2:01 ahead of Enric Mas, with Juan Ayuso moving onto the podium.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 11

Van Aert conquers the double Ventoux

On the first Tour stage to climb Mont Ventoux twice, Wout van Aert went clear from the breakaway on the second ascent and soloed to Malaucène, 1:14 ahead of Kenny Elissonde and Bauke Mollema. Behind, Jonas Vingegaard dropped Tadej Pogačar near the summit, but Pogačar, Richard Carapaz and Rigoberto Urán caught him near the flamme rouge. Urán moved up to second, 5:18 down, while Ben O'Connor lost about four minutes and David Gaudu more than 25.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 15

Kuss wins in Andorra, Poels back in polka dots

From a 32-rider breakaway, Sepp Kuss attacked 5 km from the top of the Col de Beixalis and won his first Tour stage in Andorra la Vella, 23 seconds ahead of Alejandro Valverde. Nairo Quintana was first over the Port d'Envalira, the highest point of the race, and Wout Poels took back the polka-dot jersey. Pogačar stayed in yellow 5:18 ahead of Rigoberto Urán, while Guillaume Martin lost almost four minutes and dropped to ninth.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 17

Pogačar wins atop the Col de Portet on Bastille Day

On Bastille Day Anthony Perez was the last survivor of a breakaway featuring four French riders before Tadej Pogačar attacked about 8.5 km from the summit of the Col de Portet. Only Jonas Vingegaard and Richard Carapaz could follow, and Pogačar won the three-man sprint at 2,215 m, 3 seconds ahead of Vingegaard and 4 ahead of Carapaz. Vingegaard moved up to second overall at 5:39 and Carapaz to third at 5:43, while Rigoberto Urán dropped to fourth.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 18

Pogačar doubles up at Luz Ardiden and takes polka dots

Pierre Latour was first over the Col du Tourmalet, and David Gaudu was the last of the breakaway to be caught on the final climb. Tadej Pogačar attacked 3.3 km from the top of Luz Ardiden, closed down two moves by Enric Mas in the final kilometre and won ahead of Jonas Vingegaard and Richard Carapaz, both 2 seconds back. With 80 points from his two summit wins Pogačar also took the polka-dot jersey, and his lead grew to 5:45 over Vingegaard; Rigoberto Urán lost almost nine minutes.
