In the headlines

Vuelta 2023 · Stage 4

Groves wins the sprint in Tarragona

Eduardo Sepúlveda, Ander Okamika and David González formed the break and were caught about 20 km from the finish. After a crash 4 km out, Kaden Groves won the sprint ahead of Juan Sebastián Molano and Edward Theuns. Groves took the green jersey and Sepúlveda the polka-dot jersey, while Remco Evenepoel stayed in red.

 Vuelta 2023 · Stage 7

Soupe sprints to victory in Oliva ahead of Aular

José Herrada and Ander Okamika attacked from kilometre 0 and were caught after about 160 km. Two crashes 10 and 5 km from the finish did not change the overall standings. Geoffrey Soupe won the sprint in Oliva ahead of Orluis Aular and Edward Theuns, and Lenny Martinez stayed in red.
