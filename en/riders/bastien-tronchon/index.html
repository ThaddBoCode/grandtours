Tronchon surprises the sprinters in Elche de la Sierra

Bastien Tronchon (Groupama–FDJ United) won a frantic finale in Elche de la Sierra in 4:07:41 ahead of Magnus Cort and Thibau Nys. Wout van Aert was fourth despite the heavy work of Visma–Lease a Bike. Mas kept the red jersey, with Roglič still 1:18 behind.
