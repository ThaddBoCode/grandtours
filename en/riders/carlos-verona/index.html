In the headlines

Giro 2025 · Stage 15

Verona wins alone in Asiago, Roglič loses time

Carlos Verona attacked from the break on the climb of Dori, 43 km from the finish, and won alone in Asiago, 22 seconds ahead of Florian Stork and 23 ahead of Christian Scaroni. Primož Roglič finished almost two minutes behind the winner and dropped to tenth overall, 3:53 down. Del Toro kept the maglia rosa, 1:20 ahead of Simon Yates.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 21

Sobrero wins in Verona, Hindley seals the Giro

Italian time trial champion Matteo Sobrero won the 17.4 km time trial in 22:24, 23 seconds ahead of Thymen Arensman and 40 ahead of Mathieu van der Poel, for his first Grand Tour stage win. Jai Hindley finished 15th to win the Giro by 1:18 over Carapaz, with Landa third; Hugh Carthy moved up to ninth, but Juan Pedro López kept the maglia bianca.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 9

Jungels solos to victory in Châtel

Bob Jungels attacked from the big breakaway on the Col de la Croix with 64 km to go and held on alone for his first Tour stage win, 22 seconds ahead of Jonathan Castroviejo and 26 ahead of Carlos Verona; Thibaut Pinot, who had chased hard, was passed in the final kilometre. Simon Geschke took the polka-dot jersey, and Pogačar kept yellow before the rest day.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 20

Hindley breaks Carapaz on the Marmolada

Carapaz and Hindley were separated by just three seconds after stage 16 and stayed inseparable until the penultimate stage. On the steep final kilometres of the Passo Fedaia, Hindley attacked, linked up with teammate Lennard Kämna and dropped Carapaz inside the last 3 km, taking the maglia rosa with a lead of 1:25 that he defended in the Verona time trial.

 Giro 2022

Démare rules the sprints and wins the maglia ciclamino

Arnaud Démare was the race's best sprinter, winning in Messina, Scalea and Cuneo, and took the points classification with 254 points ahead of Fernando Gaviria and Mark Cavendish. He led the classification from stage 5 all the way to Verona.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 13

Nizzolo finally wins a Grand Tour stage

Giacomo Nizzolo used Fernando Gaviria's slipstream and came past Edoardo Affini, who had attacked late, to win the flat stage in Verona. It was Nizzolo's first Grand Tour stage win after eleven second places, and Peter Sagan was third to keep the maglia ciclamino.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 8

Peters wins in Loudenvielle as Pinot's hopes fade

Nans Peters dropped Ilnur Zakarin on the descent of the Port de Balès and held on over the Peyresourde to win from a 13-man break, 47 seconds ahead of Toms Skujiņš and Carlos Verona. Behind, Tadej Pogačar attacked on the Peyresourde and gained around 40 seconds on the yellow-jersey group. Thibaut Pinot, suffering from back pain, lost almost 19 minutes, while Adam Yates kept yellow by 3 seconds.
