In the headlines

Vuelta 2024 · Stage 18

Berrade wins solo in Maeztu, Soler takes the polka-dot jersey

From a leading group of 42 riders, Urko Berrade (Equipo Kern Pharma) counter-attacked after a move by Steven Kruijswijk, held off the chasers and won in Maeztu ahead of Mauro Schmid and Max Poole (both +0:04). Marc Soler took the polka-dot jersey. In the standings O'Connor stayed 5 seconds ahead of Roglič.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 12

Omrzel wins at Calar Alto, Mas gains time on his rivals

Jakob Omrzel, the youngest rider in the race, won from the breakaway at the Calar Alto summit in 4:29:53, 1:56 ahead of Urko Berrade and 2:13 ahead of Santiago Buitrago. In the favourites' group Enric Mas attacked and took 0:27 on Roglič and Gall. Roglič was now 1:45 behind the red jersey and Gall 2:06.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 15

ProTeam Kern Pharma wins three stages, two of them by Castrillo

Pablo Castrillo (Equipo Kern Pharma) won stage 12 at Manzaneda and stage 15 at Valgrande-Pajares, each time from a breakaway, and Urko Berrade added a third win for the Spanish ProTeam on stage 18. On the Cuitu Negru Castrillo held off Aleksandr Vlasov by 12 seconds.
