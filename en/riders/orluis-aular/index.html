In the headlines

Vuelta 2023 · Stage 7

Soupe sprints to victory in Oliva ahead of Aular

José Herrada and Ander Okamika attacked from kilometre 0 and were caught after about 160 km. Two crashes 10 and 5 km from the finish did not change the overall standings. Geoffrey Soupe won the sprint in Oliva ahead of Orluis Aular and Edward Theuns, and Lenny Martinez stayed in red.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 4

Narváez wins in Cosenza, bonus seconds put Ciccone in pink

Back in Italy, Movistar tried to drop the sprinters on Cozzo Tunno for Orluis Aular; Kaden Groves and Arnaud De Lie abandoned. In the reduced sprint, though, Jhonatan Narváez (UAE Team Emirates XRG) beat Aular and Giulio Ciccone. Ciccone took the maglia rosa thanks to the bonus seconds, 4 seconds ahead of Jan Christen, the new maglia bianca, and Florian Stork.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 1

Pedersen edges van Aert in Tirana for the first maglia rosa

The Giro opened in Albania with a hilly stage from Durrës to Tirana, where a crash 5 km from the finish forced Mikel Landa out. Mads Pedersen won the sprint of a reduced group just ahead of Wout van Aert and Orluis Aular to take the maglia rosa and the maglia ciclamino. Sylvain Moniquet took the mountains lead from the day's break, Francesco Busatto the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 3

Pedersen sprints to victory in Vlorë and reclaims the maglia rosa

Lorenzo Fortunato and Pello Bilbao crossed the Llogara pass together in front but were caught 17 km from the finish in Vlorë. Led out by Giulio Ciccone and Mathias Vacek, Mads Pedersen won the sprint ahead of Corbin Strong and Orluis Aular and took back the maglia rosa, 9 seconds ahead of Roglič. Fortunato moved into the maglia azzurra.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 15

Pedersen wins from the break in Monforte de Lemos

A group of 47 riders went away after the first climb, and Jay Vine and Louis Vervaeke attacked from it before being caught by a seven-man chase group with 6.8 km to go. Mads Pedersen outsprinted Orluis Aular for his first win of the race, with the peloton crossing the line more than 13 minutes later.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 19

Philipsen takes a third stage win in Guijuelo

On the transition stage from Rueda to Guijuelo, Jasper Philipsen won the bunch sprint comfortably ahead of Mads Pedersen and Orluis Aular, his third stage of the race after stages 1 and 8. Earlier, Visma–Lease a Bike had delivered Jonas Vingegaard to the front at the intermediate sprint, where he took four bonus seconds and stretched his lead to 44 seconds.
