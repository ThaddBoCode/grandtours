In the headlines

Tour 2025 · Stage 17

Milan takes his second stage in Valence after a late crash

A crash just before the flamme rouge brought down several riders, including Biniam Girmay, and held up Tim Merlier, leaving a small group to contest the sprint. Jonathan Milan, who had been dropped on the first climb before his team brought him back, held off Jordi Meeus for his second stage win and stretched his points lead to 72 over Tadej Pogačar. Carlos Rodríguez, tenth overall, fractured his pelvis in a crash in the closing stages and did not start the next day.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 16

Vingegaard crushes the Combloux time trial

Going into the only individual time trial, 22.4 km from Passy to Combloux, Vingegaard led Pogačar by just 10 seconds. He won by 1:38 over the Slovenian, with teammate Wout van Aert third at 2:51, and stretched his lead to 1:48. Adam Yates moved up to third overall, five seconds ahead of Carlos Rodríguez.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 14

Rodríguez wins in Morzine after the Joux Plane duel

A mass crash shortly after the start led to the race being neutralised and several riders abandoned; Romain Bardet later crashed out too. On the Col de Joux Plane Pogačar could not shake off Vingegaard, and his last attack was obstructed by motorbikes crowding the road. Carlos Rodríguez caught the pair on the descent and won in Morzine, moving into third overall one second ahead of Hindley; Vingegaard now led by 10 seconds and also took the polka-dot jersey.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 20

Pogačar wins in Le Markstein as Pinot bids farewell

In his last Tour, Thibaut Pinot attacked from the breakaway and crossed the Petit Ballon alone to the cheers of thousands of fans, but was caught on the Col du Platzerwasel. Pogačar won the sprint of a small group ahead of Felix Gall and Vingegaard, cutting his deficit to 7:29, and Giulio Ciccone secured the polka-dot jersey. After crashing early in the stage, Carlos Rodríguez lost fourth place overall to Simon Yates.
