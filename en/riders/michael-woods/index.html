In the headlines

Vuelta 2024 · Stage 13

Woods wins at Puerto de Ancares, O'Connor loses almost two minutes

Michael Woods (Israel–Premier Tech) attacked alone on the last climb and won ahead of Mauro Schmid (+0:45) and Marc Soler (+1:11). In the peloton Ben O'Connor lost 1:55 to Roglič but kept the red jersey, his lead down to 1:21. Wout van Aert took the lead of the mountains classification.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 7

Woods wins in Villanueva de Valdegovía

After the first rest day, Michael Woods (EF Pro Cycling) won the stage from Vitoria-Gasteiz to Villanueva de Valdegovía ahead of Omar Fraile and Alejandro Valverde (both +0:04). Carapaz kept the red jersey, and Guillaume Martin took the lead of the mountains classification.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 9

The Tour returns to the Puy de Dôme after 35 years

For the first time since 1988 a stage finished on top of the Puy de Dôme. Matteo Jorgenson attacked from the breakaway 47 km from the finish and led alone until Michael Woods passed him in the final 500 m to win; behind, Pogačar took 8 seconds out of Vingegaard.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 16

Houle solos to his first Grand Tour stage win in Foix

Hugo Houle attacked from the breakaway on the descent of the Port de Lers, 38 km from the finish, and won alone, 1:10 ahead of Valentin Madouas and his teammate Michael Woods. Pogačar attacked three times around the top of the Port de Lers but could not shake off Vingegaard, who kept his lead of 2:22. Lennard Kämna and Jakob Fuglsang did not start.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 3

Bennett doubles up in Breda, Affini takes the lead

Sam Bennett won his second stage in a row in the sprint in Breda, again ahead of Mads Pedersen, with Daniel McLay third. Edoardo Affini took the red jersey before the first rest day, and Michael Woods left the race after a crash about 139 km from the finish.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 14

Mollema solos to Quillan, Martin jumps to second

It took almost 100 km for a 14-rider breakaway including Guillaume Martin to form, and Bauke Mollema attacked from it on a descent 42 km from the finish. He soloed to victory in Quillan, 1:04 ahead of Patrick Konrad and Sergio Higuita. Martin rose from ninth to second overall, 4:04 behind Tadej Pogačar, and Michael Woods took the polka-dot jersey from Nairo Quintana.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 6

Izagirre wins at Formigal, Carapaz takes red

Ion Izagirre (Astana) won the mountain stage to Aramón Formigal ahead of Michael Woods and Rui Costa (both +0:25). Because of French COVID-19 restrictions, the originally planned 136.6 km stage to the Col du Tourmalet had been replaced by a 146.4 km stage to Formigal. Carapaz took the red jersey, 18 seconds ahead of Carthy and 20 ahead of Dan Martin; Roglič was 30 seconds back.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 14

Wellens wins in Ourense for a second time

Tim Wellens (Lotto–Soudal) took his second stage win of this Vuelta in Ourense, ahead of Michael Woods and Zdeněk Štybar (both on the same time). The general classification was unchanged: Roglič led by 39 seconds over Carapaz and 47 over Carthy.
