In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 11

Wærenskjold wins the fastest road stage in Tour history

A four-man breakaway including Mathis Le Berre was kept on a short leash and caught 6 km from the finish. In a disorganised sprint, Søren Wærenskjold jumped across to Kooij's lead-out man Cees Bol and won ahead of Kooij and Philipsen – his first Grand Tour stage win. Raced at an average of 50.9 km/h, it was the fastest road stage in Tour history.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 7

Merlier pounces in Bordeaux

Veistroffer was in the breakaway again, this time with Jakub Otruba, but the pair were never given more than two minutes. Alpecin–Premier Tech's lead-out for Jasper Philipsen ran out too early and Tim Merlier took advantage to win ahead of Søren Wærenskjold and Biniam Girmay. The classifications were unchanged, with Pedersen leading the points by around 60.
