In the headlines

Giro 2026 · Stage 15

Breakaway survives: Dversnes surprises in Milan

The flat stage to Milan looked made for the sprinters, but a four-man breakaway held on: Fredrik Dversnes (Uno-X Mobility) won ahead of Mirco Maestri, Martin Marcellusi and Mattia Bais, with the bunch 57 seconds behind. Paul Magnier, fifth, took back the maglia ciclamino, and Jonas Vingegaard stayed in pink.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 14

Asgreen solos to Nova Gorica as a crash hits the favourites

Kasper Asgreen, Mirco Maestri and Martin Marcellusi led for much of a rainy stage before Asgreen went alone 6 km from the finish in Nova Gorica, Slovenia, and won by 16 seconds from Kaden Groves and Olav Kooij. A crash 22 km from the finish brought down or held up Juan Ayuso, Primož Roglič, Antonio Tiberi and Giulio Ciccone, who did not start the next day. Simon Yates moved up to second overall, 1:20 behind del Toro.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 18

Denz solos to Cesano Maderno as Ayuso abandons

Juan Ayuso abandoned early in the stage, while a 34-rider breakaway gained more than ten minutes. Nico Denz attacked from a leading group of eleven 18 km from the finish and won alone in Cesano Maderno, 1:01 ahead of Mirco Maestri and Edward Planckaert. The maglia rosa group finished 13:51 down, and the top of the standings was unchanged.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 12

Alaphilippe wins in Fano after a long-range attack

Julian Alaphilippe attacked with Mirco Maestri more than 125 km from the finish, dropped him on the last climb and won in Fano – his first Grand Tour stage win since the 2021 Tour de France. It made him a stage winner in all three Grand Tours, and he also took the overall combativity award.
