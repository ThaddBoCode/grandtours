In the headlines

Tour 2024

Girmay makes history and wins the green jersey

Biniam Girmay won stage 3 in Turin to become the first Black African to win a Tour stage, and on stage 5 he became the first African to wear the green jersey. He added wins in Colombey-les-Deux-Églises and Villeneuve-sur-Lot and kept green all the way to Nice with 387 points, ahead of Jasper Philipsen (354), who also won three stages.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 3

Girmay makes history in Turin, Carapaz first Ecuadorian in yellow

A crash 2.3 km from the finish disrupted the sprint in Turin at the end of the longest stage of the race, and Biniam Girmay beat Fernando Gaviria and Arnaud De Lie. He became the first Black African to win a Tour stage and the first Eritrean stage winner. Richard Carapaz, level on time with Pogačar, took yellow thanks to his better finishing positions, the first Ecuadorian to wear it.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 8

Girmay doubles up in green at Colombey-les-Deux-Églises

Jonas Abrahamsen spent much of the day alone at the front but was caught in the finale. In the slightly uphill sprint, green jersey Biniam Girmay took his second stage win ahead of Jasper Philipsen and Arnaud De Lie. Mads Pedersen did not start, not having recovered from his stage 5 crash.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 12

Girmay's third win as a crash ends Roglič's Tour

Green jersey Biniam Girmay won his third stage in Villeneuve-sur-Lot ahead of Wout van Aert and Pascal Ackermann, while Arnaud Démare and Mark Cavendish were relegated for irregular sprinting. A crash about 12 km from the finish brought down Primož Roglič, who lost almost two and a half minutes and dropped to sixth overall, 4:42 behind. Roglič did not start the next day.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 16

Philipsen's third win in Nîmes as Girmay crashes

Thomas Gachignard rode alone at the front for much of the second half but was caught 25 km from Nîmes. Green jersey Biniam Girmay crashed 1.5 km from the finish, and Jasper Philipsen, led out by Robbe Ghys and Mathieu van der Poel, won ahead of Phil Bauhaus and Alexander Kristoff. It was Philipsen's third stage win, matching Girmay's tally.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 10

Girmay makes history in Jesi – then a cork hits his eye

Biniam Girmay launched a long sprint in Jesi and held off Mathieu van der Poel to become the first Black African rider to win a Grand Tour stage. On the podium the cork of his Prosecco bottle hit his left eye; he was taken to hospital and did not start the next stage to let the injury heal.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 7

Merlier pounces in Bordeaux

Veistroffer was in the breakaway again, this time with Jakub Otruba, but the pair were never given more than two minutes. Alpecin–Premier Tech's lead-out for Jasper Philipsen ran out too early and Tim Merlier took advantage to win ahead of Søren Wærenskjold and Biniam Girmay. The classifications were unchanged, with Pedersen leading the points by around 60.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 8

Merlier makes it two in a row in Bergerac

Liam Slock, the last survivor of a three-man breakaway, held off the peloton until around the final kilometre. Merlier, still several dozen metres back with 500 m to go, had the strongest acceleration and beat Girmay, Kooij and Philipsen for his second consecutive stage win. He moved up to second in the points classification, 15 points behind Pedersen.

 Tour 2025

Milan wins green and ends Italy's wait for a stage win

Jonathan Milan won the points classification with 372 points, ahead of Pogačar and Biniam Girmay, after winning in Laval and Valence and scoring consistently in the intermediate sprints. His stage 8 win was the first Tour stage won by an Italian since Vincenzo Nibali in 2019.
