In the headlines

Vuelta 2026 · Stage 1

Pogačar wins the Monaco time trial and the first red jersey

Tadej Pogačar won the 9 km opening time trial through the streets of Monaco in 10:57 and took the first red jersey. Ethan Hayter was second at the same listed time, Joshua Tarling third at 0:04. Tarling became the first leader of the young rider classification and Callum Thornley of the mountains classification.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 10

Hoole wins the Pisa time trial, del Toro defends pink

Rain arrived during the 28.6 km time trial from Lucca to Pisa and favoured the early starters. Daan Hoole won by 7 seconds from Joshua Tarling and 10 from Ethan Hayter. Del Toro kept the maglia rosa, but his lead over Ayuso shrank to 25 seconds.
