In the headlines

Tour 2023 · Stage 15

Poels takes his first Tour stage win at Saint-Gervais

On a stage where a crash caused by a spectator brought down many riders, a big group got away. Wout Poels attacked on the Côte des Amerands 11 km from the finish and won alone at Saint-Gervais, 2:08 ahead of Wout van Aert – his first Tour stage win after years as a super-domestique. Pogačar and Vingegaard finished together, and Giulio Ciccone took the polka-dot jersey.

 Vuelta 2023 · Stage 20

Poels wins in Guadarrama, narrowly ahead of Evenepoel

A 31-rider break formed after half an hour and built a lead of up to nine minutes. On the last climb Wout Poels and Lennert Van Eetvelt attacked, and Marc Soler, Pelayo Sánchez and Remco Evenepoel joined them. Poels started the sprint early and held off Evenepoel on the line, while Kuss and his team-mates finished with the favourites and kept the red jersey with a 17-second lead.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 15

Kuss wins in Andorra, Poels back in polka dots

From a 32-rider breakaway, Sepp Kuss attacked 5 km from the top of the Col de Beixalis and won his first Tour stage in Andorra la Vella, 23 seconds ahead of Alejandro Valverde. Nairo Quintana was first over the Port d'Envalira, the highest point of the race, and Wout Poels took back the polka-dot jersey. Pogačar stayed in yellow 5:18 ahead of Rigoberto Urán, while Guillaume Martin lost almost four minutes and dropped to ninth.

 Vuelta 2023 · Stage 16

Vingegaard attacks to win at Bejes, Kuss leads by only 29 seconds

After a six-rider break was caught 10 km out, Jonas Vingegaard attacked 4 km from the summit finish at Bejes. He won 43 seconds ahead of Finn Fisher-Black and 49 seconds ahead of Wout Poels. Kuss stayed in red, but his lead over Vingegaard shrank to 29 seconds, with Roglič at 1:33.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 8

Teuns wins in Le Grand-Bornand, Pogačar storms into yellow

On the first Alpine stage, partly in the rain, Geraint Thomas and Primož Roglič lost more than 30 minutes and Mathieu van der Poel was dropped on the Col de Romme. Tadej Pogačar attacked 4 km from the top of the Romme, dropped Richard Carapaz and gained almost three and a half minutes on his rivals, while Dylan Teuns soloed to victory, 44 seconds ahead of Ion Izagirre. Pogačar took yellow with a lead of 1:48 over Wout van Aert, and Wout Poels the polka-dot jersey.
