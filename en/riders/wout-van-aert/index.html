In the headlines

Tour 2025 · Stage 21

Montmartre in the finale: van Aert wins in Paris

For the final stage the organisers added three climbs of the Butte Montmartre, inspired by the Paris 2024 Olympic road race – a change some riders had criticised beforehand. In the rain, with GC times neutralised, Wout van Aert dropped Pogačar on the last ascent and won alone on the Champs-Élysées; the organisers later said the stage had exceeded their expectations.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 8

Milan wins in Laval – first Italian Tour stage win since 2019

On a quiet day, Jonathan Milan came around Mathieu van der Poel, who was leading out Kaden Groves, and beat Wout van Aert to win his first Tour stage. He was the first Italian to win a Tour stage since Vincenzo Nibali in 2019, and he regained the green jersey. Tim Merlier was out of contention after a mechanical 12.5 km from the finish.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 20

Yates attacks as del Toro and Carapaz watch each other

On stage 20, Yates attacked 15 km from the summit of the Colle delle Finestre while del Toro and Carapaz marked each other and refused to cooperate. On the descent he was paced by teammate Wout van Aert, who had been in the break; Yates gained more than five minutes on both and turned a 1:21 deficit into a 3:56 lead.

 Giro 2025

Pedersen wins four stages and the maglia ciclamino

Mads Pedersen won stages 1, 3, 5 and 13, led the general classification after stage 1 and after stages 3 to 6, and won the points classification with 295 points ahead of Olav Kooij (185) and Wout van Aert (127).

 Giro 2025 · Stage 9

Van Aert wins on the white roads to Siena

Stage 9 crossed five gravel sectors of the Strade Bianche on the way to the Piazza del Campo. Wout van Aert was the only rider able to follow del Toro's attacks and passed him in the final metres, while Roglič crashed, punctured and dropped to tenth overall, 2:25 down.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 1

Pedersen edges van Aert in Tirana for the first maglia rosa

The Giro opened in Albania with a hilly stage from Durrës to Tirana, where a crash 5 km from the finish forced Mikel Landa out. Mads Pedersen won the sprint of a reduced group just ahead of Wout van Aert and Orluis Aular to take the maglia rosa and the maglia ciclamino. Sylvain Moniquet took the mountains lead from the day's break, Francesco Busatto the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 13

Pedersen holds off van Aert uphill in Vicenza for his fourth win

The finish in Vicenza climbed to the sanctuary of Monte Berico, with ramps of up to 12 %. Romain Bardet and Mathias Vacek were caught 500 m from the line, and maglia ciclamino Mads Pedersen launched his sprint from distance to hold off Wout van Aert for his fourth stage win, with del Toro third, 2 seconds back. Del Toro's lead over Ayuso grew to 38 seconds.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 6

Groenewegen wins by a tyre's width in windy Dijon

Echelons in the crosswind split the peloton for a while, catching out Carapaz, Ayuso and Philipsen among others, but the groups came back together. In Dijon Dylan Groenewegen won the sprint by a tyre's width; Jasper Philipsen crossed the line second but was relegated for impeding Wout van Aert, leaving second place to Biniam Girmay. Pogačar kept yellow.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 12

Girmay's third win as a crash ends Roglič's Tour

Green jersey Biniam Girmay won his third stage in Villeneuve-sur-Lot ahead of Wout van Aert and Pascal Ackermann, while Arnaud Démare and Mark Cavendish were relegated for irregular sprinting. A crash about 12 km from the finish brought down Primož Roglič, who lost almost two and a half minutes and dropped to sixth overall, 4:42 behind. Roglič did not start the next day.
