In the headlines

Tour 2024 · Stage 1

Bardet takes his first yellow jersey in his final Tour

In what he had announced as his last Tour, Romain Bardet attacked about 50 km from the finish of the opening stage, joined his teammate Frank van den Broek and won in Rimini 5 seconds ahead of the peloton. It was the first time he had worn the yellow jersey; he lost it to Pogačar the next day in Bologna.

 Giro 2022

Bardet, Yates and Almeida: contenders leave the race

Several favourites left early: Miguel Ángel López abandoned on stage 4 with a hip injury, Romain Bardet, then fourth overall, quit on stage 13 with stomach problems, and Simon Yates, winner of two stages, abandoned on stage 17. João Almeida, also fourth overall at the time, withdrew before stage 18 after testing positive for COVID-19.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 13

Démare takes his third win in Cuneo as Bardet abandons

Romain Bardet, fourth overall, abandoned with stomach problems before the Colle di Nava. The five-man break was only caught inside the final kilometre, and Arnaud Démare held off Phil Bauhaus and Mark Cavendish for his third stage win; Juan Pedro López extended his run in pink to ten days.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 14

Bardet wins at Pico Villuercas; Carapaz abandons

Romain Bardet emerged as the strongest of an 18-man break, dropped everyone on the final climb and won 44 seconds ahead of Jesús Herrada and Jay Vine, taking the mountains lead. Richard Carapaz abandoned, suffering in the high temperatures, and Eiking lost 20 seconds but stayed in red.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 13

Pedersen holds off van Aert uphill in Vicenza for his fourth win

The finish in Vicenza climbed to the sanctuary of Monte Berico, with ramps of up to 12 %. Romain Bardet and Mathias Vacek were caught 500 m from the line, and maglia ciclamino Mads Pedersen launched his sprint from distance to hold off Wout van Aert for his fourth stage win, with del Toro third, 2 seconds back. Del Toro's lead over Ayuso grew to 38 seconds.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 17

Del Toro rebounds to win in Bormio

After the Tonale and the Mortirolo, Richard Carapaz and maglia rosa Isaac del Toro caught Romain Bardet, the last of the break, before del Toro went clear alone to win in Bormio – his first Giro stage win. Bardet and Carapaz finished 4 seconds back; Carapaz moved up to second overall at 41 seconds, with Simon Yates third at 51 seconds.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 2

Vauquelin wins solo on San Luca, Pogačar takes yellow

Kévin Vauquelin went clear of the breakaway on the second ascent of San Luca and won alone in Bologna, 36 seconds ahead of Jonas Abrahamsen. Behind, Tadej Pogačar attacked on the climb and only Jonas Vingegaard could follow; Remco Evenepoel and Richard Carapaz rejoined them just before the line. Pogačar took yellow from the dropped Bardet on the same time as those three, and Abrahamsen also moved into the lead of the points classification.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 10

Paret-Peintre passes Tratnik to win at Cusano Mutri

Jan Tratnik went clear alone from a 26-rider breakaway and started the 17.9 km final climb to Bocca della Selva with a 50-second lead. Valentin Paret-Peintre caught and dropped him to win by 29 seconds over Romain Bardet, with Tratnik third. Nothing changed at the top of the standings; Olav Kooij did not start.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 14

Rodríguez wins in Morzine after the Joux Plane duel

A mass crash shortly after the start led to the race being neutralised and several riders abandoned; Romain Bardet later crashed out too. On the Col de Joux Plane Pogačar could not shake off Vingegaard, and his last attack was obstructed by motorbikes crowding the road. Carlos Rodríguez caught the pair on the descent and won in Morzine, moving into third overall one second ahead of Hindley; Vingegaard now led by 10 seconds and also took the polka-dot jersey.
