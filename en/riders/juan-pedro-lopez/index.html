In the headlines

Giro 2022 · Stage 4

Juan Pedro López wears pink for ten days

Juan Pedro López attacked from the breakaway on Etna and lost the two-man sprint to Lennard Kämna, but took the maglia rosa. The Trek–Segafredo rider held it for ten days, saved it on the Blockhaus by just 12 seconds, lost it in Turin and finished tenth overall – winning the maglia bianca as best young rider.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 14

Carapaz seizes the maglia rosa on a wild day around Turin

Bora–Hansgrohe blew the race apart with a ferocious pace on stage 14, catching the breakaway before the first ascent of Superga. Carapaz attacked on the second climb of Superga and, although Simon Yates won the stage, the Ecuadorian took the maglia rosa seven seconds ahead of Hindley; Juan Pedro López lost four and a half minutes after ten days in pink.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 6

Démare pips Ewan with a bike throw in Scalea

Diego Rosa rode alone in front for around 140 km before being caught 27.6 km from the finish. Arnaud Démare came out of Caleb Ewan's slipstream and beat him with a bike throw for his second consecutive stage win, with Mark Cavendish third; Juan Pedro López kept the maglia rosa.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 9

Hindley wins the six-man sprint on the Blockhaus

Richard Carapaz attacked 4.6 km from the top, followed only by Romain Bardet and Mikel Landa, before João Almeida, Jai Hindley and Domenico Pozzovivo bridged across. Hindley beat Bardet and Carapaz in the six-man sprint, while Juan Pedro López, distanced after unclipping from his pedal, kept the maglia rosa by 12 seconds over Almeida; Diego Rosa took the maglia azzurra.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 12

Oldani takes his first professional win in Genoa

On the race's longest stage, 204 km to Genoa, which returned to the Passo del Bocco for the first time since Wouter Weylandt's fatal crash on its descent in 2011, a 25-man breakaway was allowed to fight for the win. Stefano Oldani, Lorenzo Rota and Gijs Leemreize went clear and Oldani beat Rota in the sprint for his first professional victory; Juan Pedro López stayed in pink, while Caleb Ewan had left the race before the start.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 13

Démare takes his third win in Cuneo as Bardet abandons

Romain Bardet, fourth overall, abandoned with stomach problems before the Colle di Nava. The five-man break was only caught inside the final kilometre, and Arnaud Démare held off Phil Bauhaus and Mark Cavendish for his third stage win; Juan Pedro López extended his run in pink to ten days.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 18

De Bondt wins from the breakaway in Treviso

João Almeida, fourth overall, withdrew before the start after testing positive for COVID-19, and Juan Pedro López took back the maglia bianca. The four-man break of Magnus Cort, Edoardo Affini, Davide Gabburo and Dries De Bondt held off the sprinters, and De Bondt beat Affini by half a wheel for his first Grand Tour stage win; Carapaz stayed in pink.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 21

Sobrero wins in Verona, Hindley seals the Giro

Italian time trial champion Matteo Sobrero won the 17.4 km time trial in 22:24, 23 seconds ahead of Thymen Arensman and 40 ahead of Mathieu van der Poel, for his first Grand Tour stage win. Jai Hindley finished 15th to win the Giro by 1:18 over Carapaz, with Landa third; Hugh Carthy moved up to ninth, but Juan Pedro López kept the maglia bianca.
