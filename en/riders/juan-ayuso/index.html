In the headlines

Giro 2025 · Stage 16

Pre-race favourites Roglič and Ayuso both abandon

The two pre-race favourites did not reach Rome. Primož Roglič, the 2023 winner, lost time after crashing and puncturing on the gravel to Siena, was caught up in the crash on stage 14 and abandoned on stage 16. Juan Ayuso, winner at Tagliacozzo and second overall for much of the race, was dropped on stage 16 and withdrew on stage 18 after a bee sting.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 7

Ayuso wins at Tagliacozzo, Roglič back in pink

On the first big mountain stage Pedersen was dropped early on the final climb to Tagliacozzo. Juan Ayuso went clear 500 m from the finish and won by 4 seconds from his teammate Isaac del Toro, Egan Bernal and Primož Roglič. Roglič took back the maglia rosa, 4 seconds ahead of Ayuso, who also took the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 18

Denz solos to Cesano Maderno as Ayuso abandons

Juan Ayuso abandoned early in the stage, while a 34-rider breakaway gained more than ten minutes. Nico Denz attacked from a leading group of eleven 18 km from the finish and won alone in Cesano Maderno, 1:01 ahead of Mirco Maestri and Edward Planckaert. The maglia rosa group finished 13:51 down, and the top of the standings was unchanged.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 6

Ayuso loses seven minutes, then wins two stages

Juan Ayuso, one of UAE's two leaders, lost seven minutes on the final climb to Pal on stage 6 and dropped out of general classification contention. A day later he won from the break at Cerler (stage 7), and on stage 12 he beat Javier Romo in a two-up sprint.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 12

Ayuso beats Romo in a two-up sprint at Los Corrales de Buelna

The stage was tailor-made for a breakaway, with a 53-man group contesting the win. On the final climb, the Collada de Brenes, Juan Ayuso and Javier Romo gapped the rest, and Ayuso outsprinted Romo for his second stage of the race.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 10

Pogačar strikes again on Bastille Day

Richard Carapaz attacked on the Pas de Peyrol, but on the Col du Pertus Pogačar attacked about 15 km from the finish, passed Carapaz before the summit and rode alone to Le Lioran for his third stage win. His lead over Vingegaard grew to 3:36, and Evenepoel's sprint for the line, 32 seconds down, lifted him to third overall. Del Toro struggled on the Pertus, dropped from third to seventh and lost the white jersey to Juan Ayuso.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 14

Pogačar attacks on the Col du Haag for a fourth win

Pogačar attacked 1.5 km from the top of the Col du Haag, the last climb, and extended his lead on the plateau to the finish at Le Markstein for his fourth stage win. Del Toro and Seixas finished 38 seconds behind and Vingegaard, who had worked with Seixas to limit the losses, 44 seconds back, leaving Pogačar 4:30 ahead overall. Seixas took the white jersey from Ayuso and moved up to fourth.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 9

Del Toro leads for eleven stages and takes the maglia bianca

Isaac del Toro (UAE Team Emirates XRG), Juan Ayuso's teammate, took the maglia rosa on the gravel stage to Siena and led from stage 9 to stage 19, winning stage 17 in Bormio. He finished second overall and won the young rider classification, 5:32 ahead of Giulio Pellizzari.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 10

Hoole wins the Pisa time trial, del Toro defends pink

Rain arrived during the 28.6 km time trial from Lucca to Pisa and favoured the early starters. Daan Hoole won by 7 seconds from Joshua Tarling and 10 from Ethan Hayter. Del Toro kept the maglia rosa, but his lead over Ayuso shrank to 25 seconds.
