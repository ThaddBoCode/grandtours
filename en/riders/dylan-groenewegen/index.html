In the headlines

Tour 2024 · Stage 6

Groenewegen wins by a tyre's width in windy Dijon

Echelons in the crosswind split the peloton for a while, catching out Carapaz, Ayuso and Philipsen among others, but the groups came back together. In Dijon Dylan Groenewegen won the sprint by a tyre's width; Jasper Philipsen crossed the line second but was relegated for impeding Wout van Aert, leaving second place to Biniam Girmay. Pogačar kept yellow.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 3

Groenewegen pips van Aert, second for the third day in a row

Magnus Cort spent most of the day alone in the breakaway and again led over all three climbs. Van Aert opened the sprint in Sønderborg, but Dylan Groenewegen came past him on the line for his first WorldTour win in over two years, with Jasper Philipsen third. Van Aert kept yellow, now 7 seconds ahead of Lampaert, before the transfer to France.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 3

Magnier again: sprint victory in Sofia

The last Bulgarian stage crossed the Borovets Pass, its only climb, on the way to the capital. Paul Magnier won his second sprint ahead of Jonathan Milan and Dylan Groenewegen, strengthening his hold on the maglia ciclamino, while Guillermo Thomas Silva stayed in pink. Adam Yates and Andrea Vendrame did not start after the previous day's crash.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 11

Philipsen's fourth stage win in Moulins

Daniel Oss was the last survivor of a three-man breakaway but was caught in the finale. Without a teammate to lead him out, Jasper Philipsen won the bunch sprint in Moulins by a length from Dylan Groenewegen – his fourth stage win of the Tour. The overall standings were unchanged.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 21

Philipsen wins on the Champs-Élysées, Vingegaard crowned in Paris

Jasper Philipsen won the sprint on the Champs-Élysées ahead of Dylan Groenewegen and Alexander Kristoff for his second stage win of the Tour. Vingegaard crossed the line with his teammates, losing 51 seconds, and won the Tour by 2:43 over Pogačar, who took the white jersey for the third time, equalling Jan Ullrich and Andy Schleck. Van Aert won the green jersey, Vingegaard the polka-dot jersey and Ineos Grenadiers the team classification.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 10

Sagan wins in Foligno and takes the maglia ciclamino

Bora–Hansgrohe set such a hard pace on the Valico della Somma that Tim Merlier, Giacomo Nizzolo and Dylan Groenewegen were dropped. Peter Sagan launched his sprint from a long way out, held off Fernando Gaviria and Davide Cimolai and took the maglia ciclamino – his first Giro stage win had also come on stage 10, a year earlier.
