In the headlines

Vuelta 2024 · Stage 1

Grand Départ in Lisbon: McNulty wins the opening time trial

The Vuelta started in Portugal: the first three stages ran from Lisbon to Oeiras, Cascais to Ourém and Lousã to Castelo Branco before the race moved on to Spain. Brandon McNulty won the 12 km time trial in 12:35, two seconds ahead of Mathias Vacek, and became the first leader of the red jersey. The race ended in Madrid with another time trial.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 15

McNulty wins the Lombardia-style stage to Bergamo

Brandon McNulty won from the breakaway in Bergamo, beating Ben Healy and Marco Frigo in a three-man sprint. Bruno Armirail kept the maglia rosa, now 1:08 ahead of Geraint Thomas.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 18

Carapaz solos to victory at Orcières-Merlette

Pedersen joined a large breakaway and took maximum points at the intermediate sprint, extending his lead over Philipsen to 32 points. Valentin Paret-Peintre chased mountain points over the climbs, but Carapaz was strongest on the final ascent, going clear 3.5 km from the line to win ahead of Mauro Schmid and Matteo Jorgenson. The favourites finished together, while illness in the UAE team forced Pogačar's teammate Brandon McNulty out of the race.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 2

Groves wins the bunch sprint, Van Aert takes red through bonus seconds

Kaden Groves won the bunch sprint in Ourém, just ahead of Wout van Aert, who had come up from behind on the line. Thanks to six bonus seconds for second place, Van Aert took the red jersey, 3 seconds ahead of McNulty. Groves became the leader of the points classification.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 3

Van Aert wins the sprint in Castelo Branco and defends red

Wout van Aert, in the red jersey, won the sprint in Castelo Branco, launching it 200 metres from the line and holding off Kaden Groves. It was his first stage win of this Vuelta. He now led the general classification by 13 seconds over McNulty and also took the lead in the points classification.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 17

Pogačar beats Vingegaard again, this time at Peyragudes

Without Rafał Majka, who did not start after being injured near the end of stage 16, UAE Team Emirates relied on Brandon McNulty, who paced Pogačar and Vingegaard clear of all the other contenders. On the steep final ramp Pogačar came past Vingegaard to win his third stage, with McNulty third at 32 seconds. Bonus seconds cut Vingegaard's lead to 2:18, with Geraint Thomas third at 4:56.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 10

Sagan finally wins a Giro stage

Before the start in Lanciano, Mitchelton–Scott and Team Jumbo–Visma pulled out after positive COVID-19 tests, and Michael Matthews was also gone. Peter Sagan, part of the break, attacked on the steepest part of the climb at Tortoreto and won solo for his first Giro stage, ahead of Brandon McNulty and João Almeida, who gained four bonus seconds.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 14

Ganna wins the time trial to Valdobbiadene

Filippo Ganna won the 34.1 km time trial from Conegliano to Valdobbiadene, 26 seconds ahead of INEOS teammate Rohan Dennis and more than a minute ahead of everyone else. João Almeida extended his lead over Wilco Kelderman to 56 seconds, and Brandon McNulty, third on the stage, moved up to fourth overall.
