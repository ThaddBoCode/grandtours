In the headlines

Vuelta 2026 · Stage 7

Leknessund wins solo from the breakaway at Valdelinares

Andreas Leknessund, in the breakaway from kilometre zero, won solo at the Aramón Valdelinares summit in 3:49:47, 0:34 ahead of Tobias Halland Johannessen. It was the first stage win of this Vuelta for a breakaway rider. Pogačar kept the red jersey and Mas stayed second overall at 3:50.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 4

Paret-Peintre wins at Lago Laceno, Leknessund takes pink

Aurélien Paret-Peintre won at Lago Laceno two seconds ahead of Andreas Leknessund, with Toms Skujiņš third at 57 seconds. Leknessund took the maglia rosa, 28 seconds ahead of Remco Evenepoel, and also led the young rider classification.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 8

Narváez solos to victory in Fermo

On the hilly transition stage from Chieti to Fermo, Jhonatan Narváez (UAE Team Emirates XRG) took his second stage win, finishing solo 32 seconds ahead of Andreas Leknessund and 42 ahead of Martin Tjøtta. Nothing changed at the top of the overall standings, with Afonso Eulálio staying in pink ahead of Jonas Vingegaard.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 13

Bettiol solos to victory in Verbania

The peloton let the breakaway go, and Alberto Bettiol (XDS Astana) dropped his companions on the final climb. He won in Verbania 26 seconds ahead of Andreas Leknessund and 44 ahead of Jasper Stuyven, with no change in the overall standings.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 17

Valgren outfoxes his breakaway companions in Andalo

Michael Valgren (EF Education–EasyPost) caught out his breakaway companions in the final kilometre and won in Andalo 3 seconds ahead of Andreas Leknessund and 6 ahead of Damiano Caruso. Jhonatan Narváez was stuck in a chasing group but regained the maglia ciclamino thanks to the intermediate sprint; nothing changed at the top of the overall standings.

 Vuelta 2026

Breakaways decide five of the hardest stages

Andreas Leknessund (stage 7), Jakob Omrzel (12), Marco Brenner (14), Eddie Dunbar (19) and Mikel Landa (20) all won tough stages from the breakaway. Landa won the queen stage solo at the Collado del Alguacil.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 9

Evenepoel leaves the race in pink after a positive COVID-19 test

Remco Evenepoel, one of the pre-race favourites, won the opening time trial in Ortona and the stage 9 time trial in Cesena, where he beat Thomas by one second to take the maglia rosa back from Andreas Leknessund. Shortly afterwards he tested positive for COVID-19 and withdrew; after the rest day Thomas inherited pink, and by stage 11 four more Soudal–Quick-Step riders had tested positive.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 5

Groves wins a crash-marred stage in Salerno

On a stage marred by crashes, Kaden Groves won the sprint in Salerno ahead of Jonathan Milan and Mads Pedersen. Andreas Leknessund kept the maglia rosa, with Evenepoel still 28 seconds behind.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 6

Pedersen wins in Naples as the break is caught at the last gasp

On the stage starting and finishing in Naples the breakaway was caught at the last gasp, and Mads Pedersen won the sprint ahead of Jonathan Milan and Pascal Ackermann. Leknessund stayed in pink.
