In the headlines

Tour 2022 · Stage 2

Jakobsen wins in Nyborg, van Aert takes yellow on bonus seconds

After the crossing of the Great Belt Bridge, where race leader Lampaert was caught up in a crash, Fabio Jakobsen won a reduced bunch sprint ahead of van Aert and Mads Pedersen for his first Tour stage win. Van Aert took the yellow and green jerseys thanks to six bonus seconds, while Magnus Cort led over all three climbs to take the polka-dot jersey.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 16

Jakobsen wins three sprints and the green jersey

Fabio Jakobsen won stages 4, 8 and 16 and the points classification with 250 points, ahead of Roglič (199) and Magnus Cort (161). Jasper Philipsen had won stages 2 and 5 but withdrew before stage 11 with a mild fever.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 2

Philipsen beats Jakobsen in the Burgos sprint

Jasper Philipsen came around Juan Sebastián Molano to win the sprint ahead of Fabio Jakobsen and Michael Matthews, and took the green jersey. The breakaway of Diego Rubio was caught 20 km out, and Roglič kept the red jersey.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 4

Jakobsen wins and takes green; Taaramäe survives crash

Fabio Jakobsen outsprinted Arnaud Démare for the win and took the green jersey from Philipsen. Red jersey Taaramäe crashed 2.2 km from the line but, inside the final 3 km, was credited with the peloton's time and kept the lead.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 8

Jakobsen wins again and takes back green

Despite Astana pushing the pace for echelons 38 km out, which briefly split the bunch into three groups, the stage ended in a mass sprint: Fabio Jakobsen won ahead of Alberto Dainese and Philipsen. He took the green jersey back from Philipsen, while Roglič kept the red jersey.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 4

Philipsen doubles up on the Nogaro motor circuit

On a slow stage with no breakaway until around 100 km in, Philipsen won at the Circuit Paul Armagnac by a quarter of a wheel from Caleb Ewan. Crashes in the finale brought down Fabio Jakobsen among others, and Mathieu van der Poel was relegated for impeding Biniam Girmay. Philipsen took the green jersey from Victor Lafay.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 12

Ion Izagirre solos to Belleville-en-Beaujolais

Fabio Jakobsen did not start and David de la Cruz crashed out. After a fierce fight for the breakaway, Ion Izagirre went clear on the Col de la Croix Rosier and rode the last 30 km or so alone, winning by 58 seconds from Mathieu Burgaudeau and Matteo Jorgenson. It was Cofidis' second stage win of the Tour after Lafay's.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 5

Philipsen wins in Albacete; Elissonde takes red

Jasper Philipsen won the sprint ahead of Jakobsen and Alberto Dainese, after a big crash 11 km from the finish had caught Romain Bardet and Taaramäe among others. Kenny Elissonde took the red jersey, five seconds ahead of Roglič.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 13

Sénéchal wins his first Grand Tour stage

Deceuninck–Quick-Step set such a fast pace in the technical finale that sprinter Jakobsen was dropped, so lead-out man Florian Sénéchal sprinted himself and held off Matteo Trentin for his first Grand Tour stage win. Eiking stayed in the red jersey.
