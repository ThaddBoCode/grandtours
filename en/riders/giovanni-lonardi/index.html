In the headlines

Giro 2026 · Stage 21

Milan sprints to victory in Rome as Vingegaard celebrates

Jonathan Milan (Lidl–Trek) won the bunch sprint on the final stage in Rome ahead of Giovanni Lonardi and Paul Penhoët. Jonas Vingegaard won the Giro by 5:22 over Felix Gall and 6:25 over Jai Hindley, with Paul Magnier taking the maglia ciclamino, Giulio Ciccone the maglia azzurra and Afonso Eulálio the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 11

Milan wins in Francavilla as Merlier is relegated

The three-man breakaway was caught 35 km from the finish, and in the sprint in Francavilla al Mare Jonathan Milan came past Tim Merlier, who was relegated for deviating from his line. That left Kaden Groves second and Giovanni Lonardi third. Cian Uijtdebroeks did not start, and Antonio Tiberi took the young rider lead.
