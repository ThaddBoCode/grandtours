In the headlines

Giro 2024 · Stage 12

Alaphilippe wins in Fano after a long-range attack

Julian Alaphilippe attacked with Mirco Maestri more than 125 km from the finish, dropped him on the last climb and won in Fano – his first Grand Tour stage win since the 2021 Tour de France. It made him a stage winner in all three Grand Tours, and he also took the overall combativity award.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 6

Sánchez outsprints Alaphilippe after a gravel attack

From a seven-man breakaway, Luke Plapp attacked on the Bagnaia gravel sector 43 km from the finish, and only Pelayo Sánchez and Julian Alaphilippe could follow. The trio held off the peloton, and Sánchez won the sprint in Rapolano Terme ahead of Alaphilippe and Plapp.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 11

World champion Alaphilippe abandons with a dislocated shoulder

Julian Alaphilippe slid down in the peloton about 64 km from the finish of stage 11 and could not continue; it was later revealed that he had a dislocated shoulder. The stage ended in a bunch sprint won by Kaden Groves.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 1

Alaphilippe attacks into yellow after the mass crash

45 km from the finish a spectator's sign brought down Tony Martin and most of the peloton with him, and a second mass crash 7.5 km out ended the race for Cyril Lemoine and Ignatas Konovalovas. World champion Julian Alaphilippe attacked 2.3 km from the line on the Côte de la Fosse aux Loups and won in Landerneau, 8 seconds ahead of Michael Matthews and Primož Roglič. He took the yellow and green jerseys, Ide Schelling the polka-dot jersey.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 5

Alaphilippe loses yellow to a feeding penalty

Julian Alaphilippe won stage 2 in Nice to take the yellow jersey for the second time in his career, but after stage 5 he was given a 20-second penalty for taking an illegal feed inside the last 20 km. Adam Yates inherited the lead, saying nobody wanted to take the jersey like that, and held it for four stages until Laruns.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 2

Alaphilippe attacks, wins in Nice and takes yellow

On the last climb, the Col des Quatre Chemins, Julian Alaphilippe attacked and only Marc Hirschi and Adam Yates could join him. The trio stayed two seconds clear of the peloton and Alaphilippe beat Hirschi in the sprint to take the yellow jersey, 4 seconds ahead of Yates; Hirschi took white. Alexander Kristoff was dropped early and lost more than 28 minutes.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 9

Kooij wins in Naples as Narváez is caught near the line

In the finale Jhonatan Narváez attacked from the peloton and passed the earlier attacker Julian Alaphilippe, but was swept up by the sprinters a few metres from the line. Olav Kooij won ahead of Jonathan Milan and Juan Sebastián Molano, for whom race leader Pogačar had led the peloton in the final kilometre.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 16

Shortened stage: Pogačar takes win number five on Monte Pana

Because of the weather the Umbrail Pass was removed and, after a chaotic start, the stage was cut to 118.7 km starting in Laas. Julian Alaphilippe went solo 33 km from the finish but was caught on the final climb; Tadej Pogačar attacked, passed Giulio Pellizzari 800 m from the line and won by 16 seconds over Pellizzari and Daniel Martínez. Martínez moved back into second overall ahead of Geraint Thomas.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 2

Van der Poel takes yellow on the Mûr-de-Bretagne

Mathieu van der Poel attacked on the first ascent of the Mûr-de-Bretagne to collect the 8 bonus seconds, then went clear again on the second and won 6 seconds ahead of Tadej Pogačar, Primož Roglič and Wilco Kelderman. That earned him the yellow jersey, 8 seconds ahead of Julian Alaphilippe, as well as the polka-dot jersey. Geraint Thomas lost 17 seconds to the two Slovenians.
