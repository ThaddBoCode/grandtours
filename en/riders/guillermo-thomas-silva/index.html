In the headlines

Giro 2026 · Stage 2

Thomas Silva: first Uruguayan to win a stage and wear pink

Guillermo Thomas Silva (XDS Astana) won the reduced sprint at Veliko Tarnovo ahead of Florian Stork and Giulio Ciccone, after Vingegaard had countered an attack by Christian Scaroni on the final climb. He became the first Uruguayan to win a Giro stage and wear the maglia rosa, which he kept for two days.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 3

Magnier again: sprint victory in Sofia

The last Bulgarian stage crossed the Borovets Pass, its only climb, on the way to the capital. Paul Magnier won his second sprint ahead of Jonathan Milan and Dylan Groenewegen, strengthening his hold on the maglia ciclamino, while Guillermo Thomas Silva stayed in pink. Adam Yates and Andrea Vendrame did not start after the previous day's crash.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 12

Segaert surprises the bunch in Novi Ligure

Movistar again tried to shake off the fastest sprinters, and those who remained lacked teammates in the finale. Alec Segaert (Team Bahrain Victorious) took advantage, attacking 3 km from the line and holding on to win by 3 seconds from Toon Aerts and Guillermo Thomas Silva. Afonso Eulálio stayed in pink, 33 seconds ahead of Jonas Vingegaard.
