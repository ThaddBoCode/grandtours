In the headlines

Tour 2024 · Stage 18

Campenaerts outsprints his breakaway rivals in Barcelonnette

A breakaway of more than 30 riders went clear early, and on the last climb Michał Kwiatkowski attacked, later joined by Mattéo Vercher and Victor Campenaerts. Campenaerts won the three-man sprint in Barcelonnette ahead of Vercher and Kwiatkowski for his first Tour stage win. The yellow jersey group finished more than 13 minutes down, with no change in the overall standings.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 15

Campenaerts wins in Gorizia after a mass crash

A mass crash soon after the start forced the race to be neutralised, and Emanuel Buchmann, sixth overall, Natnael Berhane and Jos van Emden abandoned. On a route that also crossed into Slovenia, Victor Campenaerts outsprinted fellow escapee Oscar Riesebeek for his first Grand Tour stage win, with the peloton more than 17 minutes down.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 15

Wellens solos to Carcassonne for his first Tour stage

A crash early in the stage briefly caught out Jonas Vingegaard and Florian Lipowitz before the peloton regrouped. Tim Wellens attacked from the lead group 43.5 km from the finish and won alone in Carcassonne, 1:28 ahead of Victor Campenaerts, completing stage wins in all three Grand Tours. The peloton finished more than six minutes down, with no change at the top of the overall standings.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 18

Asgreen's breakaway beats the sprinters in Bourg-en-Bresse

Wout van Aert did not start, leaving the race to be with his wife, who was about to give birth. Kasper Asgreen, Jonas Abrahamsen and Victor Campenaerts, later joined by Pascal Eenkhoorn, were out front all day with barely more than a minute's lead. Three of them held off the charging bunch by a whisker, Asgreen winning ahead of Eenkhoorn and Abrahamsen, with Philipsen fourth.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 19

Černý wins in Asti after a protest shortens the stage

After riders protested against the distance in bad weather, the stage was halted after about 10 km and restarted in Abbiategrasso, cutting it to 124.5 km. Josef Černý attacked from a 14-rider break 20 km from the finish and won alone in Asti, 18 seconds ahead of Victor Campenaerts; the peloton finished about 16 minutes down.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 21

Ganna wins in Milan, Geoghegan Hart wins the Giro

With Jai Hindley and Tao Geoghegan Hart level on time, the 15.7 km time trial into Milan decided the Giro. Filippo Ganna won his fourth stage ahead of Victor Campenaerts and Rohan Dennis, while Geoghegan Hart beat Hindley by 39 seconds to take the overall win and João Almeida moved past Pello Bilbao into fourth.
