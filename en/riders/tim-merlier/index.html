In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 7

Merlier pounces in Bordeaux

Veistroffer was in the breakaway again, this time with Jakub Otruba, but the pair were never given more than two minutes. Alpecin–Premier Tech's lead-out for Jasper Philipsen ran out too early and Tim Merlier took advantage to win ahead of Søren Wærenskjold and Biniam Girmay. The classifications were unchanged, with Pedersen leading the points by around 60.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 8

Merlier makes it two in a row in Bergerac

Liam Slock, the last survivor of a three-man breakaway, held off the peloton until around the final kilometre. Merlier, still several dozen metres back with 500 m to go, had the strongest acceleration and beat Girmay, Kooij and Philipsen for his second consecutive stage win. He moved up to second in the points classification, 15 points behind Pedersen.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 12

Merlier's third win as a crash strikes behind him

A breakaway started by Veistroffer was caught 33 km from the finish, and Lidl–Trek tried in vain to prevent a bunch sprint. Tim Merlier beat Kooij and Philipsen for his third stage win of the race, while a heavy crash about 400 metres from the line brought down several riders, including Fernando Gaviria and Dorian Godon, but none of the favourites. Pedersen kept the green jersey and the GC was unchanged.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 3

Philipsen crashes out, Merlier edges Milan in Dunkirk

Green jersey Jasper Philipsen crashed heavily in the intermediate sprint and abandoned with a broken collarbone and broken ribs. After several crashes in the final 5 km, Tim Merlier beat Jonathan Milan in a photo finish, and Milan took over the green jersey. For lack of attackers, no combativity award was given.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 9

Merlier wins after van der Poel's all-day raid with Rickaert

Mathieu van der Poel and his teammate Jonas Rickaert attacked from the start and led for almost the whole day; van der Poel went solo 6 km from the finish but was caught inside the final kilometre. Tim Merlier then beat Jonathan Milan for his second stage win. At 50.013 km/h it was the second-fastest stage in Tour history, and João Almeida abandoned with injuries from his stage 7 crash.

 Giro 2024

Milan takes the maglia ciclamino, Merlier wins in Rome

Jonathan Milan won the sprints in Andora, Francavilla al Mare and Cento, led the points classification from stage 4 onwards and carried the maglia ciclamino to Rome. Tim Merlier also finished with three wins – in Fossano, Padua and on the final stage in Rome, each time ahead of Milan.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 3

Pogačar and Thomas caught at 300 m, Merlier wins in Fossano

Crosswinds split the peloton 58 km from the finish before it came back together. On the last rise just over 3 km from Fossano, race leader Tadej Pogačar and Geraint Thomas went clear but were caught 300 m from the line. Tim Merlier won the sprint ahead of Jonathan Milan and Biniam Girmay and took the lead of the points classification.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 11

Milan wins in Francavilla as Merlier is relegated

The three-man breakaway was caught 35 km from the finish, and in the sprint in Francavilla al Mare Jonathan Milan came past Tim Merlier, who was relegated for deviating from his line. That left Kaden Groves second and Giovanni Lonardi third. Cian Uijtdebroeks did not start, and Antonio Tiberi took the young rider lead.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 18

Merlier takes his second sprint win in Padua

Edoardo Affini bridged alone to the four-man breakaway 55 km from the finish, but the move ended 10 km from Padua. Jonathan Milan lost position in the hectic run-in, and Tim Merlier won his second sprint ahead of Milan and Kaden Groves.
