In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 5

Kooij beats the favourites in Pau

Baptiste Veistroffer spent most of the day alone at the front before being caught about 14 km from the finish. After a crash 6 km from the line, a group of around 20 riders contested the sprint, and Olav Kooij beat Max Kanter and Tim Merlier for his first Tour stage win. Træen kept the yellow jersey, while Pedersen extended his points lead by finishing seventh.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 12

Kooij takes the bunch sprint in Viadana

Italians Giosuè Epis, Andrea Pietrobon and Manuele Tarozzi formed the early break, with Pietrobon the last to be caught 29 km from the finish. Olav Kooij won the bunch sprint ahead of Casper van Uden and Ben Turner, and del Toro kept the maglia rosa.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 9

Kooij wins in Naples as Narváez is caught near the line

In the finale Jhonatan Narváez attacked from the peloton and passed the earlier attacker Julian Alaphilippe, but was swept up by the sprinters a few metres from the line. Olav Kooij won ahead of Jonathan Milan and Juan Sebastián Molano, for whom race leader Pogačar had led the peloton in the final kilometre.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 8

Merlier makes it two in a row in Bergerac

Liam Slock, the last survivor of a three-man breakaway, held off the peloton until around the final kilometre. Merlier, still several dozen metres back with 500 m to go, had the strongest acceleration and beat Girmay, Kooij and Philipsen for his second consecutive stage win. He moved up to second in the points classification, 15 points behind Pedersen.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 11

Wærenskjold wins the fastest road stage in Tour history

A four-man breakaway including Mathis Le Berre was kept on a short leash and caught 6 km from the finish. In a disorganised sprint, Søren Wærenskjold jumped across to Kooij's lead-out man Cees Bol and won ahead of Kooij and Philipsen – his first Grand Tour stage win. Raced at an average of 50.9 km/h, it was the fastest road stage in Tour history.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 12

Merlier's third win as a crash strikes behind him

A breakaway started by Veistroffer was caught 33 km from the finish, and Lidl–Trek tried in vain to prevent a bunch sprint. Tim Merlier beat Kooij and Philipsen for his third stage win of the race, while a heavy crash about 400 metres from the line brought down several riders, including Fernando Gaviria and Dorian Godon, but none of the favourites. Pedersen kept the green jersey and the GC was unchanged.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 17

Philipsen wins in Voiron as the green battle tightens

Pedersen bridged to a large breakaway and won the intermediate sprint, after which a series of attacks followed. In the sprint of a reduced group, Mathieu van der Poel led out Jasper Philipsen, who won ahead of Mauro Schmid and Olav Kooij. Pedersen finished only eighth and his lead in the points classification shrank to seven points.

 Giro 2025

Pedersen wins four stages and the maglia ciclamino

Mads Pedersen won stages 1, 3, 5 and 13, led the general classification after stage 1 and after stages 3 to 6, and won the points classification with 295 points ahead of Olav Kooij (185) and Wout van Aert (127).

 Giro 2025 · Stage 21

Final stage starts in the Vatican Gardens

On 29 April it was announced that the final stage would start in the Vatican Gardens to celebrate the 2025 Jubilee and pay homage to Pope Francis, who died on 21 April. Pope Leo XIV welcomed the riders at the start; in Rome, Olav Kooij won the sprint.
