In the headlines

Vuelta 2023 · Stage 12

Molano wins the sprint in Zaragoza ahead of Groves

Abel Balderstone and Jetse Bol made up the break and were caught 44 km from the finish, after which Bol went clear again. In the sprint in Zaragoza Juan Sebastián Molano, led out by Rui Oliveira, won ahead of green jersey Kaden Groves and Boy van Poppel. The overall standings did not change, with Kuss in red ahead of Marc Soler.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 21

Molano wins in Madrid, Evenepoel wins the Vuelta

Juan Sebastián Molano won the bunch sprint in Madrid ahead of green jersey Mads Pedersen and Pascal Ackermann. Remco Evenepoel completed the Vuelta in 80:26:59, 2:02 ahead of Enric Mas and 4:57 ahead of Juan Ayuso; Pedersen won the points classification, Richard Carapaz the mountains and UAE Team Emirates the team classification.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 9

Kooij wins in Naples as Narváez is caught near the line

In the finale Jhonatan Narváez attacked from the peloton and passed the earlier attacker Julian Alaphilippe, but was swept up by the sprinters a few metres from the line. Olav Kooij won ahead of Jonathan Milan and Juan Sebastián Molano, for whom race leader Pogačar had led the peloton in the final kilometre.

 Vuelta 2023 · Stage 21

Groves takes green with stage wins 4, 5 and 21

Kaden Groves won the sprints in Tarragona and Burriana and finished the Vuelta with the win in Madrid. He wore the green jersey from stage 4 to the end and won the points classification with 315 points, 79 ahead of Remco Evenepoel. The other sprint wins went to Geoffrey Soupe (stage 7), Juan Sebastián Molano (12) and Alberto Dainese (19).

 Vuelta 2023 · Stage 4

Groves wins the sprint in Tarragona

Eduardo Sepúlveda, Ander Okamika and David González formed the break and were caught about 20 km from the finish. After a crash 4 km out, Kaden Groves won the sprint ahead of Juan Sebastián Molano and Edward Theuns. Groves took the green jersey and Sepúlveda the polka-dot jersey, while Remco Evenepoel stayed in red.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 2

Philipsen beats Jakobsen in the Burgos sprint

Jasper Philipsen came around Juan Sebastián Molano to win the sprint ahead of Fabio Jakobsen and Michael Matthews, and took the green jersey. The breakaway of Diego Rubio was caught 20 km out, and Roglič kept the red jersey.
