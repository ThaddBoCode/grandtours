In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 20

Debutants del Toro and Seixas fight for the podium

Two Tour debutants fought over third place: Isaac del Toro finished third overall and won the white jersey, with Pogačar riding for him on stage 20. Paul Seixas, 19 and riding his first Grand Tour, finished fourth – with Lenny Martinez fifth, two French riders made the top five for the first time since 2014.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 2

Del Toro wins on Montjuïc with Pogačar's help

UAE Team Emirates XRG whittled down the peloton with a fast pace on three laps of the Montjuïc circuit, and in the uphill sprint Tadej Pogačar made sure his teammate Isaac del Toro won – the 22-year-old's first Tour stage victory. Remco Evenepoel was third ahead of Vingegaard, who kept yellow but saw his lead over Pogačar cut to 6 seconds. Del Toro also took the green jersey and kept the white one.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 20

Simon Yates wins the Giro on the Colle delle Finestre

Simon Yates (Visma–Lease a Bike) won his first Giro d'Italia in 82h 31' 01", taking the maglia rosa only on the penultimate stage. He finished 3:56 ahead of Isaac del Toro and 4:43 ahead of former Giro winner Richard Carapaz – without winning a stage himself.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 9

Del Toro leads for eleven stages and takes the maglia bianca

Isaac del Toro (UAE Team Emirates XRG), Juan Ayuso's teammate, took the maglia rosa on the gravel stage to Siena and led from stage 9 to stage 19, winning stage 17 in Bormio. He finished second overall and won the young rider classification, 5:32 ahead of Giulio Pellizzari.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 7

Ayuso wins at Tagliacozzo, Roglič back in pink

On the first big mountain stage Pedersen was dropped early on the final climb to Tagliacozzo. Juan Ayuso went clear 500 m from the finish and won by 4 seconds from his teammate Isaac del Toro, Egan Bernal and Primož Roglič. Roglič took back the maglia rosa, 4 seconds ahead of Ayuso, who also took the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 17

Del Toro rebounds to win in Bormio

After the Tonale and the Mortirolo, Richard Carapaz and maglia rosa Isaac del Toro caught Romain Bardet, the last of the break, before del Toro went clear alone to win in Bormio – his first Giro stage win. Bardet and Carapaz finished 4 seconds back; Carapaz moved up to second overall at 41 seconds, with Simon Yates third at 51 seconds.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 19

Prodhomme wins at Champoluc, del Toro answers Carapaz

Nicolas Prodhomme attacked from the break on the Col de Joux, 28 km from the finish, and won alone in Champoluc. Maglia rosa Isaac del Toro answered every attack from Richard Carapaz, and the pair finished 58 seconds behind the winner and 24 seconds ahead of the other favourites. Before the last mountain stage del Toro led Carapaz by 43 seconds and Simon Yates by 1:21.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 21

Kooij wins in Rome, Yates seals his Giro

The final stage started in the Vatican Gardens, where Pope Leo XIV welcomed the riders, and finished in Rome after eight laps of a local circuit. Olav Kooij won the sprint ahead of Kaden Groves and Matteo Moschetti, and his Visma–Lease a Bike teammate Simon Yates sealed overall victory, 3:56 ahead of Isaac del Toro.
