In the headlines

Vuelta 2026

Brennan wins five stages, more than anyone else

Matthew Brennan (Visma–Lease a Bike) won stages 2, 5, 11, 16 and 17, more than any other rider in this Vuelta. His teammate Wout van Aert helped lead out several of those sprints. Brennan finished second in the points classification with 273 points.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 2

Brennan sprints to victory in Manosque, Pogačar stays in red

Matthew Brennan won the sprint in Manosque in 4:47:47, after an attack by his Visma–Lease a Bike teammate Wout van Aert on the final climb. Pau Miquel was second and Pogačar third, keeping the red jersey. Brennan took the white jersey, and Van Aert moved up to second overall at 0:09 with Brennan third at 0:10.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 5

Brennan wins a second sprint in Roquetes

Matthew Brennan won the bunch sprint in Roquetes in 3:47:02 ahead of Jordi Meeus and Vincenzo Albanese, led out in the finale by Christophe Laporte and Wout van Aert. Pogačar kept the red jersey and the top of the general classification was unchanged (Roglič at 3:21).

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 11

Brennan wins his third stage in Lorca

Matthew Brennan won the sprint in Lorca in 3:17:18 ahead of Bryan Coquard and Jordi Meeus, led out by Wout van Aert. It was his third stage win of this Vuelta. The top of the general classification was unchanged: Mas led Roglič (1:18) and Gall (1:39).

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 16

Brennan takes his fourth stage in Palos de la Frontera

Matthew Brennan won the sprint in Palos de la Frontera in 3:56:33 ahead of Bryan Coquard and Vito Braet. It was his fourth stage win of this Vuelta. Mas stayed in the red jersey, with Gall second at 2:06.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 17

Brennan sprints to a fifth win in Seville

Matthew Brennan won the sprint in Seville in 4:07:15 ahead of Jordi Meeus and Magnus Cort. It was his fifth stage win, after stages 2, 5, 11 and 16, more than any other rider in this Vuelta. The general classification was unchanged, with Mas ahead of Gall and Roglič.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 15

Van Aert takes the green jersey, two stages and the combativity award

Wout van Aert won the points classification with 326 points, ahead of his teammate Matthew Brennan (273). He won stage 13 in Loja and stage 15 in Córdoba from the front group, and also took the overall combativity award.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 13

Van Aert wins from the breakaway in Loja

Wout van Aert, part of a long breakaway, attacked 3 km from the finish and beat Valentin Paret-Peintre in the sprint in Loja in 4:34:00. Matthew Brennan was third at 0:24. It was Van Aert's first stage win of this Vuelta, and Mas kept the red jersey.
