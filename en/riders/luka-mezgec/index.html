In the headlines

Tour 2020 · Stage 14

Kragh Andersen escapes in the finale in Lyon

Bora–Hansgrohe drove the pace on the Col du Béal to drop Sam Bennett and other sprinters. In a finale full of attacks, Søren Kragh Andersen went clear 3 km from the finish and won alone by 15 seconds; Luka Mezgec won the sprint for second ahead of Simone Consonni and Peter Sagan. Roglič kept yellow and Bennett the green jersey.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 19

Kragh Andersen doubles up in Champagnole

After Rémi Cavagna's early solo attack was neutralised, a 12-rider group including Sam Bennett, Peter Sagan and Matteo Trentin went clear in the finale. Søren Kragh Andersen attacked 16 km from the finish and won alone by 53 seconds ahead of Luka Mezgec and Jasper Stuyven – his second stage win of this Tour. The general classification remained unchanged before the time trial.
