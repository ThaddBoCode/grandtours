In the headlines

Giro 2025 · Stage 8

Plapp solos to Castelraimondo, Ulissi takes the maglia rosa

Luke Plapp won from the breakaway with a 46 km solo, finishing 38 seconds ahead of Wilco Kelderman and Diego Ulissi. Ulissi took the maglia rosa, 12 seconds ahead of Lorenzo Fortunato, while the group of favourites finished 4:50 behind the winner.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 6

Sánchez outsprints Alaphilippe after a gravel attack

From a seven-man breakaway, Luke Plapp attacked on the Bagnaia gravel sector 43 km from the finish, and only Pelayo Sánchez and Julian Alaphilippe could follow. The trio held off the peloton, and Sánchez won the sprint in Rapolano Terme ahead of Alaphilippe and Plapp.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 7

Pogačar overturns Ganna's lead on the climb to Perugia

Filippo Ganna was fastest at both checkpoints and still 47 seconds up on Tadej Pogačar at the second. On the closing uphill kilometres to Perugia, Pogačar overturned the deficit and won the 40.6 km time trial by 17 seconds. His lead grew to 2:36 over Daniel Martínez, and Luke Plapp took over the young rider lead.
