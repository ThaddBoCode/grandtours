In the headlines

Giro 2025 · Stage 8

Plapp solos to Castelraimondo, Ulissi takes the maglia rosa

Luke Plapp won from the breakaway with a 46 km solo, finishing 38 seconds ahead of Wilco Kelderman and Diego Ulissi. Ulissi took the maglia rosa, 12 seconds ahead of Lorenzo Fortunato, while the group of favourites finished 4:50 behind the winner.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 2

Ulissi wins the uphill finish in Agrigento

Diego Ulissi won a three-man sprint on the uphill finish in Agrigento, beating Peter Sagan and Mikkel Frølich Honoré, and moved into the maglia ciclamino. A five-man break including Thomas De Gendt was caught 9 km from the line; Filippo Ganna kept the maglia rosa and Sagan took the maglia azzurra.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 13

Ulissi strikes again in Monselice

Arnaud Démare and Peter Sagan were dropped on the day's two climbs, leaving about twenty riders to contest the finish in Monselice. Diego Ulissi won the sprint for his second stage victory, ahead of João Almeida, who gained six bonus seconds, and Patrick Konrad.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 11

Narváez makes it three in Chiavari

Jhonatan Narváez (UAE Team Emirates XRG) made the day's breakaway and beat Enric Mas in a two-up sprint in Chiavari for his third stage win, with Diego Ulissi and Chris Harper 11 seconds back. Stage 6 winner Davide Ballerini abandoned, while Afonso Eulálio stayed 27 seconds ahead of Jonas Vingegaard overall.
