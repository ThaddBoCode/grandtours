In the headlines

Giro 2020 · Stage 8

Yates out with COVID-19, Dowsett wins solo in Vieste

The day began with the news that Simon Yates had tested positive for COVID-19 and left the race. Alex Dowsett went clear from a large break and won alone in Vieste, 1:15 ahead of Salvatore Puccio, while the peloton finished about 14 minutes down and João Almeida stayed in pink.
