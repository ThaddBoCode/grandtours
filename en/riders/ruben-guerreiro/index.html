In the headlines

Giro 2020 · Stage 9

Guerreiro wins at Roccaraso and takes the maglia azzurra

Ruben Guerreiro won from the break on the Aremogna climb above Roccaraso, eight seconds ahead of Jonathan Castroviejo, and moved into the maglia azzurra. On the steep finish Jakob Fuglsang, Wilco Kelderman and Domenico Pozzovivo gained time, Kelderman moving up to second overall, 30 seconds behind João Almeida.

 Giro 2020

Démare's four sprint wins and the maglia ciclamino

Arnaud Démare won four bunch sprints – at Villafranca Tirrena, Matera, Brindisi and Rimini – and took the maglia ciclamino with 233 points to Peter Sagan's 184. Sagan, second to Démare in three of those sprints, finally won his first Giro stage in Tortoreto, while the maglia azzurra went to Ruben Guerreiro.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 16

Tratnik wins in San Daniele del Friuli

A 28-rider break went clear from the start, including mountains rivals Giovanni Visconti and Ruben Guerreiro. Jan Tratnik dropped Ben O'Connor on the climb to the finish and won by seven seconds, while João Almeida attacked in the last few hundred metres to gain two seconds on his rivals.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 17

O'Connor wins solo at Madonna di Campiglio

Once again the break decided the stage: Ben O'Connor, second the day before, won alone at Madonna di Campiglio ahead of Hermann Pernsteiner and Thomas De Gendt. The favourites finished together, so the overall standings did not change, and Ruben Guerreiro, in the break, regained the maglia azzurra.
