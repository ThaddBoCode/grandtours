In the headlines

Giro 2023 · Stage 3

Matthews claims the uphill sprint in Melfi

Michael Matthews won the uphill sprint in Melfi ahead of Mads Pedersen and Kaden Groves. Evenepoel stayed in pink, Primož Roglič moved up to third overall at 44 seconds, and Thibaut Pinot took the maglia azzurra.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 14

Matthews wins in Mende as Vingegaard stays on Pogačar's wheel

A 23-rider breakaway was allowed more than 14 minutes. Michael Matthews, briefly dropped by Alberto Bettiol on the steep Côte de la Croix Neuve, fought his way back, attacked near the top and won alone, 15 seconds ahead of Bettiol. Pogačar attacked on the same climb, but Vingegaard followed and the gap between them stayed at 2:22.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 17

Dainese wins the bunch sprint in Caorle

Alberto Dainese won the bunch sprint in Caorle ahead of Jonathan Milan and Michael Matthews. Geraint Thomas kept the maglia rosa, 18 seconds ahead of João Almeida.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 6

Pogačar wins the uphill sprint in Longwy and takes yellow

On the longest stage of the race, van Aert spent much of the day in the breakaway wearing yellow but was caught 11.1 km from the finish and dropped. Pogačar won the uphill sprint in Longwy ahead of Michael Matthews and David Gaudu and took the yellow jersey, 4 seconds ahead of Neilson Powless and 31 ahead of Vingegaard.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 8

Van Aert takes his second stage win in Lausanne

Before the start, Pogačar's teammate Vegard Stake Laengen left the race with COVID-19. Fred Wright, the last of the breakaway, was caught 3.5 km from the finish on the climb to Lausanne, where van Aert won the uphill sprint ahead of Michael Matthews and Pogačar. Pogačar's 4 bonus seconds increased his lead over Vingegaard to 39 seconds.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 1

Alaphilippe attacks into yellow after the mass crash

45 km from the finish a spectator's sign brought down Tony Martin and most of the peloton with him, and a second mass crash 7.5 km out ended the race for Cyril Lemoine and Ignatas Konovalovas. World champion Julian Alaphilippe attacked 2.3 km from the line on the Côte de la Fosse aux Loups and won in Landerneau, 8 seconds ahead of Michael Matthews and Primož Roglič. He took the yellow and green jerseys, Ide Schelling the polka-dot jersey.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 16

Konrad solos to Saint-Gaudens

Patrick Konrad bridged to the leaders on the Col de la Core and went clear alone on the Col de Portet-d'Aspet. He won in Saint-Gaudens 42 seconds ahead of Sonny Colbrelli and Michael Matthews, Bora–Hansgrohe's second stage win of the race. The yellow jersey group finished almost 14 minutes down and the top ten was unchanged.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 10

COVID-19 positives: Mitchelton–Scott and Jumbo–Visma pull out

Simon Yates tested positive for COVID-19 and did not start stage 8. Before stage 10, Mitchelton–Scott and Team Jumbo–Visma withdrew all their remaining riders following positive tests – among them Steven Kruijswijk, who had tested positive himself – and Michael Matthews also left the race.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 6

Démare again, this time in Matera

After the four-man break was caught 15 km from the finish, attacks by Vincenzo Nibali and Peter Sagan on the gentle rise to the line in Matera were shut down and Arnaud Démare sprinted to his second win, ahead of Michael Matthews and Fabio Felline. Démare took the maglia ciclamino, and João Almeida stayed in pink.
