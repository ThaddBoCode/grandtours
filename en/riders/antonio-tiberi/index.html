In the headlines

Giro 2024 · Stage 11

Uijtdebroeks pulls out, Tiberi wins the maglia bianca

Cian Uijtdebroeks, the leader of the young rider classification, did not start stage 11. Antonio Tiberi took over the lead, defended it all the way to Rome and finished fifth overall, 12:49 down.

 Giro 2027

Pogačar, Vingegaard, Seixas: who will ride?

Pogačar has named the Tour, the Worlds and the Vuelta as his 2027 goals, though he says he would rather ride the Giro; defending champion Vingegaard's programme is still open. Paul Seixas is considering a Giro debut, and Bahrain have Antonio Tiberi on their Giro programme for now. Several decisions are due at the teams' December training camps.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 14

Asgreen solos to Nova Gorica as a crash hits the favourites

Kasper Asgreen, Mirco Maestri and Martin Marcellusi led for much of a rainy stage before Asgreen went alone 6 km from the finish in Nova Gorica, Slovenia, and won by 16 seconds from Kaden Groves and Olav Kooij. A crash 22 km from the finish brought down or held up Juan Ayuso, Primož Roglič, Antonio Tiberi and Giulio Ciccone, who did not start the next day. Simon Yates moved up to second overall, 1:20 behind del Toro.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 20

Double Monte Grappa: sixth stage win for Pogačar

On the second ascent of Monte Grappa, Tadej Pogačar attacked 5 km from the summit and 36 km from the finish, caught breakaway rider Giulio Pellizzari and left him behind. After the long descent he won in Bassano del Grappa by 2:07 over Valentin Paret-Peintre, Daniel Martínez and Antonio Tiberi, taking a 9:56 lead into the final stage.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 21

Merlier wins the sprint in Rome, Pogačar wins the Giro

A four-man move was caught 13 km from the finish, and in the sprint near the Colosseum Tim Merlier took his third stage win ahead of Jonathan Milan and Kaden Groves. Tadej Pogačar won the Giro and the maglia azzurra, Milan the maglia ciclamino and Antonio Tiberi the maglia bianca.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 4

Roglič wins at Pico Villuercas and takes the red jersey

On the first summit finish of the Spanish stages Primož Roglič beat Lennert Van Eetvelt, who was given the same time in second, in the sprint; João Almeida was third. Roglič took the red jersey with an 8-second lead over Almeida and 32 over Enric Mas. Antonio Tiberi took the white jersey.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 7

Van Aert takes his second win in Córdoba

Wout van Aert won the sprint from a reduced group in Córdoba ahead of Mathias Vacek and Pau Miquel, all given the same time. Pavel Sivakov had attacked shortly before the red kite. Ben O'Connor stayed in the red jersey with Roglič at 4:45; Antonio Tiberi took over the white jersey.
