In the headlines

Giro 2022 · Stage 3

Cavendish takes his 16th Giro stage win in Balatonfüred

Bais and Tagliani were in the breakaway again, this time with Samuele Rivi, while Jan Tratnik became the first rider to abandon after his crash on stage 1. Led out by Michael Mørkøv, Mark Cavendish opened his sprint 300 m out and held off Arnaud Démare and Fernando Gaviria; van der Poel stayed in pink ahead of the first rest day and Rick Zabel took the maglia azzurra.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 13

Cavendish equals Merckx with his 34th stage win

About 60 km from the finish a crash on a descent brought down a group of riders, some of them into a small ravine, and forced Simon Yates, Lucas Hamilton and Roger Kluge out of the race. In a chaotic sprint in Carcassonne Michael Mørkøv led out Mark Cavendish, who won ahead of Mørkøv and Jasper Philipsen for his fourth stage of the race and his 34th in the Tour, equalling Eddy Merckx's record. Cavendish extended his lead in the points classification to 101 points.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 10

Bennett wins his first Tour stage and takes green

On the island-to-island stage from Oléron to Ré, crashes hit the peloton: Sam Bewley abandoned with a broken wrist, and Pogačar's teammate Davide Formolo fractured his collarbone and left the race after the stage. Led out by Michael Mørkøv, Sam Bennett held off Caleb Ewan and Peter Sagan for his first Tour de France stage win and took the green jersey. Primož Roglič stayed in yellow.
