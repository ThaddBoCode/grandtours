In the headlines

Giro 2022 · Stage 8

De Gendt outsprints his breakaway companions in Naples

From a 21-man breakaway, Davide Gabburo attacked 42.8 km from the finish and was joined by Thomas De Gendt, his Lotto–Soudal teammate Harm Vanhoucke and Jorge Arcas. Led out by Vanhoucke, De Gendt beat Gabburo and Arcas in the sprint; Guillaume Martin, part of the original break, moved up to fourth overall.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 14

Mollema solos to Quillan, Martin jumps to second

It took almost 100 km for a 14-rider breakaway including Guillaume Martin to form, and Bauke Mollema attacked from it on a descent 42 km from the finish. He soloed to victory in Quillan, 1:04 ahead of Patrick Konrad and Sergio Higuita. Martin rose from ninth to second overall, 4:04 behind Tadej Pogačar, and Michael Woods took the polka-dot jersey from Nairo Quintana.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 15

Kuss wins in Andorra, Poels back in polka dots

From a 32-rider breakaway, Sepp Kuss attacked 5 km from the top of the Col de Beixalis and won his first Tour stage in Andorra la Vella, 23 seconds ahead of Alejandro Valverde. Nairo Quintana was first over the Port d'Envalira, the highest point of the race, and Wout Poels took back the polka-dot jersey. Pogačar stayed in yellow 5:18 ahead of Rigoberto Urán, while Guillaume Martin lost almost four minutes and dropped to ninth.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 4

Roglič wins the first summit finish at Orcières-Merlette

Jumbo-Visma controlled the race's first summit finish, with Sepp Kuss setting a fierce tempo in the closing kilometres. Primož Roglič chased down Guillaume Martin's late attack and won ahead of Tadej Pogačar and Martin. Julian Alaphilippe finished fifth to keep yellow, and Pogačar took the white jersey.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 15

Pogačar wins on the Grand Colombier as Bernal cracks

The Grand Colombier hosted a stage finish for the first time, and Jumbo-Visma's relentless pace on the final climb dropped Egan Bernal, Nairo Quintana and Guillaume Martin. In the last kilometre Richie Porte and Primož Roglič accelerated, but Tadej Pogačar came past Roglič to win, with Porte third at 5 seconds. Defending champion Bernal lost 7:20, and Roglič led Pogačar by 40 seconds going into the second rest day.
