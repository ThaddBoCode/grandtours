In the headlines

Vuelta 2021 · Stage 20

Storer takes two stages and the mountains jersey

Michael Storer won stages 7 and 10 from the breakaway. He took the mountains jersey on stage 18 and finished the mountains classification with 80 points, ahead of Romain Bardet (61) and Roglič (51).

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 7

Storer solos at Alicante; Valverde crashes out

Michael Storer made his decisive move 3.3 km out from a 29-rider breakaway and won 21 seconds ahead of Carlos Verona, while Pavel Sivakov took the mountains lead. Alejandro Valverde crashed heavily on a descent (fractured collarbone) and abandoned, as did Hugh Carthy; Roglič stayed in red.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 10

Storer solos again; Eiking takes the red jersey

Michael Storer dropped his breakaway companions on the Puerto de Almáchar and soloed to a second stage win. Roglič attacked on the climb but crashed on the descent and was caught; Odd Christian Eiking, part of the break, took the red jersey, with Roglič 2' 17" down.

 Vuelta 2023 · Stage 13

Vingegaard wins on the Tourmalet as Evenepoel loses over 27 minutes

No break formed on the stage, and Michael Storer attacked on the Aubisque. On the Tourmalet Jonas Vingegaard went 8 km from the finish, and Jumbo-Visma filled the top three: Vingegaard, Sepp Kuss (+0:30) and Primož Roglič (+0:33). Kuss stayed in red with Roglič (+1:37) and Vingegaard (+1:44) now second and third; Vingegaard took the polka-dot jersey and Juan Ayuso the white jersey.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 18

López wins on the new Gamoniteiru; Roglič second

Michael Storer rode 72 km solo off the front before David de la Cruz caught him, and Miguel Ángel López then attacked from the GC group to drop de la Cruz and win solo. Roglič outsprinted Mas and Bernal for second at 14", extending his lead to 2' 30", and Storer took the mountains jersey.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 21

Roglič wins the final time trial and the Vuelta

As the last starter Roglič won the 33.8 km time trial from Padrón to Santiago de Compostela in 44' 02", 14 seconds ahead of Magnus Cort, taking his fourth stage and confirming a third straight overall win. Jakobsen (green), Storer (mountains) and Mäder (white) secured their jerseys, and Bahrain Victorious won the team classification.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 11

Gaudu wins at La Farrapona ahead of Soler

Frenchman David Gaudu (Groupama–FDJ) won the summit finish at the Alto de la Farrapona ahead of Marc Soler (+0:04) and Michael Storer (+0:52). In the general classification Roglič stayed ahead of Carapaz (listed on the same time) and Dan Martin (+0:25).
