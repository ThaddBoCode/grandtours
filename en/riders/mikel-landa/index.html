In the headlines

Giro 2021 · Stage 5

Landa crashes out in the finale at Cattolica

In the finale of stage 5 Joe Dombrowski collided with a marshal at a traffic island and Mikel Landa crashed heavily in the chain reaction. Landa was taken to hospital with a broken collarbone and broken ribs, and Dombrowski (concussion), François Bidard and Pavel Sivakov, who also crashed that day, did not start stage 6.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 1

Grande Partenza in Albania

The Giro started on 9 May in Durrës, Albania with three stages there: Durrës to Tirana, a time trial in Tirana and a loop from Vlorë over the Llogara pass. A rest day allowed the transfer across the Adriatic before the race resumed in Alberobello; Mikel Landa crashed out 5 km from the finish of the opening stage.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 8

Pedersen powers to victory in Limoges, Cavendish out

Mark Cavendish crashed and abandoned with a shoulder injury, ending his chase of the stage-win record. A crash 6 km from the line, caused by a spectator, cost Simon Yates and Mikel Landa time and forced Steff Cras out. Mads Pedersen won the uphill sprint ahead of Jasper Philipsen and Wout van Aert.

 Giro 2022

Hindley becomes the first Australian to win the Giro

Jai Hindley won the 105th Giro d'Italia by 1:18 over Richard Carapaz, with Mikel Landa third at 3:24 – his first Grand Tour victory and the first Giro win by an Australian. He started as one of Bora–Hansgrohe's three potential leaders and took over the team leadership by winning stage 9 on the Blockhaus.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 9

Hindley wins the six-man sprint on the Blockhaus

Richard Carapaz attacked 4.6 km from the top, followed only by Romain Bardet and Mikel Landa, before João Almeida, Jai Hindley and Domenico Pozzovivo bridged across. Hindley beat Bardet and Carapaz in the six-man sprint, while Juan Pedro López, distanced after unclipping from his pedal, kept the maglia rosa by 12 seconds over Almeida; Diego Rosa took the maglia azzurra.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 16

Hirt wins the queen stage at Aprica as Carapaz's lead shrinks

On a stage with 5250 m of climbing over the Mortirolo and Santa Cristina, Jan Hirt and Thymen Arensman caught Lennard Kämna, and Hirt went clear 7.9 km from the finish to hold off Arensman by seven seconds for his first Grand Tour stage win. Mikel Landa attacked the favourites, only Carapaz and Hindley could follow, and Hindley's four bonus seconds for third cut Carapaz's lead to three seconds.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 17

Buitrago wins at Lavarone despite an earlier crash

Santiago Buitrago caught Gijs Leemreize on the Monterovere and went clear to win by 35 seconds, despite having crashed earlier in the stage. Behind, Mikel Landa attacked and only Richard Carapaz and Jai Hindley could follow, sprinting to gain six seconds on him; Landa moved up to third, while Simon Yates abandoned.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 19

Bouwman wins again at Castelmonte and secures the maglia azzurra

The stage crossed into Slovenia for the Kolovrat, where Koen Bouwman, Mauro Schmid, Attila Valter and Alessandro Tonelli emerged from the breakaway, later joined by Andrea Vendrame. Bouwman took the sharp final left-hander first and held off Schmid for his second stage win, with maximum points on all four climbs making the maglia azzurra mathematically his; Carapaz, Hindley and Landa remained inseparable.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 21

Sobrero wins in Verona, Hindley seals the Giro

Italian time trial champion Matteo Sobrero won the 17.4 km time trial in 22:24, 23 seconds ahead of Thymen Arensman and 40 ahead of Mathieu van der Poel, for his first Grand Tour stage win. Jai Hindley finished 15th to win the Giro by 1:18 over Carapaz, with Landa third; Hugh Carthy moved up to ninth, but Juan Pedro López kept the maglia bianca.
