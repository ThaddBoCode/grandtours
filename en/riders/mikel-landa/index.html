In the headlines

Vuelta 2026 · Stage 20

Landa wins the queen stage, Mas defends the red jersey

Mikel Landa won solo at the Collado del Alguacil in 4:58:01 after dropping the other escapees, 0:47 ahead of Tobias Halland Johannessen. Felix Gall attacked on the final climb and took back time, but the podium stayed the same: Mas ahead of Roglič (2:15) and Gall (2:44). Sepp Kuss moved up to fifth overall.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 16

Bernal wins at a shortened finish, Landa second

A 17-man break survived to the finish, and Egan Bernal and Mikel Landa were left in front after Clément Braz Afonso punctured. Because of protests on the final climb, the organisers moved the finish line to the 8 km mark, and Bernal won the sprint ahead of Landa, his first Grand Tour stage since his career-threatening crash in 2022. Felix Gall lost 54 seconds, and Giulio Pellizzari moved above him in the general classification.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 5

Landa crashes out in the finale at Cattolica

In the finale of stage 5 Joe Dombrowski collided with a marshal at a traffic island and Mikel Landa crashed heavily in the chain reaction. Landa was taken to hospital with a broken collarbone and broken ribs, and Dombrowski (concussion), François Bidard and Pavel Sivakov, who also crashed that day, did not start stage 6.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 20

Haig steps up after Landa's exit to take third; Mäder wins white

Jack Haig started as a domestique for Mikel Landa, who struggled and abandoned on stage 17; Haig then became team leader. On stage 20 he moved past López into third overall, Gino Mäder won the white jersey and finished fifth, and Bahrain Victorious took the team classification.

 Vuelta 2026

Breakaways decide five of the hardest stages

Andreas Leknessund (stage 7), Jakob Omrzel (12), Marco Brenner (14), Eddie Dunbar (19) and Mikel Landa (20) all won tough stages from the breakaway. Landa won the queen stage solo at the Collado del Alguacil.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 1

Grande Partenza in Albania

The Giro started on 9 May in Durrës, Albania with three stages there: Durrës to Tirana, a time trial in Tirana and a loop from Vlorë over the Llogara pass. A rest day allowed the transfer across the Adriatic before the race resumed in Alberobello; Mikel Landa crashed out 5 km from the finish of the opening stage.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 8

Pedersen powers to victory in Limoges, Cavendish out

Mark Cavendish crashed and abandoned with a shoulder injury, ending his chase of the stage-win record. A crash 6 km from the line, caused by a spectator, cost Simon Yates and Mikel Landa time and forced Steff Cras out. Mads Pedersen won the uphill sprint ahead of Jasper Philipsen and Wout van Aert.

 Giro 2022

Hindley becomes the first Australian to win the Giro

Jai Hindley won the 105th Giro d'Italia by 1:18 over Richard Carapaz, with Mikel Landa third at 3:24 – his first Grand Tour victory and the first Giro win by an Australian. He started as one of Bora–Hansgrohe's three potential leaders and took over the team leadership by winning stage 9 on the Blockhaus.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 9

Hindley wins the six-man sprint on the Blockhaus

Richard Carapaz attacked 4.6 km from the top, followed only by Romain Bardet and Mikel Landa, before João Almeida, Jai Hindley and Domenico Pozzovivo bridged across. Hindley beat Bardet and Carapaz in the six-man sprint, while Juan Pedro López, distanced after unclipping from his pedal, kept the maglia rosa by 12 seconds over Almeida; Diego Rosa took the maglia azzurra.
