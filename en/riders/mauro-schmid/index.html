In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 13

Schmid wins in Belfort, Pidcock jumps to fourth

The longest stage of the race, at 205.8 km, saw a 57-rider breakaway form early on. Mauro Schmid and Harold Tejada went clear 16 km from the finish and Schmid won the sprint in Belfort for his first Tour stage win. Tom Pidcock, also in the break, gained 7:33 on the favourites and jumped from tenth to fourth overall.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 11

Abrahamsen outsprints Schmid in Toulouse, Pogačar crashes unhurt

Jonas Abrahamsen and Mauro Schmid held off the chasing Mathieu van der Poel by 7 seconds, and Abrahamsen won the two-up sprint for his and Uno-X Mobility's first Grand Tour stage. Tadej Pogačar crashed in the finale after contact with Tobias Halland Johannessen, but the GC group waited and Ben Healy kept the yellow jersey.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 11

Schmid wins on the gravel to Montalcino

On a stage with four gravel sectors, Mauro Schmid beat Alessandro Covi in a two-up sprint for his first professional win. Behind them Egan Bernal attacked to finish ahead of all the favourites, while Remco Evenepoel struggled on the gravel and lost about two minutes; Aleksandr Vlasov rose to second overall at 45 seconds.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 17

Philipsen wins in Voiron as the green battle tightens

Pedersen bridged to a large breakaway and won the intermediate sprint, after which a series of attacks followed. In the sprint of a reduced group, Mathieu van der Poel led out Jasper Philipsen, who won ahead of Mauro Schmid and Olav Kooij. Pedersen finished only eighth and his lead in the points classification shrank to seven points.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 18

Carapaz solos to victory at Orcières-Merlette

Pedersen joined a large breakaway and took maximum points at the intermediate sprint, extending his lead over Philipsen to 32 points. Valentin Paret-Peintre chased mountain points over the climbs, but Carapaz was strongest on the final ascent, going clear 3.5 km from the line to win ahead of Mauro Schmid and Matteo Jorgenson. The favourites finished together, while illness in the UAE team forced Pogačar's teammate Brandon McNulty out of the race.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 13

Woods wins at Puerto de Ancares, O'Connor loses almost two minutes

Michael Woods (Israel–Premier Tech) attacked alone on the last climb and won ahead of Mauro Schmid (+0:45) and Marc Soler (+1:11). In the peloton Ben O'Connor lost 1:55 to Roglič but kept the red jersey, his lead down to 1:21. Wout van Aert took the lead of the mountains classification.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 18

Berrade wins solo in Maeztu, Soler takes the polka-dot jersey

From a leading group of 42 riders, Urko Berrade (Equipo Kern Pharma) counter-attacked after a move by Steven Kruijswijk, held off the chasers and won in Maeztu ahead of Mauro Schmid and Max Poole (both +0:04). Marc Soler took the polka-dot jersey. In the standings O'Connor stayed 5 seconds ahead of Roglič.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 19

Bouwman wins again at Castelmonte and secures the maglia azzurra

The stage crossed into Slovenia for the Kolovrat, where Koen Bouwman, Mauro Schmid, Attila Valter and Alessandro Tonelli emerged from the breakaway, later joined by Andrea Vendrame. Bouwman took the sharp final left-hander first and held off Schmid for his second stage win, with maximum points on all four climbs making the maglia azzurra mathematically his; Carapaz, Hindley and Landa remained inseparable.
