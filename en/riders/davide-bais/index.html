Giro 2023 · Stage 7

Davide Bais wins from the breakaway on Campo Imperatore

A breakaway trio fought out the 218 km stage to Campo Imperatore on the Gran Sasso, where Davide Bais of Eolo–Kometa beat Karel Vacek by nine seconds and Simone Petilli by 16. Bais took the maglia azzurra, and Leknessund kept the maglia rosa.
