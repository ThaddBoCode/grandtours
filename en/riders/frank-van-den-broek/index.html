In the headlines

Tour 2025 · Stage 20

Groves solos to Pontarlier to complete his Grand Tour stage set

Kaden Groves went clear of what remained of a 13-rider breakaway 16.5 km from the finish and won alone in Pontarlier, 54 seconds ahead of Frank van den Broek. The win completed his set of stage victories in all three Grand Tours. Jordan Jegat, also in the break, moved into tenth overall at Ben O'Connor's expense.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 1

Bardet takes his first yellow jersey in his final Tour

In what he had announced as his last Tour, Romain Bardet attacked about 50 km from the finish of the opening stage, joined his teammate Frank van den Broek and won in Rimini 5 seconds ahead of the peloton. It was the first time he had worn the yellow jersey; he lost it to Pogačar the next day in Bologna.
