In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 13

Schmid wins in Belfort, Pidcock jumps to fourth

The longest stage of the race, at 205.8 km, saw a 57-rider breakaway form early on. Mauro Schmid and Harold Tejada went clear 16 km from the finish and Schmid won the sprint in Belfort for his first Tour stage win. Tom Pidcock, also in the break, gained 7:33 on the favourites and jumped from tenth to fourth overall.

 Vuelta 2025

Pidcock reaches his first Grand Tour podium in third

Tom Pidcock (Q36.5) finished third overall, 3:11 behind, for his first Grand Tour podium. He was consistent in the mountains, took second on stages 9 and 17, attacked twice on stage 11 and held off Jai Hindley, who finished fourth (+3:41), over the final two weeks.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 12

Pidcock becomes the youngest winner on Alpe d'Huez

On the Bastille Day queen stage, Tom Pidcock bridged across to the breakaway, attacked 10.6 km from the finish and won alone on Alpe d'Huez, 48 seconds ahead of Louis Meintjes and more than two minutes ahead of Chris Froome. It was his first WorldTour win and made him the youngest rider to win on Alpe d'Huez.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 9

Van der Poel wins the shortened heatwave stage to Ussel

Because of a red heatwave warning for the Corrèze department, the stage was shortened by 30.9 km and raced in temperatures of up to 40 °C. Mathieu van der Poel attacked from an eight-man breakaway on Mont Bessou, with only Tobias Halland Johannessen and Tom Pidcock able to follow and Alex Baudin joining on the descent. Van der Poel won the sprint six seconds ahead of the peloton – in the Limousin, home region of his grandfather Raymond Poulidor – and the GC was unchanged.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 5

Pedersen edges Zambanini in a photo finish at Matera

Mads Pedersen lost ground on the short climb 2 km from the finish but came back to beat Edoardo Zambanini in a photo finish, with Tom Pidcock third. It was his third win in four road stages, and his lead over Roglič grew to 17 seconds.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 9

Van Aert beats del Toro on the gravel to Siena

Five gravel sectors split the race on the way to Siena, where Roglič and Tom Pidcock crashed and punctured. Isaac del Toro attacked twice, only Wout van Aert could follow, and the Belgian passed the Mexican in the final metres at the Piazza del Campo. Del Toro took the maglia rosa, 1:13 ahead of Ayuso, and the maglia bianca, while Roglič dropped to tenth at 2:25.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 11

Carapaz attacks late to win at Castelnovo ne' Monti

Lorenzo Fortunato attacked from a large break 103 km from the finish, but the escapees were caught on the Pietra di Bismantova. Richard Carapaz went clear of the maglia rosa group 8 km from the line and won by 10 seconds from del Toro, Giulio Ciccone and Tom Pidcock. Del Toro stayed in pink, 31 seconds ahead of Ayuso.

 Vuelta 2025

Vingegaard wins his first Vuelta by 1:16

Jonas Vingegaard won the Vuelta for the first time in his career, taking stages 2, 9 and 20 and finishing 1:16 ahead of João Almeida. He got sick during the second week and had to defend his red jersey before sealing the overall win on the Bola del Mundo, while Almeida could only take time through bonus seconds and the time trial. Tom Pidcock was third (+3:11), his first Grand Tour podium.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 17

Pellizzari wins his first Grand Tour stage at El Morredero

After Visma–Lease a Bike set the pace on the final climb, Jai Hindley attacked, and João Almeida and Giulio Pellizzari were briefly distanced but came back. Near the top Pellizzari got a gap while the favourites looked at each other, and he held on for his first Grand Tour stage win, ahead of Tom Pidcock and Hindley; Jonas Vingegaard extended his lead over Almeida to 50 seconds.
