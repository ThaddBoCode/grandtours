In the headlines

Tour 2025 · Stage 18

O'Connor wins solo on the Col de la Loze, Pogačar drops Vingegaard

On the queen stage over the Glandon and the Madeleine, Ben O'Connor attacked in the valley before the Col de la Loze and won alone, 1:45 ahead of Tadej Pogačar, who dropped Jonas Vingegaard inside the final kilometre. Pogačar's lead grew to 4:26, while Oscar Onley gained more than a minute and a half on Florian Lipowitz to close within 22 seconds of third place. Lenny Martinez was docked 8 mountains points for taking sticky bottles.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 9

O'Connor wins at Tignes and finishes fourth overall

Ben O'Connor of AG2R Citroën, who started stage 9 8:13 behind Pogačar, went clear from a 41-rider breakaway, won at Tignes by more than five minutes and moved up to second overall. He lost about four minutes on Mont Ventoux but defended fourth place in the final time trial, finishing 10:02 behind Pogačar.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 17

O'Connor wins solo at Madonna di Campiglio

Once again the break decided the stage: Ben O'Connor, second the day before, won alone at Madonna di Campiglio ahead of Hermann Pernsteiner and Thomas De Gendt. The favourites finished together, so the overall standings did not change, and Ruben Guerreiro, in the break, regained the maglia azzurra.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 20

Groves solos to Pontarlier to complete his Grand Tour stage set

Kaden Groves went clear of what remained of a 13-rider breakaway 16.5 km from the finish and won alone in Pontarlier, 54 seconds ahead of Frank van den Broek. The win completed his set of stage victories in all three Grand Tours. Jordan Jegat, also in the break, moved into tenth overall at Ben O'Connor's expense.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 8

Pogačar wins the favourites' sprint at Prati di Tivo

Valentin Paret-Peintre, the last survivor of the breakaway, was caught by the favourites' group 4.2 km from the finish. Tadej Pogačar attacked with 150 m to go to win ahead of Daniel Martínez and Ben O'Connor, and now led Martínez by 2:40 and Geraint Thomas by 2:58. Cian Uijtdebroeks regained the young rider lead.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 10

Pello Bilbao wins in Issoire for Gino Mäder

After a chaotic start in great heat, with Vingegaard and Pogačar themselves joining early attacks, a stable breakaway only formed around halfway. Krists Neilands rode alone in front for a long time but was caught near the finish, and Pello Bilbao won the six-man sprint ahead of Georg Zimmermann and Ben O'Connor. Bilbao dedicated the win to his late teammate Gino Mäder.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 19

Mohorič wins the photo finish in Poligny

After a fast, nervous stage, Kasper Asgreen, Matej Mohorič and Ben O'Connor went clear on the Côte d'Ivory. O'Connor opened the sprint early and Mohorič beat Asgreen in a photo finish by the width of a rim – his third Tour stage win. In an emotional interview he paid tribute to his late teammate Gino Mäder.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 11

Van Aert conquers the double Ventoux

On the first Tour stage to climb Mont Ventoux twice, Wout van Aert went clear from the breakaway on the second ascent and soloed to Malaucène, 1:14 ahead of Kenny Elissonde and Bauke Mollema. Behind, Jonas Vingegaard dropped Tadej Pogačar near the summit, but Pogačar, Richard Carapaz and Rigoberto Urán caught him near the flamme rouge. Urán moved up to second, 5:18 down, while Ben O'Connor lost about four minutes and David Gaudu more than 25.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 20

Van Aert wins the time trial as Pogačar seals the Tour

Wout van Aert set the only time under 36 minutes, 35:53, to win the 30.8 km time trial from Libourne to Saint-Émilion, 21 seconds ahead of Kasper Asgreen and 32 ahead of Jonas Vingegaard. Vingegaard secured second overall, Ben O'Connor held fourth ahead of Wilco Kelderman, and Tadej Pogačar rode cautiously to eighth. Going into Paris he led Vingegaard by 5:20 and Richard Carapaz by 7:03.
