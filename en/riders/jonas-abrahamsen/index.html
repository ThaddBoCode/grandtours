In the headlines

Tour 2025 · Stage 11

Abrahamsen outsprints Schmid in Toulouse, Pogačar crashes unhurt

Jonas Abrahamsen and Mauro Schmid held off the chasing Mathieu van der Poel by 7 seconds, and Abrahamsen won the two-up sprint for his and Uno-X Mobility's first Grand Tour stage. Tadej Pogačar crashed in the finale after contact with Tobias Halland Johannessen, but the GC group waited and Ben Healy kept the yellow jersey.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 1

Bardet and van den Broek hold off the bunch, Bardet in yellow

Romain Bardet attacked on the climb of San Leo about 50 km from the finish and joined his breakaway teammate Frank van den Broek. The dsm–firmenich PostNL duo held off the favourites by 5 seconds in Rimini, and Bardet took the first yellow jersey of his career in his last Tour. Van den Broek led the points and young rider classifications, Jonas Abrahamsen the mountains classification.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 2

Vauquelin wins solo on San Luca, Pogačar takes yellow

Kévin Vauquelin went clear of the breakaway on the second ascent of San Luca and won alone in Bologna, 36 seconds ahead of Jonas Abrahamsen. Behind, Tadej Pogačar attacked on the climb and only Jonas Vingegaard could follow; Remco Evenepoel and Richard Carapaz rejoined them just before the line. Pogačar took yellow from the dropped Bardet on the same time as those three, and Abrahamsen also moved into the lead of the points classification.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 8

Girmay doubles up in green at Colombey-les-Deux-Églises

Jonas Abrahamsen spent much of the day alone at the front but was caught in the finale. In the slightly uphill sprint, green jersey Biniam Girmay took his second stage win ahead of Jasper Philipsen and Arnaud De Lie. Mads Pedersen did not start, not having recovered from his stage 5 crash.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 18

Asgreen's breakaway beats the sprinters in Bourg-en-Bresse

Wout van Aert did not start, leaving the race to be with his wife, who was about to give birth. Kasper Asgreen, Jonas Abrahamsen and Victor Campenaerts, later joined by Pascal Eenkhoorn, were out front all day with barely more than a minute's lead. Three of them held off the charging bunch by a whisker, Asgreen winning ahead of Eenkhoorn and Abrahamsen, with Philipsen fourth.
