In the headlines

Giro 2021 · Stage 17

Dan Martin completes his set at Sega di Ala

Dan Martin attacked from the break on the final climb and held on to win at Sega di Ala, completing stage wins in all three Grand Tours. João Almeida and Simon Yates were second and third, while Egan Bernal had his first bad day but still led Damiano Caruso by 2:21; a crash in the peloton involved Remco Evenepoel, who did not start the next day.
