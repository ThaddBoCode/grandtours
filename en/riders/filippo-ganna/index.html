In the headlines

Giro 2026 · Stage 10

Ganna dominates the time trial to Massa

Filippo Ganna (Netcompany INEOS) won the 42 km individual time trial from Viareggio to Massa by 1:54 over teammate Thymen Arensman, who gained the most among the favourites and moved up to third overall. Jonas Vingegaard missed the top ten but closed to 27 seconds, so Afonso Eulálio kept the maglia rosa despite a poor time trial.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 14

Ganna takes his time-trial revenge on Lake Garda

In the first time trial to Perugia, Filippo Ganna was still 47 seconds ahead of Pogačar at the second checkpoint but lost by 17 seconds on the closing climb. A week later he turned the tables, winning the time trial to Desenzano del Garda by 29 seconds over the man in pink.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 7

Pogačar overturns Ganna's lead on the climb to Perugia

Filippo Ganna was fastest at both checkpoints and still 47 seconds up on Tadej Pogačar at the second. On the closing uphill kilometres to Perugia, Pogačar overturned the deficit and won the 40.6 km time trial by 17 seconds. His lead grew to 2:36 over Daniel Martínez, and Luke Plapp took over the young rider lead.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 21

Ganna opens and closes the Giro with time trial wins

Filippo Ganna won the 8.6 km opening time trial in Turin, taking the first maglia rosa for the second year running, and, despite a puncture, the closing 30.3 km time trial into Milan – his fifth consecutive time trial win at the Giro. In between he worked for Egan Bernal, including on the gravel to Montalcino.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 1

Ganna wins in Turin and opens in pink again

Time trial world champion Filippo Ganna won the 8.6 km opener in Turin in 8:47, 10 seconds faster than Edoardo Affini and 13 faster than Tobias Foss. He took the first maglia rosa for the second year in a row, with João Almeida the best of the favourites in fourth.

 Giro 2020

World champion Ganna wins four stages

Fresh from winning the time trial world title in Imola, Filippo Ganna won all three time trials – in Palermo, Valdobbiadene and Milan – and added a solo road win at Camigliatello Silano that also put him in the maglia azzurra. With further wins from Jhonatan Narváez and Tao Geoghegan Hart, INEOS Grenadiers took seven stages.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 1

Ganna storms to Palermo and the first maglia rosa

Newly crowned time trial world champion Filippo Ganna won the 15 km opening time trial from Monreale to Palermo, 22 seconds ahead of João Almeida and Mikkel Bjerg. Geraint Thomas was fourth at 23 seconds, while Miguel Ángel López abandoned on the opening day.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 5

Ganna the climber: solo win at Camigliatello Silano

Filippo Ganna dropped his breakaway companions on the climb of the Valico di Montescuro and won alone at Camigliatello Silano, also taking a surprise maglia azzurra. João Almeida collected four bonus seconds for third place, Simon Yates lost more ground and Pello Bilbao moved up to second overall.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 14

Ganna wins the time trial to Valdobbiadene

Filippo Ganna won the 34.1 km time trial from Conegliano to Valdobbiadene, 26 seconds ahead of INEOS teammate Rohan Dennis and more than a minute ahead of everyone else. João Almeida extended his lead over Wilco Kelderman to 56 seconds, and Brandon McNulty, third on the stage, moved up to fourth overall.
