In the headlines

Vuelta 2023 · Stage 19

Dainese wins the sprint in Íscar ahead of Ganna

Four escapees were never more than three minutes clear and were caught 20 km from the finish, after which an attack by Samuele Battistella was also reeled in. A crash at the flamme rouge brought down green jersey Kaden Groves. Alberto Dainese won the sprint ahead of fellow Italian Filippo Ganna, and the overall standings did not change.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 7

Herrada wins from the break in Cistierna

Six riders went clear early and held off the peloton, with Fred Wright starting the sprint 300 m out. Jesús Herrada won his second Vuelta stage ahead of Samuele Battistella and Wright. Sam Bennett led the peloton home 29 seconds later, and Remco Evenepoel stayed in red as the top ten did not change.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 9

Meintjes wins at Les Praeres, Evenepoel extends his lead

Louis Meintjes won from the break for his first World Tour victory, 1:01 ahead of Samuele Battistella, with Edoardo Zambanini third. Remco Evenepoel dropped his rivals on the final climb, which averages 12.9 percent, finished 1:34 behind Meintjes and 34 seconds ahead of Juan Ayuso, and stretched his lead over Enric Mas to 1:12.
