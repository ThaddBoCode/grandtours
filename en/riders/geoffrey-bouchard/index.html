In the headlines

Giro 2021

Sagan in ciclamino, Bouchard in azzurra

Peter Sagan won stage 10 in Foligno, took the maglia ciclamino and kept it to Milan, finishing with 136 points to Davide Cimolai's 118. Geoffrey Bouchard took the maglia azzurra at Campo Felice and held it to the end by repeatedly taking maximum points on the early climbs.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 9

Bernal attacks on the gravel and takes pink

The stage finished on gravel roads at Campo Felice, where Egan Bernal countered Aleksandr Vlasov, dropped Giulio Ciccone and won by seven seconds to take the maglia rosa and the maglia bianca, 15 seconds ahead of Remco Evenepoel. Geoffrey Bouchard took the maglia azzurra, and Matej Mohorič abandoned after a heavy crash on the descent of the Passo Godi.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 12

Vendrame wins in Bagno di Romagna

An 18-rider break went clear, with Geoffrey Bouchard extending his lead in the mountains classification. Andrea Vendrame beat Chris Hamilton in a two-up sprint in Bagno di Romagna, while early crashes forced out Marc Soler and former race leader Alessandro De Marchi, who suffered fractures to his collarbone, ribs and vertebrae.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 21

Ganna wins in Milan, Bernal wins the Giro

Filippo Ganna won the 30.3 km time trial from Senago to Milan despite a puncture, 12 seconds ahead of Rémi Cavagna, who crashed into the barriers in the final kilometre. Egan Bernal rode safely to the finish to win the Giro by 1:29 over Damiano Caruso, with Simon Yates third, while Peter Sagan and Geoffrey Bouchard confirmed the maglia ciclamino and the maglia azzurra.
