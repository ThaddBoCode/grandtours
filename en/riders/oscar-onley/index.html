In the headlines

Vuelta 2026

Buitrago, Onley and Decathlon take the other classifications

Santiago Buitrago (Bahrain Victorious) won the mountains classification with 78 points, and Oscar Onley (Netcompany INEOS), sixth overall, took the young rider classification. Decathlon CMA CGM won the team classification, with Felix Gall finishing third overall.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 4

Pogačar wins solo in Andorra after a 50 km attack

Tadej Pogačar attacked 50 km from the finish on the Col de Beixalís and won in Andorra la Vella in 2:40:25. Jarno Widar led the chasers home 2:39 behind, with Felix Gall and Oscar Onley in the same group. Primož Roglič moved up to second overall at 3:21 ahead of Enric Mas (3:32), and Onley took the white jersey.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 9

Mas wins on the Alto de Aitana and extends his lead

Enric Mas won the mountain stage to the Alto de Aitana in 5:10:40, beating Oscar Onley, the only rider able to follow him in the final kilometre, in the sprint. Roglič was third at 0:12 and Gall fourth at 0:18. Mas now led Roglič by 1:18 and Gall by 1:39, and Santiago Buitrago took the polka-dot jersey.

 Tour 2025

Lipowitz becomes the first German on the podium since 2006

Florian Lipowitz finished third, 11:00 behind Pogačar, and won the white jersey – the first German on the Tour podium since Andreas Klöden in 2006. He moved up to third when Evenepoel abandoned on stage 14, saw Oscar Onley close to within 22 seconds at the Col de la Loze, then pulled clear again at La Plagne.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 18

O'Connor wins solo on the Col de la Loze, Pogačar drops Vingegaard

On the queen stage over the Glandon and the Madeleine, Ben O'Connor attacked in the valley before the Col de la Loze and won alone, 1:45 ahead of Tadej Pogačar, who dropped Jonas Vingegaard inside the final kilometre. Pogačar's lead grew to 4:26, while Oscar Onley gained more than a minute and a half on Florian Lipowitz to close within 22 seconds of third place. Lenny Martinez was docked 8 mountains points for taking sticky bottles.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 19

Arensman holds off Vingegaard and Pogačar at La Plagne

An outbreak of lumpy skin disease among cattle forced a late reroute that cut the stage to 93.1 km, still finishing at La Plagne. Thymen Arensman went clear 14 km from the finish and held off Jonas Vingegaard and Tadej Pogačar by 2 seconds for his second stage win. Florian Lipowitz gained 41 seconds on Oscar Onley to strengthen his hold on third place overall.

 Vuelta 2023 · Stage 2

Kron wins on Montjuïc, Piccolo takes the red jersey

In the rain, the GC time was taken 9 km from the finish, which gave the red jersey to Andrea Piccolo of the day’s break; after that the favourites eased off. Andreas Kron attacked on Montjuïc and won solo ahead of Kaden Groves and Andrea Vendrame. Oscar Onley left the race after a crash.
