In the headlines

Giro 2022

Bouwman wins the maglia azzurra and two stages

Koen Bouwman of Team Jumbo–Visma won from breakaways in Potenza and at the Santuario di Castelmonte and took the mountains classification with 294 points ahead of Giulio Ciccone. By taking maximum points on all four climbs of stage 19 he made the maglia azzurra mathematically his.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 7

Bouwman wins in Potenza and takes the maglia azzurra

A seven-man breakaway including Tom Dumoulin, Bauke Mollema and Davide Formolo fought out the hilly stage, with Koen Bouwman taking maximum points on Monte Sirino, at Viggiano and on La Sellata. Led out by Dumoulin, Bouwman won the sprint ahead of Mollema and Formolo for his first Grand Tour stage win and took the maglia azzurra; the favourites finished together almost three minutes down.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 19

Bouwman wins again at Castelmonte and secures the maglia azzurra

The stage crossed into Slovenia for the Kolovrat, where Koen Bouwman, Mauro Schmid, Attila Valter and Alessandro Tonelli emerged from the breakaway, later joined by Andrea Vendrame. Bouwman took the sharp final left-hander first and held off Schmid for his second stage win, with maximum points on all four climbs making the maglia azzurra mathematically his; Carapaz, Hindley and Landa remained inseparable.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 15

Ciccone solos to victory in Cogne

From a 28-man breakaway, Giulio Ciccone, Santiago Buitrago and Antonio Pedrero went clear on the climb to Verrogne, and Ciccone attacked twice on the steepest section of the final climb to Cogne. He won alone, 1:31 ahead of Buitrago; Koen Bouwman regained the maglia azzurra and Richard Carapaz, who had crashed early in the stage, kept pink.
