In the headlines

Vuelta 2025 · Stage 3

Gaudu out-sprints Pedersen after diving into the final corner

On the small uphill finish in Ceres, David Gaudu dive-bombed the final corner to take the lead from Mads Pedersen and win the sprint. Jonas Vingegaard took third and four bonus seconds, which kept him in the red jersey on the same time as Gaudu.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 4

Turner wins in Voiron, Gaudu takes the red jersey

The race crossed from Italy into France, and despite three categorised climbs the stage was decided by a bunch sprint, which Ben Turner won narrowly from Jasper Philipsen for his first Grand Tour stage. David Gaudu took the red jersey on the lowest sum of stage placings after finishing level on time with Jonas Vingegaard, and Mads Pedersen moved into the lead of the points classification.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 11

David Gaudu wins mountain stages 11 and 17

Frenchman David Gaudu (Groupama–FDJ) won stage 11 at the Alto de la Farrapona ahead of Marc Soler (+0:04) and stage 17 at La Covatilla ahead of Gino Mäder (+0:28). He finished eighth overall (+7:45) and second in the young rider classification behind Enric Mas.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 5

UAE win the team time trial, Vingegaard back in red

In the 24.1 km team time trial in Figueres, UAE Team Emirates XRG clocked 25:26, eight seconds ahead of Visma–Lease a Bike and nine ahead of Lidl–Trek. Jonas Vingegaard regained the red jersey as David Gaudu lost 16 seconds; protesters blocked the ride of Israel–Premier Tech, which was credited with 15 seconds.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 19

Roglič attacks on Moncalvillo and takes the red jersey back

Before stage 19, Roglič and O'Connor were only 5 seconds apart. Roglič won at the Alto de Moncalvillo, 46 seconds ahead of David Gaudu and Mattias Skjelmose, and led O'Connor by 1:54 afterwards. That effectively decided the general classification; Skjelmose took the white jersey.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 6

Pogačar wins the uphill sprint in Longwy and takes yellow

On the longest stage of the race, van Aert spent much of the day in the breakaway wearing yellow but was caught 11.1 km from the finish and dropped. Pogačar won the uphill sprint in Longwy ahead of Michael Matthews and David Gaudu and took the yellow jersey, 4 seconds ahead of Neilson Powless and 31 ahead of Vingegaard.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 11

Van Aert conquers the double Ventoux

On the first Tour stage to climb Mont Ventoux twice, Wout van Aert went clear from the breakaway on the second ascent and soloed to Malaucène, 1:14 ahead of Kenny Elissonde and Bauke Mollema. Behind, Jonas Vingegaard dropped Tadej Pogačar near the summit, but Pogačar, Richard Carapaz and Rigoberto Urán caught him near the flamme rouge. Urán moved up to second, 5:18 down, while Ben O'Connor lost about four minutes and David Gaudu more than 25.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 18

Pogačar doubles up at Luz Ardiden and takes polka dots

Pierre Latour was first over the Col du Tourmalet, and David Gaudu was the last of the breakaway to be caught on the final climb. Tadej Pogačar attacked 3.3 km from the top of Luz Ardiden, closed down two moves by Enric Mas in the final kilometre and won ahead of Jonas Vingegaard and Richard Carapaz, both 2 seconds back. With 80 points from his two summit wins Pogačar also took the polka-dot jersey, and his lead grew to 5:45 over Vingegaard; Rigoberto Urán lost almost nine minutes.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 17

Roglič survives Carapaz's attack on La Covatilla

David Gaudu won the final mountain stage, but the race was decided behind him: Carapaz cut Roglič's lead from 45 to 24 seconds and could do no more.
