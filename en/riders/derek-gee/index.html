In the headlines

Giro 2023

Derek Gee: four stage second places, runner-up twice more

Derek Gee of Israel–Premier Tech finished second on four stages – at Fossombrone, Viareggio, Cassano Magnago and Tre Cime di Lavaredo – and was runner-up in both the points and the mountains classification.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 19

Kuss wins the queen stage, Hindley moves onto the podium

On the Dolomites stage over six climbs, including the Passo Giau as the roof of the race, Sepp Kuss (Visma–Lease a Bike) won from the breakaway at Alleghe, 13 seconds ahead of Derek Gee-West and 36 ahead of Giulio Ciccone, who took the maglia azzurra. Thymen Arensman cracked and lost third place to Jai Hindley, and Jhonatan Narváez abandoned.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 9

Turgis wins the gravel stage around Troyes

On a stage with 14 gravel sectors around Troyes, the breakaway stayed away and Anthony Turgis won the sprint ahead of Tom Pidcock and Derek Gee. Tadej Pogačar and Remco Evenepoel attacked repeatedly, but Jonas Vingegaard and the other favourites always came back. The top of the general classification was unchanged going into the first rest day.

 Giro 2023

Pinot wins the maglia azzurra and finishes fifth

Thibaut Pinot won the mountains classification with 237 points ahead of Derek Gee and finished fifth overall. He was runner-up on two stages: at Crans-Montana behind Einer Rubio and at Zoldo Alto behind Filippo Zana.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 2

Milan wins in San Salvo and keeps the maglia ciclamino to Rome

Jonathan Milan won stage 2 in San Salvo and led the points classification from then on, finishing with 217 points ahead of Derek Gee. He was also second in Salerno, Naples, Tortona and Caorle.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 8

Healy solos 50 km to his first Grand Tour stage win

Ben Healy attacked about 50 km from the finish and won alone in Fossombrone, 1:49 ahead of Derek Gee, Filippo Zana and Warren Barguil, for his first Grand Tour stage win. Behind, Remco Evenepoel lost time to Primož Roglič and Geraint Thomas, while Andreas Leknessund kept pink by eight seconds over him.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 10

Cort wins in Viareggio as Thomas takes over the maglia rosa

Nine riders, including Evenepoel, Domenico Pozzovivo, Stefan Küng and Rigoberto Urán, did not start after the rest day, so Geraint Thomas rode in pink, two seconds ahead of Primož Roglič. Magnus Cort beat Derek Gee and Alessandro De Marchi, 51 seconds clear of the bunch, while Aleksandr Vlasov abandoned and Jay Vine crashed and lost more than ten minutes.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 14

Denz doubles up as Armirail takes the maglia rosa

The stage from Sierre in Switzerland went to the breakaway, with Nico Denz taking his second stage win in Cassano Magnago ahead of Derek Gee and Alberto Bettiol. Bruno Armirail took the maglia rosa, 1:41 ahead of Geraint Thomas.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 19

Buitrago wins the queen stage to Tre Cime di Lavaredo

Santiago Buitrago won the queen stage to Tre Cime di Lavaredo, 51 seconds ahead of Derek Gee. Primož Roglič gained three seconds on Geraint Thomas, cutting his deficit to 26 seconds before the mountain time trial, with João Almeida third at 59.
