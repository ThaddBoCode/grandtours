In the headlines

Giro 2021

Sagan in ciclamino, Bouchard in azzurra

Peter Sagan won stage 10 in Foligno, took the maglia ciclamino and kept it to Milan, finishing with 136 points to Davide Cimolai's 118. Geoffrey Bouchard took the maglia azzurra at Campo Felice and held it to the end by repeatedly taking maximum points on the early climbs.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 3

Van der Hoorn holds off the peloton in Canale

Taco van der Hoorn escaped from the break, dropped Simon Pellaud and held off the chasing peloton by four seconds in Canale, ahead of Davide Cimolai and Peter Sagan. It was his first Grand Tour stage win and the first for Intermarché–Wanty–Gobert Matériaux on their Giro debut; Filippo Ganna kept the maglia rosa.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 7

Ewan's second win, in Termoli

Fernando Gaviria made a surprise attack in the final kilometre, but Caleb Ewan closed the gap and came past to win ahead of Davide Cimolai and Tim Merlier. Ewan took the maglia ciclamino; Domenico Pozzovivo had not started after crashes the day before, and Attila Valter kept the maglia rosa.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 10

Sagan wins in Foligno and takes the maglia ciclamino

Bora–Hansgrohe set such a hard pace on the Valico della Somma that Tim Merlier, Giacomo Nizzolo and Dylan Groenewegen were dropped. Peter Sagan launched his sprint from a long way out, held off Fernando Gaviria and Davide Cimolai and took the maglia ciclamino – his first Giro stage win had also come on stage 10, a year earlier.
