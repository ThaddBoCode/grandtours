In the headlines

Vuelta 2026 · Stage 18

Küng wins the Jerez time trial, Mas limits his losses to Roglič

Stefan Küng won the 32.5 km time trial from El Puerto de Santa María to Jerez de la Frontera in 35:22, 0:03 ahead of Callum Thornley and 0:09 ahead of João Almeida. Roglič was fourth (0:19). Mas conceded only about half a minute to Roglič, keeping a 1:37 lead, while Gall dropped to third overall at 3:01.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 21

Küng wins the Madrid time trial as Roglič secures a fourth overall win

Stefan Küng won the 24.6 km time trial in Madrid in 26:28, 31 seconds ahead of Roglič and 42 ahead of Mattia Cattaneo. The podium did not change: Roglič won by 2:36 over Ben O'Connor and 3:13 over Enric Mas, equalling Roberto Heras' record of four Vuelta wins.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 10

Cort wins in Viareggio as Thomas takes over the maglia rosa

Nine riders, including Evenepoel, Domenico Pozzovivo, Stefan Küng and Rigoberto Urán, did not start after the rest day, so Geraint Thomas rode in pink, two seconds ahead of Primož Roglič. Magnus Cort beat Derek Gee and Alessandro De Marchi, 51 seconds clear of the bunch, while Aleksandr Vlasov abandoned and Jay Vine crashed and lost more than ten minutes.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 5

Pogačar wins the time trial, van der Poel hangs on to yellow

Tadej Pogačar won the 27.2 km time trial from Changé to Laval in 32:00, 19 seconds ahead of European champion Stefan Küng and 27 ahead of Jonas Vingegaard. Mathieu van der Poel was fifth, 31 seconds down, and kept the yellow jersey by 8 seconds over Pogačar. Primož Roglič, Richard Carapaz and Geraint Thomas were now almost two minutes behind Pogačar.
