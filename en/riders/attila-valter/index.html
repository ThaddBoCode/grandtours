In the headlines

Giro 2021 · Stage 6

Valter becomes the first Hungarian in the maglia rosa

Attila Valter (Groupama–FDJ) finished 17 seconds behind Egan Bernal's group at San Giacomo and took the maglia rosa, the first Hungarian to wear it in the history of the Giro. He kept it for three days, until he was dropped on the gravel at Campo Felice.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 19

Bouwman wins again at Castelmonte and secures the maglia azzurra

The stage crossed into Slovenia for the Kolovrat, where Koen Bouwman, Mauro Schmid, Attila Valter and Alessandro Tonelli emerged from the breakaway, later joined by Andrea Vendrame. Bouwman took the sharp final left-hander first and held off Schmid for his second stage win, with maximum points on all four climbs making the maglia azzurra mathematically his; Carapaz, Hindley and Landa remained inseparable.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 7

Ewan's second win, in Termoli

Fernando Gaviria made a surprise attack in the final kilometre, but Caleb Ewan closed the gap and came past to win ahead of Davide Cimolai and Tim Merlier. Ewan took the maglia ciclamino; Domenico Pozzovivo had not started after crashes the day before, and Attila Valter kept the maglia rosa.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 8

Lafay wins solo at Guardia Sanframondi

Victor Lafay attacked from the break on the final climb and won alone, his first professional victory and Cofidis' first Giro stage win since 2010. Points leader Caleb Ewan abandoned during the stage, so Tim Merlier took back the maglia ciclamino, while Attila Valter stayed in pink.
