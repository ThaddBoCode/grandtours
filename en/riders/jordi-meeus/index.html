In the headlines

Tour 2023 · Stage 21

Meeus pips Philipsen on the Champs-Élysées

Pogačar made the first attack on the Paris circuit, but the stage ended in a sprint that Jordi Meeus won by a tyre's width from Jasper Philipsen, denying the green jersey a fifth stage win. Vingegaard crossed the line arm in arm with his teammates to seal his second Tour victory, 7:29 ahead of Pogačar and 10:56 ahead of Adam Yates.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 5

Brennan wins a second sprint in Roquetes

Matthew Brennan won the bunch sprint in Roquetes in 3:47:02 ahead of Jordi Meeus and Vincenzo Albanese, led out in the finale by Christophe Laporte and Wout van Aert. Pogačar kept the red jersey and the top of the general classification was unchanged (Roglič at 3:21).

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 8

Pogačar crashes out, Coquard wins, Mas takes the red jersey

Tadej Pogačar crashed about 30 km from the finish, could not continue because of his injuries and left the race. Bryan Coquard won the bunch sprint in 3:47:06 ahead of Mads Pedersen and Jordi Meeus, taking his first Grand Tour stage win. Enric Mas inherited the red jersey, leading Roglič by 1:00 and Gall by 1:11.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 11

Brennan wins his third stage in Lorca

Matthew Brennan won the sprint in Lorca in 3:17:18 ahead of Bryan Coquard and Jordi Meeus, led out by Wout van Aert. It was his third stage win of this Vuelta. The top of the general classification was unchanged: Mas led Roglič (1:18) and Gall (1:39).

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 17

Brennan sprints to a fifth win in Seville

Matthew Brennan won the sprint in Seville in 4:07:15 ahead of Jordi Meeus and Magnus Cort. It was his fifth stage win, after stages 2, 5, 11 and 16, more than any other rider in this Vuelta. The general classification was unchanged, with Mas ahead of Gall and Roglič.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 17

Milan takes his second stage in Valence after a late crash

A crash just before the flamme rouge brought down several riders, including Biniam Girmay, and held up Tim Merlier, leaving a small group to contest the sprint. Jonathan Milan, who had been dropped on the first climb before his team brought him back, held off Jordi Meeus for his second stage win and stretched his points lead to 72 over Tadej Pogačar. Carlos Rodríguez, tenth overall, fractured his pelvis in a crash in the closing stages and did not start the next day.

 Tour 2023

Philipsen wins four stages and the green jersey

Jasper Philipsen won the bunch sprints in Bayonne, Nogaro, Bordeaux and Moulins and wore green from stage 4 all the way to Paris. He won the points classification for the first time, with 377 points, 119 more than Mads Pedersen, and was beaten by a tyre's width by Jordi Meeus on the Champs-Élysées.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 16

Jakobsen wins a third stage; crash victims abandon

Fabio Jakobsen won his third stage of the race in the sprint, ahead of Jordi Meeus and Matteo Trentin. After a crash a few kilometres after the start, Giulio Ciccone, Rudy Molard and Sep Vanmarcke abandoned the race; Eiking kept the red jersey.
