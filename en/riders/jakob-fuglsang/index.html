In the headlines

Tour 2022 · Stage 16

Houle solos to his first Grand Tour stage win in Foix

Hugo Houle attacked from the breakaway on the descent of the Port de Lers, 38 km from the finish, and won alone, 1:10 ahead of Valentin Madouas and his teammate Michael Woods. Pogačar attacked three times around the top of the Port de Lers but could not shake off Vingegaard, who kept his lead of 2:22. Lennard Kämna and Jakob Fuglsang did not start.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 9

Guerreiro wins at Roccaraso and takes the maglia azzurra

Ruben Guerreiro won from the break on the Aremogna climb above Roccaraso, eight seconds ahead of Jonathan Castroviejo, and moved into the maglia azzurra. On the steep finish Jakob Fuglsang, Wilco Kelderman and Domenico Pozzovivo gained time, Kelderman moving up to second overall, 30 seconds behind João Almeida.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 18

Hindley wins at Laghi di Cancano, Kelderman takes pink

Rohan Dennis led over the Stelvio, the Cima Coppi, with Tao Geoghegan Hart and Jai Hindley, after João Almeida, Vincenzo Nibali, Pello Bilbao and Jakob Fuglsang had been dropped. Hindley won the two-up sprint at Laghi di Cancano ahead of Geoghegan Hart, and Wilco Kelderman took the maglia rosa, 12 seconds ahead of his teammate Hindley and 15 ahead of Geoghegan Hart; Almeida fell to fifth at 2:16.
