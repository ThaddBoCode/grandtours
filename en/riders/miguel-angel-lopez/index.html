In the headlines

Giro 2022

Bardet, Yates and Almeida: contenders leave the race

Several favourites left early: Miguel Ángel López abandoned on stage 4 with a hip injury, Romain Bardet, then fourth overall, quit on stage 13 with stomach problems, and Simon Yates, winner of two stages, abandoned on stage 17. João Almeida, also fourth overall at the time, withdrew before stage 18 after testing positive for COVID-19.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 4

Kämna wins on Etna, Juan Pedro López takes pink

Lennard Kämna caught Juan Pedro López 2.5 km from the top of Etna and beat him in the two-man sprint after López misjudged the final corner. López took the maglia rosa and the maglia bianca, Kämna the maglia azzurra; Miguel Ángel López abandoned with a hip injury and Tom Dumoulin lost seven minutes.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 14

Carapaz wins again at La Pandera, Roglič closes in

Richard Carapaz dropped his break companions 3.3 km from the finish and won solo, 8 seconds ahead of Miguel Ángel López and Primož Roglič. Roglič attacked from the red jersey group and gained 52 seconds on a struggling Remco Evenepoel, cutting his deficit to 1:49; Enric Mas was third overall, 2:43 down.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 15

Arensman wins at Sierra Nevada, Evenepoel holds on

Thymen Arensman attacked from the break 8.7 km from the finish, dropped Marc Soler and took his first Grand Tour stage win. Enric Mas was second at 1:23 and Miguel Ángel López third at 1:25. Remco Evenepoel kept the red jersey, 1:34 ahead of Primož Roglič and 2:01 ahead of Mas.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 18

Evenepoel wins at Alto del Piornal as Vine abandons

Remco Evenepoel won his second stage: he and Enric Mas caught Robert Gesink, the last survivor of a 41-man break, in the final hundred metres before Evenepoel sprinted clear; Mas and Gesink were both 2 seconds behind. Jay Vine crashed early and abandoned, so the polka-dot jersey passed to Richard Carapaz. Evenepoel extended his lead over Mas to 2:07, and Miguel Ángel López moved up to fourth overall.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 7

Crashes and abandons: only 142 of 184 reach Santiago

142 riders finished the race, 42 fewer than started. On stage 7 Alejandro Valverde (fractured collarbone after a crash) and Hugh Carthy abandoned; after the stage 16 crash Giulio Ciccone, Rudy Molard and Sep Vanmarcke left the race, Louis Meintjes followed on stage 19 while tenth overall, and Miguel Ángel López on stage 20.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 18

New climb Altu d'El Gamoniteiru makes its Vuelta debut

Because of the COVID-19 pandemic the Vuelta stayed entirely within Spain, from Burgos to a time trial in Santiago de Compostela. On stage 18 the Altu d'El Gamoniteiru featured for the first time in Vuelta history, and Miguel Ángel López won there solo.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 17

New climbs: López conquers the Col de la Loze

The route featured four climbs used by the Tour for the first time – the Col de la Lusette, the Col de la Hourcère, the Suc au May and the Col de la Loze, at 2,304 m the highest point of the race – while the Grand Colombier hosted its first stage finish. Miguel Ángel López won alone on the Loze on the queen stage and also took the Souvenir Henri Desgrange, before dropping from third to sixth overall in the time trial.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 1

Ganna storms to Palermo and the first maglia rosa

Newly crowned time trial world champion Filippo Ganna won the 15 km opening time trial from Monreale to Palermo, 22 seconds ahead of João Almeida and Mikkel Bjerg. Geraint Thomas was fourth at 23 seconds, while Miguel Ángel López abandoned on the opening day.
