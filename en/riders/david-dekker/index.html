Milan wins the hectic finish in San Salvo

Jonathan Milan won the hectic finish in San Salvo ahead of David Dekker and Kaden Groves and took the maglia ciclamino. Remco Evenepoel stayed in pink, and Paul Lapeira took the maglia azzurra.
