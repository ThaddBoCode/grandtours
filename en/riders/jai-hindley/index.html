In the headlines

Giro 2026 · Stage 19

Kuss wins the queen stage, Hindley moves onto the podium

On the Dolomites stage over six climbs, including the Passo Giau as the roof of the race, Sepp Kuss (Visma–Lease a Bike) won from the breakaway at Alleghe, 13 seconds ahead of Derek Gee-West and 36 ahead of Giulio Ciccone, who took the maglia azzurra. Thymen Arensman cracked and lost third place to Jai Hindley, and Jhonatan Narváez abandoned.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 6

Mass crash on the way to Naples: race neutralised, Hindley out

On the 227 km stage 6 to Naples, the race was neutralised between km 156 and 166.5 after a mass crash in the peloton; former Giro winner Jai Hindley abandoned. No bonus seconds were awarded at the finish, the time gaps did not count for the general classification, and many favourites such as Pedersen and Roglič dropped back for safety reasons.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 5

Hindley wins in Laruns and takes yellow

Jai Hindley got into a big breakaway, dropped Felix Gall on the Col de Marie-Blanque and won alone in Laruns, 32 seconds ahead of Giulio Ciccone and Gall. Behind, Vingegaard attacked on the Marie-Blanque, finished 34 seconds down on Hindley and gained 1:04 on Pogačar. Hindley took yellow by 47 seconds over Vingegaard, and Gall the polka-dot jersey.

 Giro 2022

Hindley becomes the first Australian to win the Giro

Jai Hindley won the 105th Giro d'Italia by 1:18 over Richard Carapaz, with Mikel Landa third at 3:24 – his first Grand Tour victory and the first Giro win by an Australian. He started as one of Bora–Hansgrohe's three potential leaders and took over the team leadership by winning stage 9 on the Blockhaus.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 20

Hindley breaks Carapaz on the Marmolada

Carapaz and Hindley were separated by just three seconds after stage 16 and stayed inseparable until the penultimate stage. On the steep final kilometres of the Passo Fedaia, Hindley attacked, linked up with teammate Lennard Kämna and dropped Carapaz inside the last 3 km, taking the maglia rosa with a lead of 1:25 that he defended in the Verona time trial.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 9

Hindley wins the six-man sprint on the Blockhaus

Richard Carapaz attacked 4.6 km from the top, followed only by Romain Bardet and Mikel Landa, before João Almeida, Jai Hindley and Domenico Pozzovivo bridged across. Hindley beat Bardet and Carapaz in the six-man sprint, while Juan Pedro López, distanced after unclipping from his pedal, kept the maglia rosa by 12 seconds over Almeida; Diego Rosa took the maglia azzurra.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 21

Sobrero wins in Verona, Hindley seals the Giro

Italian time trial champion Matteo Sobrero won the 17.4 km time trial in 22:24, 23 seconds ahead of Thymen Arensman and 40 ahead of Mathieu van der Poel, for his first Grand Tour stage win. Jai Hindley finished 15th to win the Giro by 1:18 over Carapaz, with Landa third; Hugh Carthy moved up to ninth, but Juan Pedro López kept the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 18

Stelvio shake-up: Kelderman in pink, Hindley wins

On the queen stage over the Stelvio, the Cima Coppi, Rohan Dennis set the pace for Tao Geoghegan Hart and distanced João Almeida, Vincenzo Nibali and the other favourites. Jai Hindley won at Laghi di Cancano ahead of Geoghegan Hart, while his Sunweb teammate Wilco Kelderman took the maglia rosa, 12 seconds ahead of Hindley and 15 ahead of Geoghegan Hart.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 20

Dead heat after Sestriere: Hindley and Geoghegan Hart level

Stage 20 was shortened because French COVID-19 rules kept the race out of France, cutting the Colle dell'Agnello and the Izoard. On the climbs to Sestriere INEOS Grenadiers, driven by Rohan Dennis, dropped race leader Wilco Kelderman; Tao Geoghegan Hart beat Jai Hindley in the sprint and, thanks to the bonus seconds, drew level with him on time before the final day.
