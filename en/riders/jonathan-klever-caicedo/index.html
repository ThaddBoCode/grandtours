In the headlines

Giro 2020 · Stage 3

Caicedo wins on Etna as Thomas loses 12 minutes

Jonathan Caicedo won from the breakaway on Etna, 21 seconds ahead of Giovanni Visconti, and took the maglia azzurra. João Almeida moved into the maglia rosa, level on time with Caicedo, while Simon Yates lost more than three minutes and Geraint Thomas, who had crashed before the start, finished about 12 minutes down.

 Vuelta 2023 · Stage 11

Herrada wins from a 26-rider breakaway

A 26-rider break stayed away to the finish at La Laguna Negra, with the favourites finishing well behind. Jonathan Caicedo attacked 1.5 km out but was passed 250 m from the line by Jesús Herrada, who won ahead of Romain Grégoire and Andreas Kron. Herrada took the polka-dot jersey, while the top of the overall standings did not change.
