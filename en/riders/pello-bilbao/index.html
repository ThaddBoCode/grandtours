In the headlines

Tour 2023 · Stage 10

Pello Bilbao wins in Issoire for Gino Mäder

After a chaotic start in great heat, with Vingegaard and Pogačar themselves joining early attacks, a stable breakaway only formed around halfway. Krists Neilands rode alone in front for a long time but was caught near the finish, and Pello Bilbao won the six-man sprint ahead of Georg Zimmermann and Ben O'Connor. Bilbao dedicated the win to his late teammate Gino Mäder.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 3

Pedersen sprints to victory in Vlorë and reclaims the maglia rosa

Lorenzo Fortunato and Pello Bilbao crossed the Llogara pass together in front but were caught 17 km from the finish in Vlorë. Led out by Giulio Ciccone and Mathias Vacek, Mads Pedersen won the sprint ahead of Corbin Strong and Orluis Aular and took back the maglia rosa, 9 seconds ahead of Roglič. Fortunato moved into the maglia azzurra.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 1

Basque Grand Départ: Yates twins one-two, Mas and Carapaz out

The Tour started in Bilbao, the second Grand Départ in the Basque Country after 1992. Twins Adam and Simon Yates broke away in the finale of stage 1 and finished first and second, giving Adam the first yellow jersey. A crash ended the race for pre-race favourites Enric Mas and Richard Carapaz, who did not start stage 2 with a fractured kneecap.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 1

Van der Poel wins at Visegrád and takes the first maglia rosa

Breakaway riders Mattia Bais and Filippo Tagliani were caught 13.7 km from the finish, and Lennard Kämna's late attack on the climb to Visegrád was reeled in inside the last kilometre. Mathieu van der Poel won the uphill sprint ahead of Biniam Girmay and Pello Bilbao and took the maglia rosa, the maglia ciclamino and the maglia azzurra; Girmay took the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 20

Caruso wins at Alpe Motta, Bernal stays safe

Damiano Caruso joined a Team DSM move on the descent of the San Bernardino with teammate Pello Bilbao, dropped Romain Bardet on the final climb and won alone at Alpe Motta for his first Grand Tour stage win. Egan Bernal, paced by Daniel Martínez, finished 24 seconds down and went into the final time trial 1:59 ahead of Caruso.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 5

Ganna the climber: solo win at Camigliatello Silano

Filippo Ganna dropped his breakaway companions on the climb of the Valico di Montescuro and won alone at Camigliatello Silano, also taking a surprise maglia azzurra. João Almeida collected four bonus seconds for third place, Simon Yates lost more ground and Pello Bilbao moved up to second overall.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 18

Hindley wins at Laghi di Cancano, Kelderman takes pink

Rohan Dennis led over the Stelvio, the Cima Coppi, with Tao Geoghegan Hart and Jai Hindley, after João Almeida, Vincenzo Nibali, Pello Bilbao and Jakob Fuglsang had been dropped. Hindley won the two-up sprint at Laghi di Cancano ahead of Geoghegan Hart, and Wilco Kelderman took the maglia rosa, 12 seconds ahead of his teammate Hindley and 15 ahead of Geoghegan Hart; Almeida fell to fifth at 2:16.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 21

Ganna wins in Milan, Geoghegan Hart wins the Giro

With Jai Hindley and Tao Geoghegan Hart level on time, the 15.7 km time trial into Milan decided the Giro. Filippo Ganna won his fourth stage ahead of Victor Campenaerts and Rohan Dennis, while Geoghegan Hart beat Hindley by 39 seconds to take the overall win and João Almeida moved past Pello Bilbao into fourth.
