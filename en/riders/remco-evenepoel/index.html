In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 15

Vingegaard crashes out, Evenepoel wins at Solaison

After night-time doping tests on Vingegaard (around 2 am) and Pogačar (around 5 am), Vingegaard hit the kerb exiting a roundabout about 24 km from the finish, broke his collarbone and abandoned; Tim Merlier also left the race. On the first-ever finish at Plateau de Solaison, Pogačar and del Toro dropped Seixas and Lipowitz, but Evenepoel stayed with them and beat Pogačar in the sprint for his first road-stage win at the Tour. Evenepoel moved up to second overall, five minutes down, and del Toro regained the white jersey.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 14

Evenepoel abandons on the Tourmalet while third overall

Remco Evenepoel won the Caen time trial on stage 5 and was second overall after it, but struggled in the Pyrenees, losing time at Hautacam and Peyragudes. On stage 14 he was dropped early on the Tourmalet and climbed off while third overall; his team said he left to preserve the rest of his season.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 5

Evenepoel wins the Caen time trial, Pogačar takes yellow

World and Olympic time trial champion Remco Evenepoel won the flat 33 km time trial in Caen, 16 seconds ahead of Tadej Pogačar. Pogačar took the yellow jersey, 42 seconds ahead of Evenepoel, while Jonas Vingegaard lost 1:21 and dropped to fourth at 1:13.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 7

Debutant Evenepoel wins the time trial and finishes third

In his first Tour de France, world time trial champion Remco Evenepoel won the stage 7 time trial in Burgundy, the only rider to take time out of Pogačar that day. He finished third overall, 9:18 behind, and won the young rider classification.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 9

Evenepoel leaves the race in pink after a positive COVID-19 test

Remco Evenepoel, one of the pre-race favourites, won the opening time trial in Ortona and the stage 9 time trial in Cesena, where he beat Thomas by one second to take the maglia rosa back from Andreas Leknessund. Shortly afterwards he tested positive for COVID-19 and withdrew; after the rest day Thomas inherited pink, and by stage 11 four more Soudal–Quick-Step riders had tested positive.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 1

Evenepoel wins the Ortona time trial and takes the maglia rosa

Remco Evenepoel won the 19.6 km time trial from Fossacesia to Ortona in 21:18, 22 seconds ahead of Filippo Ganna and 29 ahead of João Almeida. He took the maglia rosa, while Primož Roglič lost 43 seconds and Geraint Thomas 55; Tao Geoghegan Hart took the maglia azzurra.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 17

Evenepoel's comeback ends in a crash

Remco Evenepoel was riding his first race since his crash at Il Lombardia in 2020 and was second overall after stage 9. He lost two minutes on the gravel to Montalcino and more than 24 minutes on the stage to Cortina, and after a crash on stage 17 he did not start stage 18.

 Tour 2027 · Stage 7

71 km of time trials – the most since 2012

Two individual time trials, at Chambord and Toulouse, and no team time trial. The route is seen as a chance for Remco Evenepoel to gain time before the mountains.

 Tour 2026

Pogačar wins his fifth Tour, equalling the record

Tadej Pogačar won the Tour for the fifth time and the third year in a row, joining Jacques Anquetil, Eddy Merckx, Bernard Hinault and Miguel Induráin as the only five-time winners. He won five stages and finished 6:26 ahead of Remco Evenepoel.
