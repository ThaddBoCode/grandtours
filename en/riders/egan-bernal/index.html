In the headlines

Giro 2021

Bernal wins the Giro, the second Colombian to do so

Egan Bernal won the Giro by 1:29 over Damiano Caruso, with Simon Yates third at 4:15 – his second Grand Tour title after the 2019 Tour de France. He became the second Colombian winner after Nairo Quintana in 2014, held the maglia rosa from stage 9 to Milan and also won the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 9

Bernal takes pink on the gravel of Campo Felice

Stage 9 finished on gravel roads at Campo Felice. Bernal countered an attack by Aleksandr Vlasov, then dropped Giulio Ciccone and won alone, taking the maglia rosa 15 seconds ahead of Remco Evenepoel.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 16

Shortened queen stage: Bernal solo over the Giau

Heavy rain and snow forced the organisers to remove the Passo Fedaia and the Passo Pordoi from the queen stage to Cortina d'Ampezzo, making the Giau the Cima Coppi. Bernal attacked on the Giau, crossed the summit alone and won by 27 seconds over Romain Bardet and Damiano Caruso, extending his lead to 2:24, while Remco Evenepoel lost more than 24 minutes.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 20

Caruso wins at Alpe Motta, Bernal stays safe

Damiano Caruso joined a Team DSM move on the descent of the San Bernardino with teammate Pello Bilbao, dropped Romain Bardet on the final climb and won alone at Alpe Motta for his first Grand Tour stage win. Egan Bernal, paced by Daniel Martínez, finished 24 seconds down and went into the final time trial 1:59 ahead of Caruso.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 15

Defending champion Bernal cracks and leaves the race

Egan Bernal was second overall after stage 9, but on the Grand Colombier he was dropped early and lost 7:20, ending INEOS Grenadiers' hopes of a sixth consecutive Tour win; he did not start stage 17. INEOS responded on stage 18 with a one-two for Michał Kwiatkowski and Richard Carapaz at La Roche-sur-Foron.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 1

Visma win the team time trial, Vingegaard takes yellow

For the first time since 1971 the Tour opened with a team time trial, under a new format in which each rider received his own time. Visma–Lease a Bike were 8 seconds faster than Netcompany INEOS and 12 faster than UAE Team Emirates XRG, with Jonas Vingegaard attacking the final climb to Montjuïc to take his first yellow jersey since 2023. Egan Bernal and Tadej Pogačar received the green and polka-dot jerseys for the fastest times on the opening and closing sections.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 7

Ayuso wins at Tagliacozzo, Roglič back in pink

On the first big mountain stage Pedersen was dropped early on the final climb to Tagliacozzo. Juan Ayuso went clear 500 m from the finish and won by 4 seconds from his teammate Isaac del Toro, Egan Bernal and Primož Roglič. Roglič took back the maglia rosa, 4 seconds ahead of Ayuso, who also took the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 6

Valter becomes the first Hungarian in the maglia rosa

Attila Valter (Groupama–FDJ) finished 17 seconds behind Egan Bernal's group at San Giacomo and took the maglia rosa, the first Hungarian to wear it in the history of the Giro. He kept it for three days, until he was dropped on the gravel at Campo Felice.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 21

Ganna opens and closes the Giro with time trial wins

Filippo Ganna won the 8.6 km opening time trial in Turin, taking the first maglia rosa for the second year running, and, despite a puncture, the closing 30.3 km time trial into Milan – his fifth consecutive time trial win at the Giro. In between he worked for Egan Bernal, including on the gravel to Montalcino.
