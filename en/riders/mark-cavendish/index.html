In the headlines

Tour 2024 · Stage 5

Cavendish breaks Merckx's record with his 35th stage win

Mark Cavendish won stage 5 in Saint-Vulbas for his 35th Tour de France stage win, breaking the record of 34 he had shared with Eddy Merckx. It was his last Tour. On the final podium in Nice he received a special presentation: a framed black jersey bearing the letters CAXXXV.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 8

Crash ends Cavendish's chase of the stage-win record

Mark Cavendish started his last Tour level with Eddy Merckx on 34 stage wins. In Bordeaux he finished second to Jasper Philipsen after his gears slipped in the final metres; the next day he crashed on the road to Limoges and had to abandon injured.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 7

Philipsen again in Bordeaux as Cavendish's gears slip

Simon Guglielmi spent most of the day alone in front. In the sprint along the quays of Bordeaux Philipsen narrowly beat Mark Cavendish, whose gears slipped in the final metres, with Biniam Girmay third. It was Philipsen's third stage win, and Vingegaard kept yellow.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 21

Cavendish wins the final stage in Rome

Mark Cavendish of Astana Qazaqstan Team won the closing sprint in Rome ahead of Alex Kirsch and Filippo Fiorelli as Roglič sealed his overall victory. It was Cavendish's only stage win at this Giro.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 3

Cavendish takes his 16th Giro stage win in Balatonfüred

Bais and Tagliani were in the breakaway again, this time with Samuele Rivi, while Jan Tratnik became the first rider to abandon after his crash on stage 1. Led out by Michael Mørkøv, Mark Cavendish opened his sprint 300 m out and held off Arnaud Démare and Fernando Gaviria; van der Poel stayed in pink ahead of the first rest day and Rick Zabel took the maglia azzurra.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 13

Cavendish equals Merckx's record of 34 stage wins

Mark Cavendish, a late replacement for the injured Sam Bennett at Deceuninck–Quick-Step, won in Fougères for his first Tour stage win since 2016 and added further wins in Châteauroux, Valence and Carcassonne. His fourth victory was his 34th Tour stage win, equalling Eddy Merckx's record. He wore the green jersey from stage 4 to Paris and won the points classification for the second time.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 4

Cavendish takes his first Tour stage win since 2016

The peloton stopped for a minute at kilometre zero in protest at the previous day's crashes. Brent Van Moer went clear from the breakaway and was caught only in the final 200 metres, where Mark Cavendish beat Nacer Bouhanni and Jasper Philipsen for his 31st Tour stage win and his first since 2016. Cavendish took the green jersey and van der Poel stayed in yellow.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 6

Cavendish wins in Châteauroux for the third time

Greg Van Avermaet and Roger Kluge were the last breakaway survivors and were caught 2.5 km from the finish. Mark Cavendish came past Jasper Philipsen and Nacer Bouhanni in the final 150 metres for his 32nd Tour stage win, his third in Châteauroux after 2008 and 2011. Van der Poel stayed in yellow and the top ten was unchanged.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 10

Cavendish wins in Valence for number 33

Hugo Houle and Tosh Van der Sande formed the day's breakaway and were caught before the finale; crosswinds split the peloton several times, but no favourite lost time. Deceuninck–Quick-Step led out Mark Cavendish perfectly for his third stage win of the race and his 33rd in the Tour, ahead of Wout van Aert and Jasper Philipsen. The overall standings were unchanged.
