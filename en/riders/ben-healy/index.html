In the headlines

Tour 2025 · Stage 10

Healy: a stage win, two days in yellow and the combativity prize

Ben Healy won stage 6 to Vire Normandie with a solo of more than 40 km, then took the yellow jersey at Puy de Sancy on stage 10 from a 29-rider breakaway and wore it for two days. He finished ninth overall and was named the race's most combative rider.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 6

Healy solos to Vire, van der Poel back in yellow by one second

Ben Healy attacked from an eight-rider breakaway about 42 km from the finish and soloed to his first Tour stage win in Vire Normandie, 2:44 ahead of Quinn Simmons. Mathieu van der Poel, also in the break, gained 1:29 on the favourites and took the yellow jersey back from Tadej Pogačar by a single second.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 16

Paret-Peintre beats Healy on Mont Ventoux

Without Mathieu van der Poel, who did not start because of pneumonia, the race headed to the first summit finish on Mont Ventoux since 2013. Valentin Paret-Peintre came around Ben Healy in the sprint of the breakaway to become the first French stage winner of this Tour. Behind, Tadej Pogačar answered every attack from Jonas Vingegaard and gained 2 seconds, extending his lead to 4:15.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 8

Healy solos 50 km to his first Grand Tour stage win

Ben Healy attacked about 50 km from the finish and won alone in Fossombrone, 1:49 ahead of Derek Gee, Filippo Zana and Warren Barguil, for his first Grand Tour stage win. Behind, Remco Evenepoel lost time to Primož Roglič and Geraint Thomas, while Andreas Leknessund kept pink by eight seconds over him.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 12

Pogačar's Pyrenean double at Hautacam and Peyragudes

On stage 12, the first high-mountain stage, Pogačar attacked 12 km from the top of Hautacam, beat Vingegaard by 2:10 and took the yellow jersey back from Ben Healy. The next day he won the 10.9 km mountain time trial to Peyragudes by 36 seconds – his 21st Tour stage win – and led by 4:07.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 11

Abrahamsen outsprints Schmid in Toulouse, Pogačar crashes unhurt

Jonas Abrahamsen and Mauro Schmid held off the chasing Mathieu van der Poel by 7 seconds, and Abrahamsen won the two-up sprint for his and Uno-X Mobility's first Grand Tour stage. Tadej Pogačar crashed in the finale after contact with Tobias Halland Johannessen, but the GC group waited and Ben Healy kept the yellow jersey.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 14

Pogačar strikes at Pla d'Adet, Vingegaard moves up to second

After the Tourmalet and the Hourquette d'Ancizan, Tadej Pogačar attacked just under 5 km from the summit finish at Pla d'Adet, passing Ben Healy and his teammate Adam Yates. He won 39 seconds ahead of Jonas Vingegaard, with Remco Evenepoel a further 31 seconds back. Vingegaard moved up to second overall at 1:57, while Evenepoel dropped to third at 2:22.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 15

McNulty wins the Lombardia-style stage to Bergamo

Brandon McNulty won from the breakaway in Bergamo, beating Ben Healy and Marco Frigo in a three-man sprint. Bruno Armirail kept the maglia rosa, now 1:08 ahead of Geraint Thomas.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 16

Almeida outduels Thomas on Monte Bondone, Thomas back in pink

João Almeida won atop Monte Bondone ahead of Geraint Thomas on the same time, with Primož Roglič and Eddie Dunbar 25 seconds behind. Thomas took back the maglia rosa, 18 seconds ahead of Almeida and 29 ahead of Roglič, and Ben Healy took the maglia azzurra.
