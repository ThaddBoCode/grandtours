In the headlines

Giro 2027 · Stage 20

The Zoncolan returns on the penultimate day

Stage 20 finishes on Monte Zoncolan from the Ovaro side – 10.1 km at 11.9% with ramps of 22%. It is the mountain's eighth Giro finish and the first since Lorenzo Fortunato won in 2021; on this side Chris Froome won in 2018.

 Giro 2026

Vingegaard wins the Giro and completes the Grand Tour set

Jonas Vingegaard won the Giro d'Italia at his first attempt, becoming the eighth rider to win all three Grand Tours and the first to do so since Chris Froome in 2018. The Dane won five stages, four of them in the mountains, took the maglia rosa on stage 14 and finished in Rome 5:22 ahead of Felix Gall and 6:25 ahead of Jai Hindley.

 Tour 2022

COVID-19 and abandons: the fewest finishers since 2000

In total, 17 riders had to leave the race because of COVID-19, among them stage winners Magnus Cort and Simon Clarke and former winner Chris Froome. Only 135 of the 176 starters reached Paris, the fewest finishers since 2000, and both Vingegaard's and Pogačar's teams were badly depleted by the end.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 12

Pidcock becomes the youngest winner on Alpe d'Huez

On the Bastille Day queen stage, Tom Pidcock bridged across to the breakaway, attacked 10.6 km from the finish and won alone on Alpe d'Huez, 48 seconds ahead of Louis Meintjes and more than two minutes ahead of Chris Froome. It was his first WorldTour win and made him the youngest rider to win on Alpe d'Huez.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 18

Vingegaard wins at Hautacam and all but seals the Tour

Pogačar attacked repeatedly on the Col de Spandelles, crashed on the descent and was waited for by Vingegaard. On Hautacam, Sepp Kuss and Wout van Aert set a fierce pace before Vingegaard went clear about 4 km from the top and won, 1:04 ahead of Pogačar, with van Aert third. Vingegaard's lead grew to 3:26 and he also took the polka-dot jersey from Simon Geschke; Chris Froome had not started after testing positive for COVID-19.
