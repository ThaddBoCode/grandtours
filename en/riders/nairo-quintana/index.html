In the headlines

Giro 2024 · Stage 15

Queen stage to Livigno: Pogačar reels in Quintana

On the longest stage of the Giro at 222 km, Pogačar attacked alone on the final climb to Mottolino, picked off the breakaway riders one by one and caught Nairo Quintana 2 km from the summit. He won by 29 seconds and stretched his lead to 6:41 over Geraint Thomas.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 11

Vingegaard breaks Pogačar on the Granon and takes yellow

Jumbo–Visma attacked Pogačar repeatedly on the Télégraphe and the Galibier, where Warren Barguil was first over the top. On the Col du Granon Vingegaard went clear 4.5 km from the finish and won alone, 59 seconds ahead of Nairo Quintana, while Pogačar lost 2:51. Vingegaard took yellow, 2:16 ahead of Romain Bardet and 2:22 ahead of Pogačar; Mathieu van der Poel abandoned.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 9

O'Connor triumphs at Tignes as Roglič and van der Poel quit

Primož Roglič and Mathieu van der Poel did not start. Ben O'Connor went clear from a 41-rider breakaway on the final climb to Tignes and won 5:07 ahead of Mattia Cattaneo, while Tadej Pogačar dropped his rivals near the top and gained 32 seconds. O'Connor moved up to second overall, 2:01 behind Pogačar, Nairo Quintana took the polka-dot jersey, and seven riders, including Arnaud Démare and Bryan Coquard, missed the time cut.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 14

Mollema solos to Quillan, Martin jumps to second

It took almost 100 km for a 14-rider breakaway including Guillaume Martin to form, and Bauke Mollema attacked from it on a descent 42 km from the finish. He soloed to victory in Quillan, 1:04 ahead of Patrick Konrad and Sergio Higuita. Martin rose from ninth to second overall, 4:04 behind Tadej Pogačar, and Michael Woods took the polka-dot jersey from Nairo Quintana.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 15

Kuss wins in Andorra, Poels back in polka dots

From a 32-rider breakaway, Sepp Kuss attacked 5 km from the top of the Col de Beixalis and won his first Tour stage in Andorra la Vella, 23 seconds ahead of Alejandro Valverde. Nairo Quintana was first over the Port d'Envalira, the highest point of the race, and Wout Poels took back the polka-dot jersey. Pogačar stayed in yellow 5:18 ahead of Rigoberto Urán, while Guillaume Martin lost almost four minutes and dropped to ninth.

 Giro 2021

Bernal wins the Giro, the second Colombian to do so

Egan Bernal won the Giro by 1:29 over Damiano Caruso, with Simon Yates third at 4:15 – his second Grand Tour title after the 2019 Tour de France. He became the second Colombian winner after Nairo Quintana in 2014, held the maglia rosa from stage 9 to Milan and also won the maglia bianca.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 15

Pogačar wins on the Grand Colombier as Bernal cracks

The Grand Colombier hosted a stage finish for the first time, and Jumbo-Visma's relentless pace on the final climb dropped Egan Bernal, Nairo Quintana and Guillaume Martin. In the last kilometre Richie Porte and Primož Roglič accelerated, but Tadej Pogačar came past Roglič to win, with Porte third at 5 seconds. Defending champion Bernal lost 7:20, and Roglič led Pogačar by 40 seconds going into the second rest day.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 16

Kämna makes up for Puy Mary with a solo win at Villard-de-Lans

A breakaway of more than 20 riders built a lead of over 12 minutes, and Richard Carapaz's repeated accelerations on the climb to Saint-Nizier-du-Moucherotte broke it apart. Lennard Kämna attacked just before the summit and won alone, 1:27 ahead of Carapaz and 1:56 ahead of Sébastien Reichenbach. Apart from Tom Dumoulin and Nairo Quintana, the top ten finished together and the first eight places overall were unchanged.
