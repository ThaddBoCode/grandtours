In the headlines

Vuelta 2025 · Stage 6

Ayuso loses seven minutes, then wins two stages

Juan Ayuso, one of UAE's two leaders, lost seven minutes on the final climb to Pal on stage 6 and dropped out of general classification contention. A day later he won from the break at Cerler (stage 7), and on stage 12 he beat Javier Romo in a two-up sprint.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 12

Ayuso beats Romo in a two-up sprint at Los Corrales de Buelna

The stage was tailor-made for a breakaway, with a 53-man group contesting the win. On the final climb, the Collada de Brenes, Juan Ayuso and Javier Romo gapped the rest, and Ayuso outsprinted Romo for his second stage of the race.
