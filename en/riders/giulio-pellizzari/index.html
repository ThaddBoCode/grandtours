In the headlines

Giro 2026 · Stage 9

Vingegaard counters Gall at Corno alle Scale

Decathlon CMA CGM rode for Felix Gall, but Jonas Vingegaard countered 1 km from the finish and won 12 seconds ahead of the Austrian. Gall still gained time on the other contenders, while Giulio Pellizzari struggled on the final climb. Afonso Eulálio stayed in pink, his lead over Vingegaard down to 2:24.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 16

Swiss showcase: Vingegaard wins at Carì

On the stage from Bellinzona to Carì, held entirely in Ticino, Switzerland, Jonas Vingegaard won solo again after strong work by his team, 1:09 ahead of Felix Gall and 1:11 ahead of Jai Hindley. Gall moved up to second at 4:03, Afonso Eulálio dropped to fifth, and Giulio Pellizzari lost more than ten minutes.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 9

Del Toro leads for eleven stages and takes the maglia bianca

Isaac del Toro (UAE Team Emirates XRG), Juan Ayuso's teammate, took the maglia rosa on the gravel stage to Siena and led from stage 9 to stage 19, winning stage 17 in Bormio. He finished second overall and won the young rider classification, 5:32 ahead of Giulio Pellizzari.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 16

Shortened stage: Pogačar takes win number five on Monte Pana

Because of the weather the Umbrail Pass was removed and, after a chaotic start, the stage was cut to 118.7 km starting in Laas. Julian Alaphilippe went solo 33 km from the finish but was caught on the final climb; Tadej Pogačar attacked, passed Giulio Pellizzari 800 m from the line and won by 16 seconds over Pellizzari and Daniel Martínez. Martínez moved back into second overall ahead of Geraint Thomas.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 20

Double Monte Grappa: sixth stage win for Pogačar

On the second ascent of Monte Grappa, Tadej Pogačar attacked 5 km from the summit and 36 km from the finish, caught breakaway rider Giulio Pellizzari and left him behind. After the long descent he won in Bassano del Grappa by 2:07 over Valentin Paret-Peintre, Daniel Martínez and Antonio Tiberi, taking a 9:56 lead into the final stage.
