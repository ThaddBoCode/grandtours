In the headlines

Tour 2022 · Stage 9

Jungels solos to victory in Châtel

Bob Jungels attacked from the big breakaway on the Col de la Croix with 64 km to go and held on alone for his first Tour stage win, 22 seconds ahead of Jonathan Castroviejo and 26 ahead of Carlos Verona; Thibaut Pinot, who had chased hard, was passed in the final kilometre. Simon Geschke took the polka-dot jersey, and Pogačar kept yellow before the rest day.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 18

Vingegaard wins at Hautacam and all but seals the Tour

Pogačar attacked repeatedly on the Col de Spandelles, crashed on the descent and was waited for by Vingegaard. On Hautacam, Sepp Kuss and Wout van Aert set a fierce pace before Vingegaard went clear about 4 km from the top and won, 1:04 ahead of Pogačar, with van Aert third. Vingegaard's lead grew to 3:26 and he also took the polka-dot jersey from Simon Geschke; Chris Froome had not started after testing positive for COVID-19.
