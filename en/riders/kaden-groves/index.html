In the headlines

Tour 2025 · Stage 20

Groves solos to Pontarlier to complete his Grand Tour stage set

Kaden Groves went clear of what remained of a 13-rider breakaway 16.5 km from the finish and won alone in Pontarlier, 54 seconds ahead of Frank van den Broek. The win completed his set of stage victories in all three Grand Tours. Jordan Jegat, also in the break, moved into tenth overall at Ben O'Connor's expense.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 6

Groves wins in Naples after a crash and neutralisation

The 227 km stage to Naples, the longest of the race, was neutralised between km 156 and 166.5 after a mass crash; Jai Hindley, Juri Hollmann and Dion Smith abandoned. No points or bonus seconds were awarded at the last intermediate sprint and the finish, and the time gaps did not count for the general classification. Kaden Groves won the sprint ahead of Milan Fretin and Paul Magnier.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 5

Groves wins a crash-marred stage in Salerno

On a stage marred by crashes, Kaden Groves won the sprint in Salerno ahead of Jonathan Milan and Mads Pedersen. Andreas Leknessund kept the maglia rosa, with Evenepoel still 28 seconds behind.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 4

Narváez wins in Cosenza, bonus seconds put Ciccone in pink

Back in Italy, Movistar tried to drop the sprinters on Cozzo Tunno for Orluis Aular; Kaden Groves and Arnaud De Lie abandoned. In the reduced sprint, though, Jhonatan Narváez (UAE Team Emirates XRG) beat Aular and Giulio Ciccone. Ciccone took the maglia rosa thanks to the bonus seconds, 4 seconds ahead of Jan Christen, the new maglia bianca, and Florian Stork.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 8

Milan wins in Laval – first Italian Tour stage win since 2019

On a quiet day, Jonathan Milan came around Mathieu van der Poel, who was leading out Kaden Groves, and beat Wout van Aert to win his first Tour stage. He was the first Italian to win a Tour stage since Vincenzo Nibali in 2019, and he regained the green jersey. Tim Merlier was out of contention after a mechanical 12.5 km from the finish.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 14

Asgreen solos to Nova Gorica as a crash hits the favourites

Kasper Asgreen, Mirco Maestri and Martin Marcellusi led for much of a rainy stage before Asgreen went alone 6 km from the finish in Nova Gorica, Slovenia, and won by 16 seconds from Kaden Groves and Olav Kooij. A crash 22 km from the finish brought down or held up Juan Ayuso, Primož Roglič, Antonio Tiberi and Giulio Ciccone, who did not start the next day. Simon Yates moved up to second overall, 1:20 behind del Toro.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 21

Kooij wins in Rome, Yates seals his Giro

The final stage started in the Vatican Gardens, where Pope Leo XIV welcomed the riders, and finished in Rome after eight laps of a local circuit. Olav Kooij won the sprint ahead of Kaden Groves and Matteo Moschetti, and his Visma–Lease a Bike teammate Simon Yates sealed overall victory, 3:56 ahead of Isaac del Toro.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 4

Milan sprints to victory in Andora and takes ciclamino

After a rain-hit stage, the last breakaway riders were caught on the Capo Mele 5 km from the finish; Filippo Ganna attacked a kilometre later and stayed clear until 500 m to go. Jonathan Milan won the bunch sprint ahead of Kaden Groves and Phil Bauhaus and took the maglia ciclamino; Biniam Girmay abandoned.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 11

Milan wins in Francavilla as Merlier is relegated

The three-man breakaway was caught 35 km from the finish, and in the sprint in Francavilla al Mare Jonathan Milan came past Tim Merlier, who was relegated for deviating from his line. That left Kaden Groves second and Giovanni Lonardi third. Cian Uijtdebroeks did not start, and Antonio Tiberi took the young rider lead.
