In the headlines

Tour 2025 · Stage 20

Groves solos to Pontarlier to complete his Grand Tour stage set

Kaden Groves went clear of what remained of a 13-rider breakaway 16.5 km from the finish and won alone in Pontarlier, 54 seconds ahead of Frank van den Broek. The win completed his set of stage victories in all three Grand Tours. Jordan Jegat, also in the break, moved into tenth overall at Ben O'Connor's expense.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 6

Groves wins in Naples after a crash and neutralisation

The 227 km stage to Naples, the longest of the race, was neutralised between km 156 and 166.5 after a mass crash; Jai Hindley, Juri Hollmann and Dion Smith abandoned. No points or bonus seconds were awarded at the last intermediate sprint and the finish, and the time gaps did not count for the general classification. Kaden Groves won the sprint ahead of Milan Fretin and Paul Magnier.

 Vuelta 2024

Groves wins three sprints and the green jersey with 226 points

Kaden Groves (Alpecin–Deceuninck) won stages 2, 14 and 17 and took the points lead after Van Aert's exit, finishing with 226 points. Roglič was second with 140. Twice Van Aert was beaten by him in a sprint, on stages 2 and 14.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 2

Groves wins the bunch sprint, Van Aert takes red through bonus seconds

Kaden Groves won the bunch sprint in Ourém, just ahead of Wout van Aert, who had come up from behind on the line. Thanks to six bonus seconds for second place, Van Aert took the red jersey, 3 seconds ahead of McNulty. Groves became the leader of the points classification.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 14

Groves beats Van Aert in the Villablino sprint

After the Puerto de Leitariegos, where the last breakaway riders were caught, the stage came down to a sprint: Kaden Groves held off Wout van Aert on his wheel to win his second stage, with Corbin Strong third. The general classification did not change: O'Connor led Roglič by 1:21.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 17

Groves wins his third stage in the rain in Santander

A four-man breakaway including Xabier Isasa stayed ahead for a long time but was caught 200 metres from the line in the rain. Kaden Groves, now the points leader, won the sprint ahead of Pavel Bittner and Vito Braet. Roglič remained 5 seconds behind O'Connor in the red jersey.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 5

Groves wins a crash-marred stage in Salerno

On a stage marred by crashes, Kaden Groves won the sprint in Salerno ahead of Jonathan Milan and Mads Pedersen. Andreas Leknessund kept the maglia rosa, with Evenepoel still 28 seconds behind.

 Vuelta 2023 · Stage 21

Groves takes green with stage wins 4, 5 and 21

Kaden Groves won the sprints in Tarragona and Burriana and finished the Vuelta with the win in Madrid. He wore the green jersey from stage 4 to the end and won the points classification with 315 points, 79 ahead of Remco Evenepoel. The other sprint wins went to Geoffrey Soupe (stage 7), Juan Sebastián Molano (12) and Alberto Dainese (19).

 Vuelta 2023 · Stage 12

Molano wins the sprint in Zaragoza ahead of Groves

Abel Balderstone and Jetse Bol made up the break and were caught 44 km from the finish, after which Bol went clear again. In the sprint in Zaragoza Juan Sebastián Molano, led out by Rui Oliveira, won ahead of green jersey Kaden Groves and Boy van Poppel. The overall standings did not change, with Kuss in red ahead of Marc Soler.
