Giro 2025 · Stage 19

Prodhomme wins at Champoluc, del Toro answers Carapaz

Nicolas Prodhomme attacked from the break on the Col de Joux, 28 km from the finish, and won alone in Champoluc. Maglia rosa Isaac del Toro answered every attack from Richard Carapaz, and the pair finished 58 seconds behind the winner and 24 seconds ahead of the other favourites. Before the last mountain stage del Toro led Carapaz by 43 seconds and Simon Yates by 1:21.
