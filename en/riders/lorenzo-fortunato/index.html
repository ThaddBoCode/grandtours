In the headlines

Giro 2025 · Stage 16

Fortunato takes the maglia azzurra and the combativity award

Lorenzo Fortunato (XDS Astana Team) led the mountains classification from stage 3 to Rome and won it with 355 points ahead of teammate Christian Scaroni (201); he was also named the race's most combative rider. On stage 16 the two rode clear together to San Valentino, where Fortunato left the win to Scaroni.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 14

Fortunato conquers the Zoncolan

Lorenzo Fortunato (Eolo–Kometa) bridged across to Jan Tratnik on Monte Zoncolan, dropped him on the steep section and won by 26 seconds for his first professional victory. Egan Bernal followed Simon Yates' attack and then dropped him in the final kilometre to lead Yates by 1:33, while Remco Evenepoel lost more than a minute and a half.

 Giro 2027 · Stage 20

The Zoncolan returns on the penultimate day

Stage 20 finishes on Monte Zoncolan from the Ovaro side – 10.1 km at 11.9% with ramps of 22%. It is the mountain's eighth Giro finish and the first since Lorenzo Fortunato won in 2021; on this side Chris Froome won in 2018.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 3

Pedersen sprints to victory in Vlorë and reclaims the maglia rosa

Lorenzo Fortunato and Pello Bilbao crossed the Llogara pass together in front but were caught 17 km from the finish in Vlorë. Led out by Giulio Ciccone and Mathias Vacek, Mads Pedersen won the sprint ahead of Corbin Strong and Orluis Aular and took back the maglia rosa, 9 seconds ahead of Roglič. Fortunato moved into the maglia azzurra.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 8

Plapp solos to Castelraimondo, Ulissi takes the maglia rosa

Luke Plapp won from the breakaway with a 46 km solo, finishing 38 seconds ahead of Wilco Kelderman and Diego Ulissi. Ulissi took the maglia rosa, 12 seconds ahead of Lorenzo Fortunato, while the group of favourites finished 4:50 behind the winner.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 11

Carapaz attacks late to win at Castelnovo ne' Monti

Lorenzo Fortunato attacked from a large break 103 km from the finish, but the escapees were caught on the Pietra di Bismantova. Richard Carapaz went clear of the maglia rosa group 8 km from the line and won by 10 seconds from del Toro, Giulio Ciccone and Tom Pidcock. Del Toro stayed in pink, 31 seconds ahead of Ayuso.
