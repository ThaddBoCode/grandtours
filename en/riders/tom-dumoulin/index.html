In the headlines

Giro 2022 · Stage 2

Simon Yates wins in Budapest, van der Poel stays in pink

Simon Yates set the best time of 11:50 on the 9.2 km time trial in Budapest, three seconds faster than Mathieu van der Poel, the last rider to start, and five faster than Tom Dumoulin. Van der Poel kept the maglia rosa, while Italian time trial champion Matteo Sobrero took the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 4

Kämna wins on Etna, Juan Pedro López takes pink

Lennard Kämna caught Juan Pedro López 2.5 km from the top of Etna and beat him in the two-man sprint after López misjudged the final corner. López took the maglia rosa and the maglia bianca, Kämna the maglia azzurra; Miguel Ángel López abandoned with a hip injury and Tom Dumoulin lost seven minutes.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 7

Bouwman wins in Potenza and takes the maglia azzurra

A seven-man breakaway including Tom Dumoulin, Bauke Mollema and Davide Formolo fought out the hilly stage, with Koen Bouwman taking maximum points on Monte Sirino, at Viggiano and on La Sellata. Led out by Dumoulin, Bouwman won the sprint ahead of Mollema and Formolo for his first Grand Tour stage win and took the maglia azzurra; the favourites finished together almost three minutes down.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 9

Roglič and Jumbo-Visma rule the race – until the time trial

Primož Roglič took the yellow jersey in Laruns on stage 9, the first Slovenian to wear it, and kept it for eleven stages. Jumbo-Visma controlled the mountains with Wout van Aert, Sepp Kuss, George Bennett and Tom Dumoulin setting the pace, and after the Col de la Loze Roglič led Pogačar by 57 seconds – before losing everything in the final time trial.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 16

Kämna makes up for Puy Mary with a solo win at Villard-de-Lans

A breakaway of more than 20 riders built a lead of over 12 minutes, and Richard Carapaz's repeated accelerations on the climb to Saint-Nizier-du-Moucherotte broke it apart. Lennard Kämna attacked just before the summit and won alone, 1:27 ahead of Carapaz and 1:56 ahead of Sébastien Reichenbach. Apart from Tom Dumoulin and Nairo Quintana, the top ten finished together and the first eight places overall were unchanged.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 20

Pogačar overturns Roglič on La Planche des Belles Filles

Starting 57 seconds behind his compatriot, Tadej Pogačar won the 36.2 km time trial by 1:21 over Tom Dumoulin and Richie Porte, while Primož Roglič finished fifth, 1:56 down. Pogačar took the yellow jersey with a 59-second lead, also claimed the polka-dot jersey from Richard Carapaz and kept the white one; Porte moved up to third overall. It was the first time since 2011 that the yellow jersey changed hands on the eve of the finish in Paris.
