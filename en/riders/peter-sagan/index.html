In the headlines

Giro 2021

Sagan in ciclamino, Bouchard in azzurra

Peter Sagan won stage 10 in Foligno, took the maglia ciclamino and kept it to Milan, finishing with 136 points to Davide Cimolai's 118. Geoffrey Bouchard took the maglia azzurra at Campo Felice and held it to the end by repeatedly taking maximum points on the early climbs.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 10

Sagan wins in Foligno and takes the maglia ciclamino

Bora–Hansgrohe set such a hard pace on the Valico della Somma that Tim Merlier, Giacomo Nizzolo and Dylan Groenewegen were dropped. Peter Sagan launched his sprint from a long way out, held off Fernando Gaviria and Davide Cimolai and took the maglia ciclamino – his first Giro stage win had also come on stage 10, a year earlier.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 21

Bennett beats Sagan to the green jersey

Sam Bennett won his first Tour stage on Île de Ré, took the green jersey that day and kept it to Paris, where he also won on the Champs-Élysées. Peter Sagan, chasing a record eighth points title, was relegated in Poitiers for shouldering Wout van Aert and could not close the gap – it was the first Tour he finished without winning the points classification.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 11

Ewan wins a photo finish in Poitiers, Sagan relegated

After Mathieu Ladagnous's solo breakaway was caught, four riders threw their bikes at the line and Caleb Ewan won his second stage of the Tour. Peter Sagan, initially second, was relegated for shouldering Wout van Aert in the sprint, so Sam Bennett moved up to second and extended his green-jersey lead to 68 points. Ion Izagirre abandoned after a crash 30 km from the finish.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 4

Démare edges Sagan in a photo finish

Arnaud Démare beat Peter Sagan and Davide Ballerini in a photo finish at Villafranca Tirrena after a three-man break was caught 15 km from the end. Sagan took the maglia ciclamino, João Almeida gained two bonus seconds at an intermediate sprint to stay in pink, and Geraint Thomas did not start.

 Tour 2022

Van Aert's record green jersey crowns a Jumbo–Visma sweep

Wout van Aert won the points classification with 480 points, breaking Peter Sagan's modern record, won three stages and was named the Tour's most combative rider. With Vingegaard in yellow and polka dots, it was the first time since Eddy Merckx's Faema team in 1969 that one team won the yellow and green jerseys and the mountains classification.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 3

Merlier wins in Pontivy as crashes hit Roglič and Ewan

On a crash-strewn stage Geraint Thomas dislocated his shoulder, Robert Gesink and Jack Haig abandoned, and Primož Roglič crashed about 10 km from the finish and lost more than a minute. Caleb Ewan went down in the sprint, taking Peter Sagan with him, as Tim Merlier won ahead of his teammate Jasper Philipsen. Mathieu van der Poel stayed in yellow and Richard Carapaz moved up to third.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 12

Politt solos to victory in Nîmes

After crosswinds split the peloton into three groups early on, a 13-rider breakaway went clear and was never chased. Nils Politt made the decisive attack 12 km from the finish and won his first Tour stage, 31 seconds ahead of Imanol Erviti and Harry Sweeny. The peloton finished around 16 minutes down; Peter Sagan had not started because of a knee injury.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 13

Nizzolo finally wins a Grand Tour stage

Giacomo Nizzolo used Fernando Gaviria's slipstream and came past Edoardo Affini, who had attacked late, to win the flat stage in Verona. It was Nizzolo's first Grand Tour stage win after eleven second places, and Peter Sagan was third to keep the maglia ciclamino.
