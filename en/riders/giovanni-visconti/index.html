In the headlines

Giro 2020 · Stage 3

Caicedo wins on Etna as Thomas loses 12 minutes

Jonathan Caicedo won from the breakaway on Etna, 21 seconds ahead of Giovanni Visconti, and took the maglia azzurra. João Almeida moved into the maglia rosa, level on time with Caicedo, while Simon Yates lost more than three minutes and Geraint Thomas, who had crashed before the start, finished about 12 minutes down.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 15

Geoghegan Hart wins at Piancavallo, Almeida holds on

Jai Hindley set the pace on the final climb for his Sunweb leader Wilco Kelderman, and only Tao Geoghegan Hart could follow, going on to win ahead of Kelderman and Hindley. João Almeida lost 37 seconds but kept the maglia rosa, 15 seconds ahead of Kelderman, and Giovanni Visconti took the maglia azzurra.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 16

Tratnik wins in San Daniele del Friuli

A 28-rider break went clear from the start, including mountains rivals Giovanni Visconti and Ruben Guerreiro. Jan Tratnik dropped Ben O'Connor on the climb to the finish and won by seven seconds, while João Almeida attacked in the last few hundred metres to gain two seconds on his rivals.
