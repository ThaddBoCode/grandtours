In the headlines

Tour 2027

Vingegaard aims to return after his crash

The 2022 and 2023 winner broke his collarbone on stage 15 in 2026. His team still plans the Tour as his main goal.

 Giro 2027

Pogačar, Vingegaard, Seixas: who will ride?

Pogačar has named the Tour, the Worlds and the Vuelta as his 2027 goals, though he says he would rather ride the Giro; defending champion Vingegaard's programme is still open. Paul Seixas is considering a Giro debut, and Bahrain have Antonio Tiberi on their Giro programme for now. Several decisions are due at the teams' December training camps.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 15

Vingegaard crashes out with a broken collarbone

Jonas Vingegaard, the Giro d'Italia winner and second overall at the time, hit the kerb exiting a roundabout about 24 km from the finish of stage 15, broke his collarbone and abandoned. He had been woken around 2 am for a doping test authorised by a Paris prosecutor; the night-time tests on him and Pogačar sparked debate, and Pogačar suggested the lost sleep may have contributed to the crash.

 Giro 2026

Vingegaard wins the Giro and completes the Grand Tour set

Jonas Vingegaard won the Giro d'Italia at his first attempt, becoming the eighth rider to win all three Grand Tours and the first to do so since Chris Froome in 2018. The Dane won five stages, four of them in the mountains, took the maglia rosa on stage 14 and finished in Rome 5:22 ahead of Felix Gall and 6:25 ahead of Jai Hindley.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 14

Solo to Pila: Vingegaard takes the maglia rosa

Despite wins at Blockhaus and Corno alle Scale, Vingegaard still trailed Afonso Eulálio until Visma–Lease a Bike set him up on stage 14 to Pila: he won solo and took the maglia rosa with a 2:26 lead. He extended it with further wins at Carì and Piancavallo; Felix Gall, second, took his first Grand Tour podium, with Jai Hindley third.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 7

Vingegaard strikes on the Blockhaus

At the end of the longest stage, 244 km, Jonas Vingegaard (Visma–Lease a Bike) attacked 5 km from the top of the Blockhaus and won solo; only Felix Gall limited his loss to under a minute, at 13 seconds. Afonso Eulálio kept the maglia rosa but lost about half his advantage, now leading Vingegaard – the new maglia azzurra – by 3:17.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 9

Vingegaard counters Gall at Corno alle Scale

Decathlon CMA CGM rode for Felix Gall, but Jonas Vingegaard countered 1 km from the finish and won 12 seconds ahead of the Austrian. Gall still gained time on the other contenders, while Giulio Pellizzari struggled on the final climb. Afonso Eulálio stayed in pink, his lead over Vingegaard down to 2:24.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 16

Swiss showcase: Vingegaard wins at Carì

On the stage from Bellinzona to Carì, held entirely in Ticino, Switzerland, Jonas Vingegaard won solo again after strong work by his team, 1:09 ahead of Felix Gall and 1:11 ahead of Jai Hindley. Gall moved up to second at 4:03, Afonso Eulálio dropped to fifth, and Giulio Pellizzari lost more than ten minutes.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 20

Fifth stage win: Vingegaard triumphs at Piancavallo

The start in Gemona del Friuli commemorated the 1976 earthquake, and the finale climbed twice to Piancavallo. Jonas Vingegaard attacked 10 km from the finish and won his fifth stage, 1:15 ahead of Felix Gall and Jai Hindley, who secured their podium places. He went into the final day with a 5:22 lead.
