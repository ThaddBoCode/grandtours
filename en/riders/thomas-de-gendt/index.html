In the headlines

Giro 2022 · Stage 8

De Gendt outsprints his breakaway companions in Naples

From a 21-man breakaway, Davide Gabburo attacked 42.8 km from the finish and was joined by Thomas De Gendt, his Lotto–Soudal teammate Harm Vanhoucke and Jorge Arcas. Led out by Vanhoucke, De Gendt beat Gabburo and Arcas in the sprint; Guillaume Martin, part of the original break, moved up to fourth overall.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 2

Ulissi wins the uphill finish in Agrigento

Diego Ulissi won a three-man sprint on the uphill finish in Agrigento, beating Peter Sagan and Mikkel Frølich Honoré, and moved into the maglia ciclamino. A five-man break including Thomas De Gendt was caught 9 km from the line; Filippo Ganna kept the maglia rosa and Sagan took the maglia azzurra.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 17

O'Connor wins solo at Madonna di Campiglio

Once again the break decided the stage: Ben O'Connor, second the day before, won alone at Madonna di Campiglio ahead of Hermann Pernsteiner and Thomas De Gendt. The favourites finished together, so the overall standings did not change, and Ruben Guerreiro, in the break, regained the maglia azzurra.
