In the headlines

Giro 2024 · Stage 18

Merlier takes his second sprint win in Padua

Edoardo Affini bridged alone to the four-man breakaway 55 km from the finish, but the move ended 10 km from Padua. Jonathan Milan lost position in the hectic run-in, and Tim Merlier won his second sprint ahead of Milan and Kaden Groves.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 18

De Bondt wins from the breakaway in Treviso

João Almeida, fourth overall, withdrew before the start after testing positive for COVID-19, and Juan Pedro López took back the maglia bianca. The four-man break of Magnus Cort, Edoardo Affini, Davide Gabburo and Dries De Bondt held off the sprinters, and De Bondt beat Affini by half a wheel for his first Grand Tour stage win; Carapaz stayed in pink.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 1

Ganna wins in Turin and opens in pink again

Time trial world champion Filippo Ganna won the 8.6 km opener in Turin in 8:47, 10 seconds faster than Edoardo Affini and 13 faster than Tobias Foss. He took the first maglia rosa for the second year in a row, with João Almeida the best of the favourites in fourth.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 13

Nizzolo finally wins a Grand Tour stage

Giacomo Nizzolo used Fernando Gaviria's slipstream and came past Edoardo Affini, who had attacked late, to win the flat stage in Verona. It was Nizzolo's first Grand Tour stage win after eleven second places, and Peter Sagan was third to keep the maglia ciclamino.
