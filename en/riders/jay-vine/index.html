In the headlines

Giro 2026 · Stage 2

Mass crash in Bulgaria ends Yates' and Buitrago's Giro

On stage 2 to Veliko Tarnovo, around ten riders – several of them GC contenders – went down 23 km from the finish on a slippery road at high speed. Santiago Buitrago and UAE's Jay Vine and Marc Soler abandoned, and podium contender Adam Yates did not start stage 3.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 2

Tarling wins the Tirana time trial, Roglič takes pink by a second

Joshua Tarling won the 13.7 km time trial in Tirana by one second from Primož Roglič, with Jay Vine third at 3 seconds. Roglič took the maglia rosa, one second ahead of Mads Pedersen, and Mathias Vacek moved into the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 10

Cort wins in Viareggio as Thomas takes over the maglia rosa

Nine riders, including Evenepoel, Domenico Pozzovivo, Stefan Küng and Rigoberto Urán, did not start after the rest day, so Geraint Thomas rode in pink, two seconds ahead of Primož Roglič. Magnus Cort beat Derek Gee and Alessandro De Marchi, 51 seconds clear of the bunch, while Aleksandr Vlasov abandoned and Jay Vine crashed and lost more than ten minutes.
