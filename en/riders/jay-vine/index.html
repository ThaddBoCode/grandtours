In the headlines

Vuelta 2022 · Stage 6

Jay Vine wins the first two summit finishes

Jay Vine won the first two summit finishes of the race: Pico Jano on stage 6, his first professional win, and Colláu Fancuaya on stage 8. He led the mountains classification from stage 8 until a crash on stage 18 forced him to abandon, after which Richard Carapaz took the polka-dot jersey.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 2

Mass crash in Bulgaria ends Yates' and Buitrago's Giro

On stage 2 to Veliko Tarnovo, around ten riders – several of them GC contenders – went down 23 km from the finish on a slippery road at high speed. Santiago Buitrago and UAE's Jay Vine and Marc Soler abandoned, and podium contender Adam Yates did not start stage 3.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 2

Tarling wins the Tirana time trial, Roglič takes pink by a second

Joshua Tarling won the 13.7 km time trial in Tirana by one second from Primož Roglič, with Jay Vine third at 3 seconds. Roglič took the maglia rosa, one second ahead of Mads Pedersen, and Mathias Vacek moved into the maglia bianca.

 Vuelta 2025

UAE win seven stages and the team classification

UAE Team Emirates XRG won seven stages: the team time trial (5), Jay Vine on stages 6 and 10, Juan Ayuso on 7 and 12, João Almeida on the Angliru (13) and Marc Soler at La Farrapona (14). They also took the team classification, 23:01 ahead of Visma–Lease a Bike, second place overall for Almeida and the mountains jersey for Vine.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 6

Vine wins at Pal as Træen takes red and Ayuso loses seven minutes

On the first proper mountain stage, finishing at Pal in Andorra, Jay Vine attacked from a ten-man break over the Alto de la Comella and held off Torstein Træen on the final climb. Træen finished about three and a half minutes ahead of the main GC group and took the red jersey, while Juan Ayuso lost seven minutes; Vine moved into the polka-dot jersey and Giulio Pellizzari into the white jersey.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 10

Vine wins solo at Belagua as Vingegaard retakes red

After a fight for the break that lasted more than 100 km, 28 riders got away, and Jay Vine proved strongest on the Puerto de Belagua to win his second stage solo, 35 seconds ahead of Pablo Castrillo. Torstein Træen lost more than a minute among the favourites, and Jonas Vingegaard regained the red jersey with 26 seconds on Træen and 38 on João Almeida.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 15

Pedersen wins from the break in Monforte de Lemos

A group of 47 riders went away after the first climb, and Jay Vine and Louis Vervaeke attacked from it before being caught by a seven-man chase group with 6.8 km to go. Mads Pedersen outsprinted Orluis Aular for his first win of the race, with the peloton crossing the line more than 13 minutes later.

 Vuelta 2025 · Stage 18

Ganna wins the shortened time trial, Vingegaard leads by 40 seconds

The day before, the organisers shortened the individual time trial in Valladolid from 27.2 to 12.2 km because of the threat of protests. Filippo Ganna won in exactly 13:00, with only Jay Vine coming close at one second. Jonas Vingegaard lost ten seconds to João Almeida, who was third on the stage, and led the general classification by 40 seconds.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 16

Van Aert crashes on a wet descent and abandons after three stage wins

Wout van Aert had won stages 3, 7 and 10 and was far ahead in the points classification, with 291 points before he left. On stage 16 he crashed on a slippery descent, suffered a knee injury and abandoned. The green jersey passed to Kaden Groves and the polka-dot jersey to Jay Vine.
