In the headlines

Giro 2024

Pogačar wins his first Giro by 9:56

Tadej Pogačar won the Giro on his first participation, taking six stages and the maglia azzurra of the mountains classification as well. His margin of 9:56 over Daniel Martínez was the biggest since 1965, and his six stage wins put him level with Eddy Merckx (1973) as the post-war overall winner with the most stage victories. Geraint Thomas completed the podium in third.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 2

Puncture, crash, then victory: Pogačar takes pink at Oropa

At the foot of the final climb to the Santuario di Oropa, Pogačar punctured and then crashed, but quickly regained the peloton. He attacked about 4.5 km from the finish, won alone by 27 seconds over Daniel Martínez and Geraint Thomas, and took the maglia rosa, which he kept all the way to Rome.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 7

Pogačar overturns Ganna's lead on the climb to Perugia

Filippo Ganna was fastest at both checkpoints and still 47 seconds up on Tadej Pogačar at the second. On the closing uphill kilometres to Perugia, Pogačar overturned the deficit and won the 40.6 km time trial by 17 seconds. His lead grew to 2:36 over Daniel Martínez, and Luke Plapp took over the young rider lead.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 8

Pogačar wins the favourites' sprint at Prati di Tivo

Valentin Paret-Peintre, the last survivor of the breakaway, was caught by the favourites' group 4.2 km from the finish. Tadej Pogačar attacked with 150 m to go to win ahead of Daniel Martínez and Ben O'Connor, and now led Martínez by 2:40 and Geraint Thomas by 2:58. Cian Uijtdebroeks regained the young rider lead.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 16

Shortened stage: Pogačar takes win number five on Monte Pana

Because of the weather the Umbrail Pass was removed and, after a chaotic start, the stage was cut to 118.7 km starting in Laas. Julian Alaphilippe went solo 33 km from the finish but was caught on the final climb; Tadej Pogačar attacked, passed Giulio Pellizzari 800 m from the line and won by 16 seconds over Pellizzari and Daniel Martínez. Martínez moved back into second overall ahead of Geraint Thomas.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 20

Double Monte Grappa: sixth stage win for Pogačar

On the second ascent of Monte Grappa, Tadej Pogačar attacked 5 km from the summit and 36 km from the finish, caught breakaway rider Giulio Pellizzari and left him behind. After the long descent he won in Bassano del Grappa by 2:07 over Valentin Paret-Peintre, Daniel Martínez and Antonio Tiberi, taking a 9:56 lead into the final stage.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 20

Caruso wins at Alpe Motta, Bernal stays safe

Damiano Caruso joined a Team DSM move on the descent of the San Bernardino with teammate Pello Bilbao, dropped Romain Bardet on the final climb and won alone at Alpe Motta for his first Grand Tour stage win. Egan Bernal, paced by Daniel Martínez, finished 24 seconds down and went into the final time trial 1:59 ahead of Caruso.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 13

Martínez wins on Puy Mary as the Slovenians break clear

From a 17-rider breakaway, Daniel Martínez beat Lennard Kämna by 4 seconds on the steep climb to Puy Mary, with Maximilian Schachmann third. Behind, Tadej Pogačar and Primož Roglič broke clear of the favourites on the Pas de Peyrol; Pogačar moved up to second overall, 44 seconds down, and retook the white jersey, while Egan Bernal lost 38 seconds and fell to third. Bauke Mollema abandoned after a crash, and Romain Bardet, concussed in a fall, did not start the next day.
