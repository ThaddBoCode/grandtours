In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 16

Lipowitz crashes out in the time trial

Florian Lipowitz crashed on a descent in the stage 16 time trial to Thonon-les-Bains, broke his collarbone and had to abandon. The stage went to Remco Evenepoel, a day after his first road-stage win at the Tour at Solaison; he went on to finish second overall.

 Tour 2025

Lipowitz becomes the first German on the podium since 2006

Florian Lipowitz finished third, 11:00 behind Pogačar, and won the white jersey – the first German on the Tour podium since Andreas Klöden in 2006. He moved up to third when Evenepoel abandoned on stage 14, saw Oscar Onley close to within 22 seconds at the Col de la Loze, then pulled clear again at La Plagne.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 15

Vingegaard crashes out, Evenepoel wins at Solaison

After night-time doping tests on Vingegaard (around 2 am) and Pogačar (around 5 am), Vingegaard hit the kerb exiting a roundabout about 24 km from the finish, broke his collarbone and abandoned; Tim Merlier also left the race. On the first-ever finish at Plateau de Solaison, Pogačar and del Toro dropped Seixas and Lipowitz, but Evenepoel stayed with them and beat Pogačar in the sprint for his first road-stage win at the Tour. Evenepoel moved up to second overall, five minutes down, and del Toro regained the white jersey.

 Tour 2025

Pogačar wins his fourth Tour and the polka-dot jersey

Tadej Pogačar won the Tour for the fourth time, 4:24 ahead of Jonas Vingegaard and 11:00 ahead of Florian Lipowitz, and also took the mountains classification. He won four stages – Rouen, Mûr-de-Bretagne, Hautacam and the Peyragudes mountain time trial – and answered every attack Vingegaard launched in the final week.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 12

Pogačar dominates on Hautacam and retakes yellow

After a minute's silence for the under-23 rider Samuele Privitera, who had died after a crash the previous day, UAE set a hard pace on Hautacam and Tadej Pogačar attacked 12 km from the top. He won 2:10 ahead of Jonas Vingegaard and 2:23 ahead of Florian Lipowitz; Ben Healy lost 13 minutes, and Pogačar took back the yellow jersey with a 3:31 lead over Vingegaard.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 13

Pogačar wins the Peyragudes mountain time trial

Tadej Pogačar was fastest at both time checks and climbed the 10.9 km to the Peyragudes altiport in exactly 23 minutes, 36 seconds faster than Jonas Vingegaard, with Primož Roglič third. It was his 21st Tour stage win, and his lead grew to 4:07. Remco Evenepoel, caught by Vingegaard near the finish, kept third overall by only 6 seconds over Florian Lipowitz.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 14

Evenepoel quits on the Tourmalet, Arensman wins at Superbagnères

Third-placed Remco Evenepoel was dropped early on the Tourmalet and abandoned, while Mattias Skjelmose also left after a crash. Thymen Arensman attacked from the break on the Peyresourde, 37 km from the finish, and won his first Tour stage at Superbagnères, 1:08 ahead of Tadej Pogačar, who gained 4 seconds on Vingegaard in the final metres. Florian Lipowitz moved up to third overall and took the white jersey.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 15

Wellens solos to Carcassonne for his first Tour stage

A crash early in the stage briefly caught out Jonas Vingegaard and Florian Lipowitz before the peloton regrouped. Tim Wellens attacked from the lead group 43.5 km from the finish and won alone in Carcassonne, 1:28 ahead of Victor Campenaerts, completing stage wins in all three Grand Tours. The peloton finished more than six minutes down, with no change at the top of the overall standings.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 18

O'Connor wins solo on the Col de la Loze, Pogačar drops Vingegaard

On the queen stage over the Glandon and the Madeleine, Ben O'Connor attacked in the valley before the Col de la Loze and won alone, 1:45 ahead of Tadej Pogačar, who dropped Jonas Vingegaard inside the final kilometre. Pogačar's lead grew to 4:26, while Oscar Onley gained more than a minute and a half on Florian Lipowitz to close within 22 seconds of third place. Lenny Martinez was docked 8 mountains points for taking sticky bottles.
