In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 4

Pedersen wins from the break, Træen inherits yellow

In temperatures of around 40 °C, which led the UCI to relax the rules on handing up water, a breakaway of around 35 riders was allowed to go clear. Lidl–Trek controlled the attacks in the finale and Mads Pedersen won the sprint ahead of teammate Quinn Simmons and Raúl García Pierna, taking the green jersey. The peloton finished almost 13 minutes down, so Torstein Træen became the first Uno-X Mobility rider to wear yellow, 28 seconds ahead of Sean Quinn, with Pogačar fourth at 7:53.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 5

Kooij beats the favourites in Pau

Baptiste Veistroffer spent most of the day alone at the front before being caught about 14 km from the finish. After a crash 6 km from the line, a group of around 20 riders contested the sprint, and Olav Kooij beat Max Kanter and Tim Merlier for his first Tour stage win. Træen kept the yellow jersey, while Pedersen extended his points lead by finishing seventh.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 6

Pogačar goes solo on the Tourmalet and takes yellow

On the first high-mountain stage, del Toro attacked 5 km from the top of the Tourmalet and Pogačar went clear alone 43 km from the finish, crossing the summit 31 seconds ahead of Vingegaard and extending his lead on the descent. He won at Gavarnie-Gèdre by 2:38 over Vingegaard – his largest winning margin at the Tour – with the other contenders a further 19 seconds back. Pogačar took the yellow and polka-dot jerseys and del Toro the white, while yellow jersey Torstein Træen crashed on the descent and left the race with concussion and rib fractures.
