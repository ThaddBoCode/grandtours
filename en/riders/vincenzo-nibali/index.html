In the headlines

Tour 2025

Milan wins green and ends Italy's wait for a stage win

Jonathan Milan won the points classification with 372 points, ahead of Pogačar and Biniam Girmay, after winning in Laval and Valence and scoring consistently in the intermediate sprints. His stage 8 win was the first Tour stage won by an Italian since Vincenzo Nibali in 2019.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 8

Milan wins in Laval – first Italian Tour stage win since 2019

On a quiet day, Jonathan Milan came around Mathieu van der Poel, who was leading out Kaden Groves, and beat Wout van Aert to win his first Tour stage. He was the first Italian to win a Tour stage since Vincenzo Nibali in 2019, and he regained the green jersey. Tim Merlier was out of contention after a mechanical 12.5 km from the finish.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 18

Stelvio shake-up: Kelderman in pink, Hindley wins

On the queen stage over the Stelvio, the Cima Coppi, Rohan Dennis set the pace for Tao Geoghegan Hart and distanced João Almeida, Vincenzo Nibali and the other favourites. Jai Hindley won at Laghi di Cancano ahead of Geoghegan Hart, while his Sunweb teammate Wilco Kelderman took the maglia rosa, 12 seconds ahead of Hindley and 15 ahead of Geoghegan Hart.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 6

Démare again, this time in Matera

After the four-man break was caught 15 km from the finish, attacks by Vincenzo Nibali and Peter Sagan on the gentle rise to the line in Matera were shut down and Arnaud Démare sprinted to his second win, ahead of Michael Matthews and Fabio Felline. Démare took the maglia ciclamino, and João Almeida stayed in pink.
