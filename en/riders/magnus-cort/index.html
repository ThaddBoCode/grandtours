In the headlines

Vuelta 2026 · Stage 10

Tronchon surprises the sprinters in Elche de la Sierra

Bastien Tronchon (Groupama–FDJ United) won a frantic finale in Elche de la Sierra in 4:07:41 ahead of Magnus Cort and Thibau Nys. Wout van Aert was fourth despite the heavy work of Visma–Lease a Bike. Mas kept the red jersey, with Roglič still 1:18 behind.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 17

Brennan sprints to a fifth win in Seville

Matthew Brennan won the sprint in Seville in 4:07:15 ahead of Jordi Meeus and Magnus Cort. It was his fifth stage win, after stages 2, 5, 11 and 16, more than any other rider in this Vuelta. The general classification was unchanged, with Mas ahead of Gall and Roglič.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 10

Cort wins in Viareggio as Thomas takes over the maglia rosa

Nine riders, including Evenepoel, Domenico Pozzovivo, Stefan Küng and Rigoberto Urán, did not start after the rest day, so Geraint Thomas rode in pink, two seconds ahead of Primož Roglič. Magnus Cort beat Derek Gee and Alessandro De Marchi, 51 seconds clear of the bunch, while Aleksandr Vlasov abandoned and Jay Vine crashed and lost more than ten minutes.

 Tour 2022

COVID-19 and abandons: the fewest finishers since 2000

In total, 17 riders had to leave the race because of COVID-19, among them stage winners Magnus Cort and Simon Clarke and former winner Chris Froome. Only 135 of the 176 starters reached Paris, the fewest finishers since 2000, and both Vingegaard's and Pogačar's teams were badly depleted by the end.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 1

Copenhagen's Grand Départ and Cort's mountain record

The Tour started in Copenhagen for the first time, with three stages in Denmark, and Yves Lampaert surprised the favourites to win the opening time trial and the first yellow jersey. Home rider Magnus Cort took all the mountain points on offer in Denmark and on stage 4 broke Federico Bahamontes' record for the most consecutive summits won.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 2

Jakobsen wins in Nyborg, van Aert takes yellow on bonus seconds

After the crossing of the Great Belt Bridge, where race leader Lampaert was caught up in a crash, Fabio Jakobsen won a reduced bunch sprint ahead of van Aert and Mads Pedersen for his first Tour stage win. Van Aert took the yellow and green jerseys thanks to six bonus seconds, while Magnus Cort led over all three climbs to take the polka-dot jersey.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 3

Groenewegen pips van Aert, second for the third day in a row

Magnus Cort spent most of the day alone in the breakaway and again led over all three climbs. Van Aert opened the sprint in Sønderborg, but Dylan Groenewegen came past him on the line for his first WorldTour win in over two years, with Jasper Philipsen third. Van Aert kept yellow, now 7 seconds ahead of Lampaert, before the transfer to France.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 4

Van Aert attacks in yellow and wins alone in Calais

Jumbo–Visma blew the race apart on the Côte du Cap Blanc-Nez, and van Aert went clear near the top to solo to victory, 8 seconds ahead of the peloton led home by Jasper Philipsen, who celebrated thinking he had won. Earlier Magnus Cort led over the first five climbs, breaking Federico Bahamontes' record for consecutive summits won. Van Aert's lead grew to 25 seconds over Lampaert.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 10

Cort wins a photo finish in Megève, Kämna comes close to yellow

Climate protesters on the road forced the race to stop, after which it was restarted with the previous gaps. From a 25-rider breakaway, Magnus Cort beat Nick Schultz in a photo finish at the Megève altiport. Lennard Kämna, who was also in the move, rose to second overall, just 11 seconds behind Pogačar.
