In the headlines

Giro 2022

Démare rules the sprints and wins the maglia ciclamino

Arnaud Démare was the race's best sprinter, winning in Messina, Scalea and Cuneo, and took the points classification with 254 points ahead of Fernando Gaviria and Mark Cavendish. He led the classification from stage 5 all the way to Verona.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 5

Démare wins in Messina and takes the maglia ciclamino

Alpecin–Fenix raised the pace on the Portella Mandrazzi, dropping Caleb Ewan and Mark Cavendish for good, while Arnaud Démare fought his way back after the descent. Démare then beat Fernando Gaviria and Giacomo Nizzolo in the sprint – his first Grand Tour stage win in two years – and took the maglia ciclamino.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 6

Démare pips Ewan with a bike throw in Scalea

Diego Rosa rode alone in front for around 140 km before being caught 27.6 km from the finish. Arnaud Démare came out of Caleb Ewan's slipstream and beat him with a bike throw for his second consecutive stage win, with Mark Cavendish third; Juan Pedro López kept the maglia rosa.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 13

Démare takes his third win in Cuneo as Bardet abandons

Romain Bardet, fourth overall, abandoned with stomach problems before the Colle di Nava. The five-man break was only caught inside the final kilometre, and Arnaud Démare held off Phil Bauhaus and Mark Cavendish for his third stage win; Juan Pedro López extended his run in pink to ten days.

 Giro 2020

Démare's four sprint wins and the maglia ciclamino

Arnaud Démare won four bunch sprints – at Villafranca Tirrena, Matera, Brindisi and Rimini – and took the maglia ciclamino with 233 points to Peter Sagan's 184. Sagan, second to Démare in three of those sprints, finally won his first Giro stage in Tortoreto, while the maglia azzurra went to Ruben Guerreiro.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 4

Démare edges Sagan in a photo finish

Arnaud Démare beat Peter Sagan and Davide Ballerini in a photo finish at Villafranca Tirrena after a three-man break was caught 15 km from the end. Sagan took the maglia ciclamino, João Almeida gained two bonus seconds at an intermediate sprint to stay in pink, and Geraint Thomas did not start.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 6

Démare again, this time in Matera

After the four-man break was caught 15 km from the finish, attacks by Vincenzo Nibali and Peter Sagan on the gentle rise to the line in Matera were shut down and Arnaud Démare sprinted to his second win, ahead of Michael Matthews and Fabio Felline. Démare took the maglia ciclamino, and João Almeida stayed in pink.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 7

Démare's hat-trick on the fastest stage in Giro history

Arnaud Démare won again in Brindisi, with Peter Sagan second for the third time and Michael Matthews third. Pushed along by a strong wind, the short stage was ridden at 51.234 km/h, the highest average speed for a stage in Giro history, and all four jerseys stayed put.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 11

Démare makes it four in Rimini

Arnaud Démare took his fourth stage win in Rimini, once again ahead of Peter Sagan, after the break was caught 6 km from the finish. Elia Viviani was involved in a crash with a motorbike, and all the jerseys stayed unchanged.
