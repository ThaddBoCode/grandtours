In the headlines

Tour 2023 · Stage 2

Lafay strikes under the flamme rouge in San Sebastián

Richard Carapaz did not start after his crash. On the Jaizkibel Pogačar took the 8 bonus seconds ahead of Vingegaard, and in the finale Victor Lafay attacked under the flamme rouge and held off the group led by Wout van Aert – Cofidis' first Tour stage win since 2008. Adam Yates stayed in yellow, six seconds ahead of Pogačar, and Lafay took green.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 8

Lafay wins solo at Guardia Sanframondi

Victor Lafay attacked from the break on the final climb and won alone, his first professional victory and Cofidis' first Giro stage win since 2010. Points leader Caleb Ewan abandoned during the stage, so Tim Merlier took back the maglia ciclamino, while Attila Valter stayed in pink.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 4

Philipsen doubles up on the Nogaro motor circuit

On a slow stage with no breakaway until around 100 km in, Philipsen won at the Circuit Paul Armagnac by a quarter of a wheel from Caleb Ewan. Crashes in the finale brought down Fabio Jakobsen among others, and Mathieu van der Poel was relegated for impeding Biniam Girmay. Philipsen took the green jersey from Victor Lafay.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 12

Ion Izagirre solos to Belleville-en-Beaujolais

Fabio Jakobsen did not start and David de la Cruz crashed out. After a fierce fight for the breakaway, Ion Izagirre went clear on the Col de la Croix Rosier and rode the last 30 km or so alone, winning by 58 seconds from Mathieu Burgaudeau and Matteo Jorgenson. It was Cofidis' second stage win of the Tour after Lafay's.
