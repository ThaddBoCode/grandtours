In the headlines

Giro 2021 · Stage 20

From domestique to runner-up: Caruso's Giro

Damiano Caruso started the Giro as a domestique for Mikel Landa and became Bahrain Victorious' leader when Landa crashed out on stage 5. The Italian was consistently among the best in the mountains, won stage 20 at Alpe Motta for his first Grand Tour stage win and finished second overall.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 17

Valgren outfoxes his breakaway companions in Andalo

Michael Valgren (EF Education–EasyPost) caught out his breakaway companions in the final kilometre and won in Andalo 3 seconds ahead of Andreas Leknessund and 6 ahead of Damiano Caruso. Jhonatan Narváez was stuck in a chasing group but regained the maglia ciclamino thanks to the intermediate sprint; nothing changed at the top of the overall standings.

 Giro 2021

Bernal wins the Giro, the second Colombian to do so

Egan Bernal won the Giro by 1:29 over Damiano Caruso, with Simon Yates third at 4:15 – his second Grand Tour title after the 2019 Tour de France. He became the second Colombian winner after Nairo Quintana in 2014, held the maglia rosa from stage 9 to Milan and also won the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 16

Shortened queen stage: Bernal solo over the Giau

Heavy rain and snow forced the organisers to remove the Passo Fedaia and the Passo Pordoi from the queen stage to Cortina d'Ampezzo, making the Giau the Cima Coppi. Bernal attacked on the Giau, crossed the summit alone and won by 27 seconds over Romain Bardet and Damiano Caruso, extending his lead to 2:24, while Remco Evenepoel lost more than 24 minutes.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 17

Dan Martin completes his set at Sega di Ala

Dan Martin attacked from the break on the final climb and held on to win at Sega di Ala, completing stage wins in all three Grand Tours. João Almeida and Simon Yates were second and third, while Egan Bernal had his first bad day but still led Damiano Caruso by 2:21; a crash in the peloton involved Remco Evenepoel, who did not start the next day.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 19

Yates wins at Alpe di Mera

With the Mottarone removed out of respect for the victims of the cable car disaster, Simon Yates attacked on the final climb and won alone at Alpe di Mera, 11 seconds ahead of João Almeida. Egan Bernal limited his losses to 28 seconds and led Damiano Caruso by 2:29, with Yates 20 seconds further back in third.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 21

Ganna wins in Milan, Bernal wins the Giro

Filippo Ganna won the 30.3 km time trial from Senago to Milan despite a puncture, 12 seconds ahead of Rémi Cavagna, who crashed into the barriers in the final kilometre. Egan Bernal rode safely to the finish to win the Giro by 1:29 over Damiano Caruso, with Simon Yates third, while Peter Sagan and Geoffrey Bouchard confirmed the maglia ciclamino and the maglia azzurra.
