In the headlines

Giro 2022 · Stage 11

Dainese wins in Reggio Emilia, the first Italian stage win

On a pan-flat stage, Dries De Bondt's solo attack was caught 1.3 km from the line. Alberto Dainese came round Fernando Gaviria and Simone Consonni to win his first Grand Tour stage, the first by an Italian at this Giro, while Richard Carapaz took three bonus seconds and moved up to second overall.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 14

Kragh Andersen escapes in the finale in Lyon

Bora–Hansgrohe drove the pace on the Col du Béal to drop Sam Bennett and other sprinters. In a finale full of attacks, Søren Kragh Andersen went clear 3 km from the finish and won alone by 15 seconds; Luka Mezgec won the sprint for second ahead of Simone Consonni and Peter Sagan. Roglič kept yellow and Bennett the green jersey.
