In the headlines

Tour 2020 · Stage 8

Peters wins in Loudenvielle as Pinot's hopes fade

Nans Peters dropped Ilnur Zakarin on the descent of the Port de Balès and held on over the Peyresourde to win from a 13-man break, 47 seconds ahead of Toms Skujiņš and Carlos Verona. Behind, Tadej Pogačar attacked on the Peyresourde and gained around 40 seconds on the yellow-jersey group. Thibaut Pinot, suffering from back pain, lost almost 19 minutes, while Adam Yates kept yellow by 3 seconds.
