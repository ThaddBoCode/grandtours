In the headlines

Vuelta 2024 · Stage 15

ProTeam Kern Pharma wins three stages, two of them by Castrillo

Pablo Castrillo (Equipo Kern Pharma) won stage 12 at Manzaneda and stage 15 at Valgrande-Pajares, each time from a breakaway, and Urko Berrade added a third win for the Spanish ProTeam on stage 18. On the Cuitu Negru Castrillo held off Aleksandr Vlasov by 12 seconds.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 11

Geoghegan Hart breaks his hip in a crash on a wet descent

A crash on a wet descent late in stage 11 brought down Thomas, Roglič, Pavel Sivakov and Tao Geoghegan Hart. Thomas and Roglič rejoined the peloton, but Geoghegan Hart, third overall, abandoned with a broken hip and Sivakov lost 13 minutes; the day before, Aleksandr Vlasov, also in the top ten, had abandoned midway through stage 10.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 10

Cort wins in Viareggio as Thomas takes over the maglia rosa

Nine riders, including Evenepoel, Domenico Pozzovivo, Stefan Küng and Rigoberto Urán, did not start after the rest day, so Geraint Thomas rode in pink, two seconds ahead of Primož Roglič. Magnus Cort beat Derek Gee and Alessandro De Marchi, 51 seconds clear of the bunch, while Aleksandr Vlasov abandoned and Jay Vine crashed and lost more than ten minutes.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 9

Bernal takes pink on the gravel of Campo Felice

Stage 9 finished on gravel roads at Campo Felice. Bernal countered an attack by Aleksandr Vlasov, then dropped Giulio Ciccone and won alone, taking the maglia rosa 15 seconds ahead of Remco Evenepoel.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 11

Schmid wins on the gravel to Montalcino

On a stage with four gravel sectors, Mauro Schmid beat Alessandro Covi in a two-up sprint for his first professional win. Behind them Egan Bernal attacked to finish ahead of all the favourites, while Remco Evenepoel struggled on the gravel and lost about two minutes; Aleksandr Vlasov rose to second overall at 45 seconds.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 12

Carthy wins on the Angliru, Carapaz back in red

On the 109.4 km stage finishing atop the Alto de L'Angliru, Hugh Carthy beat Aleksandr Vlasov, Enric Mas and Richard Carapaz (all +0:16). Roglič finished 26 seconds behind Carthy, Carapaz took the red jersey with a 10-second lead, and Carthy rose to third overall.
