In the headlines

Giro 2025 · Stage 16

Pre-race favourites Roglič and Ayuso both abandon

The two pre-race favourites did not reach Rome. Primož Roglič, the 2023 winner, lost time after crashing and puncturing on the gravel to Siena, was caught up in the crash on stage 14 and abandoned on stage 16. Juan Ayuso, winner at Tagliacozzo and second overall for much of the race, was dropped on stage 16 and withdrew on stage 18 after a bee sting.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 2

Tarling wins the Tirana time trial, Roglič takes pink by a second

Joshua Tarling won the 13.7 km time trial in Tirana by one second from Primož Roglič, with Jay Vine third at 3 seconds. Roglič took the maglia rosa, one second ahead of Mads Pedersen, and Mathias Vacek moved into the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 7

Ayuso wins at Tagliacozzo, Roglič back in pink

On the first big mountain stage Pedersen was dropped early on the final climb to Tagliacozzo. Juan Ayuso went clear 500 m from the finish and won by 4 seconds from his teammate Isaac del Toro, Egan Bernal and Primož Roglič. Roglič took back the maglia rosa, 4 seconds ahead of Ayuso, who also took the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 15

Verona wins alone in Asiago, Roglič loses time

Carlos Verona attacked from the break on the climb of Dori, 43 km from the finish, and won alone in Asiago, 22 seconds ahead of Florian Stork and 23 ahead of Christian Scaroni. Primož Roglič finished almost two minutes behind the winner and dropped to tenth overall, 3:53 down. Del Toro kept the maglia rosa, 1:20 ahead of Simon Yates.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 12

Crashes end the Tour for Roglič and Pedersen

Primož Roglič, one of the pre-race 'Big Four', crashed on stage 11 and again about 12 km from the finish of stage 12, losing almost two and a half minutes; he did not start the next day. Mads Pedersen, who crashed in the stage 5 sprint, did not start stage 8. In all, 141 of the 176 starters reached Nice.

 Giro 2023

Roglič becomes the first Slovenian to win the Giro

Primož Roglič won the 106th Giro d'Italia by 14 seconds over Geraint Thomas, with João Almeida third at 1:15 – his fourth Grand Tour victory and the first Giro win by a Slovenian. He only took the maglia rosa on the penultimate day.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 20

Roglič overturns Thomas in the Monte Lussari time trial

Geraint Thomas led by 26 seconds before the 18.6 km mountain time trial from Tarvisio to Monte Lussari. Roglič won it by 40 seconds over Thomas and 42 over Almeida, turning his deficit into a 14-second lead that he held to Rome.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 5

Chaos on the cobbles: Roglič dislocates his shoulder

Stage 5 brought cobbles back to the Tour for the first time since 2018. Vingegaard needed three bike changes after a mechanical and Roglič crashed, dislocated his shoulder and lost more than two minutes, while Pogačar attacked with Jasper Stuyven and gained time on all his rivals. Roglič did not start stage 15, the day Steven Kruijswijk also crashed out.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 3

Crashes end Roglič's Tour

Pre-race favourite Primož Roglič crashed about 10 km from the finish of stage 3, a day of crashes on which Jack Haig and Robert Gesink abandoned, Geraint Thomas dislocated his shoulder and Caleb Ewan broke his collarbone. The peloton stopped for a minute at the start of stage 4 in protest at the dangerous run-in. Roglič lost more than 30 minutes on stage 8 and did not start stage 9.
