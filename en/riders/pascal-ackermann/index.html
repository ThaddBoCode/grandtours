In the headlines

Giro 2023 · Stage 11

Ackermann wins photo finish in Tortona, Geoghegan Hart crashes out

Four Soudal–Quick-Step riders had tested positive for COVID-19 before the stage. Late on, a crash on a wet descent brought down Thomas, Roglič, Pavel Sivakov and Tao Geoghegan Hart; Geoghegan Hart, third overall, abandoned with a broken hip and Sivakov lost 13 minutes. Pascal Ackermann was awarded the photo-finish sprint ahead of Jonathan Milan and Mark Cavendish.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 9

Bennett relegated, Ackermann promoted to the stage 9 win

Sam Bennett (Deceuninck–Quick-Step) first won the sprint in Aguilar de Campoo but was relegated by the race jury for aggressively shoulder-barging Emīls Liepiņš in the run-in. Pascal Ackermann (Bora–Hansgrohe) was promoted to the winner of the stage.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 18

Ackermann wins in Madrid, Roglič seals the overall

Pascal Ackermann (Bora–Hansgrohe) won the final sprint in Madrid ahead of Sam Bennett and Max Kanter for his second stage win. Primož Roglič finished the Vuelta as overall winner in 72:46:12, ahead of Richard Carapaz (+0:24) and Hugh Carthy (+1:15), and also won the points classification.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 10

Philipsen finally gets his win in Saint-Amand-Montrond

After a quiet stage from Orléans, Jasper Philipsen, led out by Mathieu van der Poel, won the bunch sprint for his first stage win of this Tour. Green jersey Biniam Girmay was second and Pascal Ackermann third. There were no changes at the top of the classifications.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 12

Girmay's third win as a crash ends Roglič's Tour

Green jersey Biniam Girmay won his third stage in Villeneuve-sur-Lot ahead of Wout van Aert and Pascal Ackermann, while Arnaud Démare and Mark Cavendish were relegated for irregular sprinting. A crash about 12 km from the finish brought down Primož Roglič, who lost almost two and a half minutes and dropped to sixth overall, 4:42 behind. Roglič did not start the next day.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 13

Philipsen wins a contested sprint in Pau

With Primož Roglič a non-starter and Juan Ayuso abandoning during the stage, crosswinds again briefly split the peloton before the race came back together for a sprint in Pau. Jasper Philipsen won ahead of Wout van Aert, Pascal Ackermann and Biniam Girmay, after a heavy crash 700 m from the line had held up many riders. Rivals criticised Philipsen's sprint, and Ackermann refused to shake his hand.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 6

Pedersen wins in Naples as the break is caught at the last gasp

On the stage starting and finishing in Naples the breakaway was caught at the last gasp, and Mads Pedersen won the sprint ahead of Jonathan Milan and Pascal Ackermann. Leknessund stayed in pink.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 13

Pedersen wins in Montilla

Mads Pedersen passed Pascal Ackermann, who had launched an early sprint on the uphill drag to Montilla, and took his first stage win of this Vuelta, ahead of Bryan Coquard and Ackermann. Juan Ayuso had tested positive for COVID-19 before the stage but stayed in the race after a PCR test showed a low risk of infectivity. Remco Evenepoel kept the red jersey.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 16

Pedersen wins in Tomares as Roglič crashes

In a five-man sprint, Mads Pedersen won ahead of Pascal Ackermann and Danny van Poppel, but Primož Roglič collided with Fred Wright and crashed heavily, finishing with cuts on his right side. The peloton came in eight seconds later, with Remco Evenepoel credited with its time after a mechanical problem inside the last 3 km. Roglič was given the lead group's time and stayed second overall, 1:26 behind.
