In the headlines

Tour 2024 · Stage 5

Cavendish takes his 35th Tour stage win and the record

On the flat run to Saint-Vulbas, Mark Cavendish won the sprint ahead of Jasper Philipsen and Alexander Kristoff for his 35th Tour de France stage win, breaking the record he had shared with Eddy Merckx. Mads Pedersen crashed in the final metres, and Phil Bauhaus was relegated for a dangerous change of line. Biniam Girmay took the green jersey, the first African rider to wear it.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 16

Philipsen's third win in Nîmes as Girmay crashes

Thomas Gachignard rode alone at the front for much of the second half but was caught 25 km from Nîmes. Green jersey Biniam Girmay crashed 1.5 km from the finish, and Jasper Philipsen, led out by Robbe Ghys and Mathieu van der Poel, won ahead of Phil Bauhaus and Alexander Kristoff. It was Philipsen's third stage win, matching Girmay's tally.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 4

Milan sprints to victory in Andora and takes ciclamino

After a rain-hit stage, the last breakaway riders were caught on the Capo Mele 5 km from the finish; Filippo Ganna attacked a kilometre later and stayed clear until 500 m to go. Jonathan Milan won the bunch sprint ahead of Kaden Groves and Phil Bauhaus and took the maglia ciclamino; Biniam Girmay abandoned.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 13

Milan claims his third stage win in Cento

On a flat stage with some crosswind splits, the three Italian breakaway riders were caught 53 km from the finish. The peloton stayed together to Cento, where Jonathan Milan, in the maglia ciclamino, won ahead of Stanisław Aniołkowski and Phil Bauhaus.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 3

Philipsen opens his account in Bayonne

After a two-man breakaway by Neilson Powless and Laurent Pichon, Jasper Philipsen won the sprint into Bayonne ahead of Phil Bauhaus and Caleb Ewan following a strong lead-out by Mathieu van der Poel. The commissaires reviewed his sprint duel with Wout van Aert but let the result stand. Adam Yates kept yellow and Powless added to his mountains lead.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 17

Gall wins at Courchevel as Pogačar cracks

On the queen stage Felix Gall attacked 6.5 km from the top of the Col de la Loze and won alone at Courchevel on his Tour debut, 34 seconds ahead of Simon Yates. Pogačar was dropped on the Loze and lost heavily; Vingegaard, fourth on the stage, now led by 7:35. Phil Bauhaus abandoned.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 13

Démare takes his third win in Cuneo as Bardet abandons

Romain Bardet, fourth overall, abandoned with stomach problems before the Colle di Nava. The five-man break was only caught inside the final kilometre, and Arnaud Démare held off Phil Bauhaus and Mark Cavendish for his third stage win; Juan Pedro López extended his run in pink to ten days.
