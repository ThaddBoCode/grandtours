In the headlines

Vuelta 2022 · Stage 6

Pico Jano: Evenepoel takes the red jersey

In the rain at Pico Jano, Jay Vine caught the breakaway rider Mark Padun and won the stage. Remco Evenepoel dropped everyone except Enric Mas about 8 km from the summit and finished second, 15 seconds behind Vine; Primož Roglič lost 1:28 to him once bonus seconds are counted. Evenepoel became the sixth leader in six days, 21 seconds ahead of Rudy Molard.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 12

Narváez wins the Nove Colli stage in Cesenatico

On Marco Pantani's home roads, over a route modelled on the Nove Colli gran fondo, a 14-rider break stayed clear. Jhonatan Narváez won alone in Cesenatico, 1:08 ahead of Mark Padun, who had been slowed by a mechanical problem; the maglia rosa group finished about eight minutes down with no change among the leaders.
