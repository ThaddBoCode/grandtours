In the headlines

Giro 2026 · Stage 2

Mass crash in Bulgaria ends Yates' and Buitrago's Giro

On stage 2 to Veliko Tarnovo, around ten riders – several of them GC contenders – went down 23 km from the finish on a slippery road at high speed. Santiago Buitrago and UAE's Jay Vine and Marc Soler abandoned, and podium contender Adam Yates did not start stage 3.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 3

Magnier again: sprint victory in Sofia

The last Bulgarian stage crossed the Borovets Pass, its only climb, on the way to the capital. Paul Magnier won his second sprint ahead of Jonathan Milan and Dylan Groenewegen, strengthening his hold on the maglia ciclamino, while Guillermo Thomas Silva stayed in pink. Adam Yates and Andrea Vendrame did not start after the previous day's crash.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 14

Pogačar strikes at Pla d'Adet, Vingegaard moves up to second

After the Tourmalet and the Hourquette d'Ancizan, Tadej Pogačar attacked just under 5 km from the summit finish at Pla d'Adet, passing Ben Healy and his teammate Adam Yates. He won 39 seconds ahead of Jonas Vingegaard, with Remco Evenepoel a further 31 seconds back. Vingegaard moved up to second overall at 1:57, while Evenepoel dropped to third at 2:22.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 9

Yates wins solo in Granada, O'Connor saves red

Adam Yates won in Granada after a solo move from the breakaway, 1:39 ahead of Richard Carapaz. Ben O'Connor took the sprint of a ten-rider group, 3:45 behind Yates, and stayed in red ahead of Roglič (3:53); Carapaz moved up to third overall. Yates took the polka-dot jersey and Florian Lipowitz the white.

 Tour 2023

Vingegaard defends his title by 7:29 over Pogačar

Jonas Vingegaard won the Tour for the second year in a row, 7:29 ahead of Tadej Pogačar and 10:56 ahead of Adam Yates – the largest winning margin since 2014. After two weeks in which only seconds separated the two rivals, he settled the race in the Alps, and his Jumbo–Visma team also won the team classification.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 16

Vingegaard crushes the Combloux time trial

Going into the only individual time trial, 22.4 km from Passy to Combloux, Vingegaard led Pogačar by just 10 seconds. He won by 1:38 over the Slovenian, with teammate Wout van Aert third at 2:51, and stretched his lead to 1:48. Adam Yates moved up to third overall, five seconds ahead of Carlos Rodríguez.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 1

Yates twins one-two in Bilbao, Adam takes yellow

Adam Yates won the opening stage around Bilbao four seconds ahead of his twin brother Simon after the pair broke away from the favourites in the finale. Pogačar led the chasers home 12 seconds down; Adam Yates took the yellow and green jerseys and Neilson Powless the polka-dot jersey. Enric Mas abandoned after a crash.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 2

Lafay strikes under the flamme rouge in San Sebastián

Richard Carapaz did not start after his crash. On the Jaizkibel Pogačar took the 8 bonus seconds ahead of Vingegaard, and in the finale Victor Lafay attacked under the flamme rouge and held off the group led by Wout van Aert – Cofidis' first Tour stage win since 2008. Adam Yates stayed in yellow, six seconds ahead of Pogačar, and Lafay took green.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 3

Philipsen opens his account in Bayonne

After a two-man breakaway by Neilson Powless and Laurent Pichon, Jasper Philipsen won the sprint into Bayonne ahead of Phil Bauhaus and Caleb Ewan following a strong lead-out by Mathieu van der Poel. The commissaires reviewed his sprint duel with Wout van Aert but let the result stand. Adam Yates kept yellow and Powless added to his mountains lead.
