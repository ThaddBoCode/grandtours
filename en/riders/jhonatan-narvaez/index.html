In the headlines

Giro 2026 · Stage 4

Narváez wins in Cosenza, bonus seconds put Ciccone in pink

Back in Italy, Movistar tried to drop the sprinters on Cozzo Tunno for Orluis Aular; Kaden Groves and Arnaud De Lie abandoned. In the reduced sprint, though, Jhonatan Narváez (UAE Team Emirates XRG) beat Aular and Giulio Ciccone. Ciccone took the maglia rosa thanks to the bonus seconds, 4 seconds ahead of Jan Christen, the new maglia bianca, and Florian Stork.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 8

Narváez solos to victory in Fermo

On the hilly transition stage from Chieti to Fermo, Jhonatan Narváez (UAE Team Emirates XRG) took his second stage win, finishing solo 32 seconds ahead of Andreas Leknessund and 42 ahead of Martin Tjøtta. Nothing changed at the top of the overall standings, with Afonso Eulálio staying in pink ahead of Jonas Vingegaard.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 11

Narváez makes it three in Chiavari

Jhonatan Narváez (UAE Team Emirates XRG) made the day's breakaway and beat Enric Mas in a two-up sprint in Chiavari for his third stage win, with Diego Ulissi and Chris Harper 11 seconds back. Stage 6 winner Davide Ballerini abandoned, while Afonso Eulálio stayed 27 seconds ahead of Jonas Vingegaard overall.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 1

Narváez beats Pogačar on the opening day in Turin

Pogačar attacked on the final climb as early as stage 1, but Jhonatan Narváez and Maximilian Schachmann went with him, and Narváez won the three-man sprint in Turin. The Ecuadorian thus wore the first maglia rosa of the race before Pogačar took it the next day.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 9

Kooij wins in Naples as Narváez is caught near the line

In the finale Jhonatan Narváez attacked from the peloton and passed the earlier attacker Julian Alaphilippe, but was swept up by the sprinters a few metres from the line. Olav Kooij won ahead of Jonathan Milan and Juan Sebastián Molano, for whom race leader Pogačar had led the peloton in the final kilometre.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 12

Narváez wins the Nove Colli stage in Cesenatico

On Marco Pantani's home roads, over a route modelled on the Nove Colli gran fondo, a 14-rider break stayed clear. Jhonatan Narváez won alone in Cesenatico, 1:08 ahead of Mark Padun, who had been slowed by a mechanical problem; the maglia rosa group finished about eight minutes down with no change among the leaders.

 Giro 2026

Magnier takes three stages and the maglia ciclamino

France's Paul Magnier won at Burgas, Sofia and Pieve di Soligo and briefly wore the maglia rosa after stage 1. From stage 14 he duelled for the points lead with three-time stage winner Jhonatan Narváez, the jersey changing hands four times; when Narváez abandoned on stage 19, the maglia ciclamino was all but Magnier's.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 14

Vingegaard wins at Pila and takes the maglia rosa

On the short mountain stage around Aosta, Visma–Lease a Bike set up Jonas Vingegaard, who won solo at Pila for his third stage victory, 49 seconds ahead of Felix Gall and 58 ahead of Jai Hindley. Afonso Eulálio resisted for a long time but had to surrender the maglia rosa and now trailed by 2:26. Jhonatan Narváez took the maglia ciclamino, and Christian Scaroni abandoned.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 17

Valgren outfoxes his breakaway companions in Andalo

Michael Valgren (EF Education–EasyPost) caught out his breakaway companions in the final kilometre and won in Andalo 3 seconds ahead of Andreas Leknessund and 6 ahead of Damiano Caruso. Jhonatan Narváez was stuck in a chasing group but regained the maglia ciclamino thanks to the intermediate sprint; nothing changed at the top of the overall standings.
