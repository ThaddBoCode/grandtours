In the headlines

Giro 2026 · Stage 5

Eulálio: nine days in pink and the maglia bianca

Portugal's Afonso Eulálio (Bahrain Victorious) took the maglia rosa after a chaotic breakaway stage to Potenza and held it from stage 5 to stage 13 – keeping it by 27 seconds after the Massa time trial. He only lost it to Vingegaard at Pila, and finished sixth overall and winner of the young rider classification.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 14

Solo to Pila: Vingegaard takes the maglia rosa

Despite wins at Blockhaus and Corno alle Scale, Vingegaard still trailed Afonso Eulálio until Visma–Lease a Bike set him up on stage 14 to Pila: he won solo and took the maglia rosa with a 2:26 lead. He extended it with further wins at Carì and Piancavallo; Felix Gall, second, took his first Grand Tour podium, with Jai Hindley third.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 6

Ballerini profits from a last-corner crash in Naples

The stage to Naples was heading for a bunch sprint until the leading Unibet Rose Rockets train crashed on wet cobbles approaching the final corner, a few hundred metres from the line. Davide Ballerini (XDS Astana) and Jasper Stuyven came through unhindered on the inside, and Ballerini won ahead of Stuyven and Paul Magnier. Afonso Eulálio stayed in pink.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 7

Vingegaard strikes on the Blockhaus

At the end of the longest stage, 244 km, Jonas Vingegaard (Visma–Lease a Bike) attacked 5 km from the top of the Blockhaus and won solo; only Felix Gall limited his loss to under a minute, at 13 seconds. Afonso Eulálio kept the maglia rosa but lost about half his advantage, now leading Vingegaard – the new maglia azzurra – by 3:17.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 8

Narváez solos to victory in Fermo

On the hilly transition stage from Chieti to Fermo, Jhonatan Narváez (UAE Team Emirates XRG) took his second stage win, finishing solo 32 seconds ahead of Andreas Leknessund and 42 ahead of Martin Tjøtta. Nothing changed at the top of the overall standings, with Afonso Eulálio staying in pink ahead of Jonas Vingegaard.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 9

Vingegaard counters Gall at Corno alle Scale

Decathlon CMA CGM rode for Felix Gall, but Jonas Vingegaard countered 1 km from the finish and won 12 seconds ahead of the Austrian. Gall still gained time on the other contenders, while Giulio Pellizzari struggled on the final climb. Afonso Eulálio stayed in pink, his lead over Vingegaard down to 2:24.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 10

Ganna dominates the time trial to Massa

Filippo Ganna (Netcompany INEOS) won the 42 km individual time trial from Viareggio to Massa by 1:54 over teammate Thymen Arensman, who gained the most among the favourites and moved up to third overall. Jonas Vingegaard missed the top ten but closed to 27 seconds, so Afonso Eulálio kept the maglia rosa despite a poor time trial.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 11

Narváez makes it three in Chiavari

Jhonatan Narváez (UAE Team Emirates XRG) made the day's breakaway and beat Enric Mas in a two-up sprint in Chiavari for his third stage win, with Diego Ulissi and Chris Harper 11 seconds back. Stage 6 winner Davide Ballerini abandoned, while Afonso Eulálio stayed 27 seconds ahead of Jonas Vingegaard overall.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 12

Segaert surprises the bunch in Novi Ligure

Movistar again tried to shake off the fastest sprinters, and those who remained lacked teammates in the finale. Alec Segaert (Team Bahrain Victorious) took advantage, attacking 3 km from the line and holding on to win by 3 seconds from Toon Aerts and Guillermo Thomas Silva. Afonso Eulálio stayed in pink, 33 seconds ahead of Jonas Vingegaard.
