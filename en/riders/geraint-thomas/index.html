In the headlines

Giro 2024

Pogačar wins his first Giro by 9:56

Tadej Pogačar won the Giro on his first participation, taking six stages and the maglia azzurra of the mountains classification as well. His margin of 9:56 over Daniel Martínez was the biggest since 1965, and his six stage wins put him level with Eddy Merckx (1973) as the post-war overall winner with the most stage victories. Geraint Thomas completed the podium in third.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 2

Puncture, crash, then victory: Pogačar takes pink at Oropa

At the foot of the final climb to the Santuario di Oropa, Pogačar punctured and then crashed, but quickly regained the peloton. He attacked about 4.5 km from the finish, won alone by 27 seconds over Daniel Martínez and Geraint Thomas, and took the maglia rosa, which he kept all the way to Rome.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 15

Queen stage to Livigno: Pogačar reels in Quintana

On the longest stage of the Giro at 222 km, Pogačar attacked alone on the final climb to Mottolino, picked off the breakaway riders one by one and caught Nairo Quintana 2 km from the summit. He won by 29 seconds and stretched his lead to 6:41 over Geraint Thomas.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 3

Pogačar and Thomas caught at 300 m, Merlier wins in Fossano

Crosswinds split the peloton 58 km from the finish before it came back together. On the last rise just over 3 km from Fossano, race leader Tadej Pogačar and Geraint Thomas went clear but were caught 300 m from the line. Tim Merlier won the sprint ahead of Jonathan Milan and Biniam Girmay and took the lead of the points classification.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 8

Pogačar wins the favourites' sprint at Prati di Tivo

Valentin Paret-Peintre, the last survivor of the breakaway, was caught by the favourites' group 4.2 km from the finish. Tadej Pogačar attacked with 150 m to go to win ahead of Daniel Martínez and Ben O'Connor, and now led Martínez by 2:40 and Geraint Thomas by 2:58. Cian Uijtdebroeks regained the young rider lead.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 14

Ganna beats Pogačar in the Desenzano time trial

A week after his defeat in Perugia, Filippo Ganna won the 31.2 km time trial to Desenzano del Garda by 29 seconds over Tadej Pogačar, with Thymen Arensman third. Geraint Thomas, fourth on the day, moved up to second overall, 3:41 behind Pogačar.

 Giro 2024 · Stage 16

Shortened stage: Pogačar takes win number five on Monte Pana

Because of the weather the Umbrail Pass was removed and, after a chaotic start, the stage was cut to 118.7 km starting in Laas. Julian Alaphilippe went solo 33 km from the finish but was caught on the final climb; Tadej Pogačar attacked, passed Giulio Pellizzari 800 m from the line and won by 16 seconds over Pellizzari and Daniel Martínez. Martínez moved back into second overall ahead of Geraint Thomas.

 Giro 2023

Roglič becomes the first Slovenian to win the Giro

Primož Roglič won the 106th Giro d'Italia by 14 seconds over Geraint Thomas, with João Almeida third at 1:15 – his fourth Grand Tour victory and the first Giro win by a Slovenian. He only took the maglia rosa on the penultimate day.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 20

Roglič overturns Thomas in the Monte Lussari time trial

Geraint Thomas led by 26 seconds before the 18.6 km mountain time trial from Tarvisio to Monte Lussari. Roglič won it by 40 seconds over Thomas and 42 over Almeida, turning his deficit into a 14-second lead that he held to Rome.
