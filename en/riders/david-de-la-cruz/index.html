In the headlines

Tour 2023 · Stage 12

Ion Izagirre solos to Belleville-en-Beaujolais

Fabio Jakobsen did not start and David de la Cruz crashed out. After a fierce fight for the breakaway, Ion Izagirre went clear on the Col de la Croix Rosier and rode the last 30 km or so alone, winning by 58 seconds from Mathieu Burgaudeau and Matteo Jorgenson. It was Cofidis' second stage win of the Tour after Lafay's.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 18

López wins on the new Gamoniteiru; Roglič second

Michael Storer rode 72 km solo off the front before David de la Cruz caught him, and Miguel Ángel López then attacked from the GC group to drop de la Cruz and win solo. Roglič outsprinted Mas and Bernal for second at 14", extending his lead to 2' 30", and Storer took the mountains jersey.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 19

Cort wins a third stage; Meintjes crashes out of the top 10

Magnus Cort won the sprint of a seven-man breakaway that stayed about half a minute clear of the peloton, ahead of Rui Oliveira and Quinn Simmons, for his third stage win. With 43 km to go a crash forced Louis Meintjes, tenth on GC, to abandon, and David de la Cruz moved into the top ten.
