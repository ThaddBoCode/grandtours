In the headlines

Giro 2020 · Stage 18

Stelvio shake-up: Kelderman in pink, Hindley wins

On the queen stage over the Stelvio, the Cima Coppi, Rohan Dennis set the pace for Tao Geoghegan Hart and distanced João Almeida, Vincenzo Nibali and the other favourites. Jai Hindley won at Laghi di Cancano ahead of Geoghegan Hart, while his Sunweb teammate Wilco Kelderman took the maglia rosa, 12 seconds ahead of Hindley and 15 ahead of Geoghegan Hart.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 8

Plapp solos to Castelraimondo, Ulissi takes the maglia rosa

Luke Plapp won from the breakaway with a 46 km solo, finishing 38 seconds ahead of Wilco Kelderman and Diego Ulissi. Ulissi took the maglia rosa, 12 seconds ahead of Lorenzo Fortunato, while the group of favourites finished 4:50 behind the winner.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 2

Van der Poel takes yellow on the Mûr-de-Bretagne

Mathieu van der Poel attacked on the first ascent of the Mûr-de-Bretagne to collect the 8 bonus seconds, then went clear again on the second and won 6 seconds ahead of Tadej Pogačar, Primož Roglič and Wilco Kelderman. That earned him the yellow jersey, 8 seconds ahead of Julian Alaphilippe, as well as the polka-dot jersey. Geraint Thomas lost 17 seconds to the two Slovenians.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 20

Van Aert wins the time trial as Pogačar seals the Tour

Wout van Aert set the only time under 36 minutes, 35:53, to win the 30.8 km time trial from Libourne to Saint-Émilion, 21 seconds ahead of Kasper Asgreen and 32 ahead of Jonas Vingegaard. Vingegaard secured second overall, Ben O'Connor held fourth ahead of Wilco Kelderman, and Tadej Pogačar rode cautiously to eighth. Going into Paris he led Vingegaard by 5:20 and Richard Carapaz by 7:03.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 21

Geoghegan Hart wins the Giro without ever wearing pink

Tao Geoghegan Hart won the Giro by 39 seconds over Jai Hindley after the pair started the final time trial into Milan level on time, with Hindley in pink on countback. The British rider became the first in Giro history to win the race without having worn the maglia rosa before the final stage, and he also took the maglia bianca. Wilco Kelderman finished third at 1:29.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 3

Almeida's 15 days in the maglia rosa

João Almeida took the maglia rosa on Etna on stage 3 and defended it for 15 days, still leading Wilco Kelderman by 17 seconds after stage 17. He cracked on the Stelvio on stage 18 and finished the Giro fourth at 2:57.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 20

Dead heat after Sestriere: Hindley and Geoghegan Hart level

Stage 20 was shortened because French COVID-19 rules kept the race out of France, cutting the Colle dell'Agnello and the Izoard. On the climbs to Sestriere INEOS Grenadiers, driven by Rohan Dennis, dropped race leader Wilco Kelderman; Tao Geoghegan Hart beat Jai Hindley in the sprint and, thanks to the bonus seconds, drew level with him on time before the final day.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 9

Guerreiro wins at Roccaraso and takes the maglia azzurra

Ruben Guerreiro won from the break on the Aremogna climb above Roccaraso, eight seconds ahead of Jonathan Castroviejo, and moved into the maglia azzurra. On the steep finish Jakob Fuglsang, Wilco Kelderman and Domenico Pozzovivo gained time, Kelderman moving up to second overall, 30 seconds behind João Almeida.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 14

Ganna wins the time trial to Valdobbiadene

Filippo Ganna won the 34.1 km time trial from Conegliano to Valdobbiadene, 26 seconds ahead of INEOS teammate Rohan Dennis and more than a minute ahead of everyone else. João Almeida extended his lead over Wilco Kelderman to 56 seconds, and Brandon McNulty, third on the stage, moved up to fourth overall.
