In the headlines

Tour 2025 · Stage 3

Crashes knock out Philipsen and Almeida

Jasper Philipsen, winner of stage 1 and the first yellow jersey, crashed in the intermediate sprint on stage 3 and abandoned with a broken collarbone and broken ribs. Pogačar's teammate João Almeida broke a rib in a crash on stage 7 and abandoned two days later, while Filippo Ganna and Stefan Bissegger had already left on stage 1 with concussions.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 16

Almeida outduels Thomas on Monte Bondone, Thomas back in pink

João Almeida won atop Monte Bondone ahead of Geraint Thomas on the same time, with Primož Roglič and Eddie Dunbar 25 seconds behind. Thomas took back the maglia rosa, 18 seconds ahead of Almeida and 29 ahead of Roglič, and Ben Healy took the maglia azzurra.

 Giro 2022

Bardet, Yates and Almeida: contenders leave the race

Several favourites left early: Miguel Ángel López abandoned on stage 4 with a hip injury, Romain Bardet, then fourth overall, quit on stage 13 with stomach problems, and Simon Yates, winner of two stages, abandoned on stage 17. João Almeida, also fourth overall at the time, withdrew before stage 18 after testing positive for COVID-19.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 3

Almeida's 15 days in the maglia rosa

João Almeida took the maglia rosa on Etna on stage 3 and defended it for 15 days, still leading Wilco Kelderman by 17 seconds after stage 17. He cracked on the Stelvio on stage 18 and finished the Giro fourth at 2:57.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 15

Geoghegan Hart wins at Piancavallo, Almeida holds on

Jai Hindley set the pace on the final climb for his Sunweb leader Wilco Kelderman, and only Tao Geoghegan Hart could follow, going on to win ahead of Kelderman and Hindley. João Almeida lost 37 seconds but kept the maglia rosa, 15 seconds ahead of Kelderman, and Giovanni Visconti took the maglia azzurra.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 7

Pogačar wins on the Mûr-de-Bretagne and retakes yellow

A high-speed crash in the finale cost Pogačar's teammate João Almeida more than ten minutes and a broken rib. On the second ascent of the Mûr-de-Bretagne Tadej Pogačar won the uphill sprint ahead of Jonas Vingegaard and took the yellow jersey back from the dropped Mathieu van der Poel. He now led Remco Evenepoel by 54 seconds and Vingegaard by 1:17.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 9

Merlier wins after van der Poel's all-day raid with Rickaert

Mathieu van der Poel and his teammate Jonas Rickaert attacked from the start and led for almost the whole day; van der Poel went solo 6 km from the finish but was caught inside the final kilometre. Tim Merlier then beat Jonathan Milan for his second stage win. At 50.013 km/h it was the second-fastest stage in Tour history, and João Almeida abandoned with injuries from his stage 7 crash.

 Giro 2023

Roglič becomes the first Slovenian to win the Giro

Primož Roglič won the 106th Giro d'Italia by 14 seconds over Geraint Thomas, with João Almeida third at 1:15 – his fourth Grand Tour victory and the first Giro win by a Slovenian. He only took the maglia rosa on the penultimate day.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 20

Roglič overturns Thomas in the Monte Lussari time trial

Geraint Thomas led by 26 seconds before the 18.6 km mountain time trial from Tarvisio to Monte Lussari. Roglič won it by 40 seconds over Thomas and 42 over Almeida, turning his deficit into a 14-second lead that he held to Rome.
