In the headlines

Giro 2024 · Stage 5

Breakaway survives: Benjamin Thomas wins in Lucca

A group of four went clear 76 km from the finish and held a lead of a few seconds over the peloton all the way to the line. Benjamin Thomas won the sprint ahead of Michael Valgren and Andrea Pietrobon, with Enzo Paleni 3 seconds back. Pogačar stayed in pink and Jonathan Milan kept the maglia ciclamino.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 15

Philipsen wins in Carcassonne on a bad day for Jumbo–Visma

Magnus Cort and Simon Clarke left the race with COVID-19 before the start and Roglič did not start; Steven Kruijswijk then abandoned with a dislocated shoulder after a crash, and Vingegaard also went down but continued. Benjamin Thomas was caught inside the final kilometre, and Jasper Philipsen came past van Aert to win his first Tour stage.
