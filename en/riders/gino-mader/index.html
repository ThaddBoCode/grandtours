In the headlines

Tour 2023 · Stage 10

Pello Bilbao wins in Issoire for Gino Mäder

After a chaotic start in great heat, with Vingegaard and Pogačar themselves joining early attacks, a stable breakaway only formed around halfway. Krists Neilands rode alone in front for a long time but was caught near the finish, and Pello Bilbao won the six-man sprint ahead of Georg Zimmermann and Ben O'Connor. Bilbao dedicated the win to his late teammate Gino Mäder.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 6

Mäder wins at San Giacomo, Valter in pink

Gino Mäder attacked from the break on the first summit finish and held off Egan Bernal's group by 12 seconds at San Giacomo, also taking the maglia azzurra. Attila Valter took the maglia rosa, the first Hungarian ever to wear it, with Remco Evenepoel 11 seconds and Bernal 16 seconds behind.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 20

Haig steps up after Landa's exit to take third; Mäder wins white

Jack Haig started as a domestique for Mikel Landa, who struggled and abandoned on stage 17; Haig then became team leader. On stage 20 he moved past López into third overall, Gino Mäder won the white jersey and finished fifth, and Bahrain Victorious took the team classification.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 19

Mohorič wins the photo finish in Poligny

After a fast, nervous stage, Kasper Asgreen, Matej Mohorič and Ben O'Connor went clear on the Côte d'Ivory. O'Connor opened the sprint early and Mohorič beat Asgreen in a photo finish by the width of a rim – his third Tour stage win. In an emotional interview he paid tribute to his late teammate Gino Mäder.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 21

Roglič wins the final time trial and the Vuelta

As the last starter Roglič won the 33.8 km time trial from Padrón to Santiago de Compostela in 44' 02", 14 seconds ahead of Magnus Cort, taking his fourth stage and confirming a third straight overall win. Jakobsen (green), Storer (mountains) and Mäder (white) secured their jerseys, and Bahrain Victorious won the team classification.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 11

David Gaudu wins mountain stages 11 and 17

Frenchman David Gaudu (Groupama–FDJ) won stage 11 at the Alto de la Farrapona ahead of Marc Soler (+0:04) and stage 17 at La Covatilla ahead of Gino Mäder (+0:28). He finished eighth overall (+7:45) and second in the young rider classification behind Enric Mas.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 17

Gaudu wins at La Covatilla, Carapaz gains time

David Gaudu won the final mountain stage at La Covatilla ahead of Gino Mäder (+0:28) and Ion Izagirre (+1:05). Carapaz cut his deficit to Roglič from 45 to 24 seconds; Roglič kept the red jersey and Carthy remained third (+0:47).
