In the headlines

Giro 2021 · Stage 5

Landa crashes out in the finale at Cattolica

In the finale of stage 5 Joe Dombrowski collided with a marshal at a traffic island and Mikel Landa crashed heavily in the chain reaction. Landa was taken to hospital with a broken collarbone and broken ribs, and Dombrowski (concussion), François Bidard and Pavel Sivakov, who also crashed that day, did not start stage 6.
