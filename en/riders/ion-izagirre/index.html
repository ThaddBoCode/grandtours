In the headlines

Tour 2023 · Stage 12

Ion Izagirre solos to Belleville-en-Beaujolais

Fabio Jakobsen did not start and David de la Cruz crashed out. After a fierce fight for the breakaway, Ion Izagirre went clear on the Col de la Croix Rosier and rode the last 30 km or so alone, winning by 58 seconds from Mathieu Burgaudeau and Matteo Jorgenson. It was Cofidis' second stage win of the Tour after Lafay's.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 8

Teuns wins in Le Grand-Bornand, Pogačar storms into yellow

On the first Alpine stage, partly in the rain, Geraint Thomas and Primož Roglič lost more than 30 minutes and Mathieu van der Poel was dropped on the Col de Romme. Tadej Pogačar attacked 4 km from the top of the Romme, dropped Richard Carapaz and gained almost three and a half minutes on his rivals, while Dylan Teuns soloed to victory, 44 seconds ahead of Ion Izagirre. Pogačar took yellow with a lead of 1:48 over Wout van Aert, and Wout Poels the polka-dot jersey.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 11

Ewan wins a photo finish in Poitiers, Sagan relegated

After Mathieu Ladagnous's solo breakaway was caught, four riders threw their bikes at the line and Caleb Ewan won his second stage of the Tour. Peter Sagan, initially second, was relegated for shouldering Wout van Aert in the sprint, so Sam Bennett moved up to second and extended his green-jersey lead to 68 points. Ion Izagirre abandoned after a crash 30 km from the finish.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 6

Izagirre wins at Formigal, Carapaz takes red

Ion Izagirre (Astana) won the mountain stage to Aramón Formigal ahead of Michael Woods and Rui Costa (both +0:25). Because of French COVID-19 restrictions, the originally planned 136.6 km stage to the Col du Tourmalet had been replaced by a 146.4 km stage to Formigal. Carapaz took the red jersey, 18 seconds ahead of Carthy and 20 ahead of Dan Martin; Roglič was 30 seconds back.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 17

Gaudu wins at La Covatilla, Carapaz gains time

David Gaudu won the final mountain stage at La Covatilla ahead of Gino Mäder (+0:28) and Ion Izagirre (+1:05). Carapaz cut his deficit to Roglič from 45 to 24 seconds; Roglič kept the red jersey and Carthy remained third (+0:47).
