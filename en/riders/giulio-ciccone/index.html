In the headlines

Giro 2026 · Stage 19

Ciccone claims the maglia azzurra in the Dolomites

Giulio Ciccone, who spent a day in pink after stage 4, took the mountains lead from Vingegaard on the queen stage to Alleghe, which crossed the Passo Giau as the roof of the race, and went on to win the maglia azzurra as well as the Cima Coppi. It was his second mountains title at the Giro and his third in a Grand Tour.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 4

Narváez wins in Cosenza, bonus seconds put Ciccone in pink

Back in Italy, Movistar tried to drop the sprinters on Cozzo Tunno for Orluis Aular; Kaden Groves and Arnaud De Lie abandoned. In the reduced sprint, though, Jhonatan Narváez (UAE Team Emirates XRG) beat Aular and Giulio Ciccone. Ciccone took the maglia rosa thanks to the bonus seconds, 4 seconds ahead of Jan Christen, the new maglia bianca, and Florian Stork.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 20

Ciccone takes the polka-dot jersey, Italy's first since 1992

After Neilson Powless, Felix Gall and Vingegaard had all led the mountains classification, Giulio Ciccone took over on stage 15 and sealed it in the Vosges on stage 20, finishing with 106 points to Gall's 92. He was the first Italian to win the classification since Claudio Chiappucci in 1992.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 15

Ciccone solos to victory in Cogne

From a 28-man breakaway, Giulio Ciccone, Santiago Buitrago and Antonio Pedrero went clear on the climb to Verrogne, and Ciccone attacked twice on the steepest section of the final climb to Cogne. He won alone, 1:31 ahead of Buitrago; Koen Bouwman regained the maglia azzurra and Richard Carapaz, who had crashed early in the stage, kept pink.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 2

Thomas Silva: first Uruguayan to win a stage and wear pink

Guillermo Thomas Silva (XDS Astana) won the reduced sprint at Veliko Tarnovo ahead of Florian Stork and Giulio Ciccone, after Vingegaard had countered an attack by Christian Scaroni on the final climb. He became the first Uruguayan to win a Giro stage and wear the maglia rosa, which he kept for two days.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 21

Milan sprints to victory in Rome as Vingegaard celebrates

Jonathan Milan (Lidl–Trek) won the bunch sprint on the final stage in Rome ahead of Giovanni Lonardi and Paul Penhoët. Jonas Vingegaard won the Giro by 5:22 over Felix Gall and 6:25 over Jai Hindley, with Paul Magnier taking the maglia ciclamino, Giulio Ciccone the maglia azzurra and Afonso Eulálio the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 3

Pedersen sprints to victory in Vlorë and reclaims the maglia rosa

Lorenzo Fortunato and Pello Bilbao crossed the Llogara pass together in front but were caught 17 km from the finish in Vlorë. Led out by Giulio Ciccone and Mathias Vacek, Mads Pedersen won the sprint ahead of Corbin Strong and Orluis Aular and took back the maglia rosa, 9 seconds ahead of Roglič. Fortunato moved into the maglia azzurra.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 11

Carapaz attacks late to win at Castelnovo ne' Monti

Lorenzo Fortunato attacked from a large break 103 km from the finish, but the escapees were caught on the Pietra di Bismantova. Richard Carapaz went clear of the maglia rosa group 8 km from the line and won by 10 seconds from del Toro, Giulio Ciccone and Tom Pidcock. Del Toro stayed in pink, 31 seconds ahead of Ayuso.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 14

Asgreen solos to Nova Gorica as a crash hits the favourites

Kasper Asgreen, Mirco Maestri and Martin Marcellusi led for much of a rainy stage before Asgreen went alone 6 km from the finish in Nova Gorica, Slovenia, and won by 16 seconds from Kaden Groves and Olav Kooij. A crash 22 km from the finish brought down or held up Juan Ayuso, Primož Roglič, Antonio Tiberi and Giulio Ciccone, who did not start the next day. Simon Yates moved up to second overall, 1:20 behind del Toro.
