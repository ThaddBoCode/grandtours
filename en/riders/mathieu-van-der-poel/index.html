In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 9

Van der Poel wins the shortened heatwave stage to Ussel

Because of a red heatwave warning for the Corrèze department, the stage was shortened by 30.9 km and raced in temperatures of up to 40 °C. Mathieu van der Poel attacked from an eight-man breakaway on Mont Bessou, with only Tobias Halland Johannessen and Tom Pidcock able to follow and Alex Baudin joining on the descent. Van der Poel won the sprint six seconds ahead of the peloton – in the Limousin, home region of his grandfather Raymond Poulidor – and the GC was unchanged.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 17

Philipsen wins in Voiron as the green battle tightens

Pedersen bridged to a large breakaway and won the intermediate sprint, after which a series of attacks followed. In the sprint of a reduced group, Mathieu van der Poel led out Jasper Philipsen, who won ahead of Mauro Schmid and Olav Kooij. Pedersen finished only eighth and his lead in the points classification shrank to seven points.

 Tour 2025

Van der Poel: yellow twice, an all-day break, then out ill

Mathieu van der Poel won stage 2 to take the yellow jersey and won it back by a single second from the stage 6 breakaway. On stage 9 he spent almost the whole day out front with teammate Jonas Rickaert before being caught inside the final kilometre, and pneumonia forced him out before stage 16.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 2

Van der Poel beats Pogačar in Boulogne and takes yellow

After a nervous finale over three late climbs, Mathieu van der Poel opened the sprint early in Boulogne-sur-Mer and held off Tadej Pogačar, with Jonas Vingegaard third. Van der Poel took the yellow jersey from his teammate Jasper Philipsen, with Pogačar 4 seconds and Vingegaard 6 seconds behind.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 4

Pogačar's 100th career win in Rouen, van der Poel stays in yellow

Tadej Pogačar attacked on the steep Rampe Saint-Hilaire, with only Jonas Vingegaard able to follow, before a small group regrouped on the descent. Pogačar came around Mathieu van der Poel in the sprint for the 100th win of his career. Van der Poel kept the yellow jersey on the same time as Pogačar, ahead on the sum of stage placings.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 6

Healy solos to Vire, van der Poel back in yellow by one second

Ben Healy attacked from an eight-rider breakaway about 42 km from the finish and soloed to his first Tour stage win in Vire Normandie, 2:44 ahead of Quinn Simmons. Mathieu van der Poel, also in the break, gained 1:29 on the favourites and took the yellow jersey back from Tadej Pogačar by a single second.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 7

Pogačar wins on the Mûr-de-Bretagne and retakes yellow

A high-speed crash in the finale cost Pogačar's teammate João Almeida more than ten minutes and a broken rib. On the second ascent of the Mûr-de-Bretagne Tadej Pogačar won the uphill sprint ahead of Jonas Vingegaard and took the yellow jersey back from the dropped Mathieu van der Poel. He now led Remco Evenepoel by 54 seconds and Vingegaard by 1:17.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 8

Milan wins in Laval – first Italian Tour stage win since 2019

On a quiet day, Jonathan Milan came around Mathieu van der Poel, who was leading out Kaden Groves, and beat Wout van Aert to win his first Tour stage. He was the first Italian to win a Tour stage since Vincenzo Nibali in 2019, and he regained the green jersey. Tim Merlier was out of contention after a mechanical 12.5 km from the finish.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 9

Merlier wins after van der Poel's all-day raid with Rickaert

Mathieu van der Poel and his teammate Jonas Rickaert attacked from the start and led for almost the whole day; van der Poel went solo 6 km from the finish but was caught inside the final kilometre. Tim Merlier then beat Jonathan Milan for his second stage win. At 50.013 km/h it was the second-fastest stage in Tour history, and João Almeida abandoned with injuries from his stage 7 crash.
