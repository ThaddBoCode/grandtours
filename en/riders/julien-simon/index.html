In the headlines

Giro 2025 · Stage 20

Simon Yates wins the Giro on the Colle delle Finestre

Simon Yates (Visma–Lease a Bike) won his first Giro d'Italia in 82h 31' 01", taking the maglia rosa only on the penultimate stage. He finished 3:56 ahead of Isaac del Toro and 4:43 ahead of former Giro winner Richard Carapaz – without winning a stage himself.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 2

Simon Yates wins in Budapest, van der Poel stays in pink

Simon Yates set the best time of 11:50 on the 9.2 km time trial in Budapest, three seconds faster than Mathieu van der Poel, the last rider to start, and five faster than Tom Dumoulin. Van der Poel kept the maglia rosa, while Italian time trial champion Matteo Sobrero took the maglia bianca.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 10

Yates wins on Bastille Day, Healy takes the yellow jersey

On the first mountain stage, through the Massif Central, Simon Yates attacked twice on the Puy de Sancy and won from a 29-rider breakaway, 9 seconds ahead of Thymen Arensman. Ben Healy, third from the same break, took the yellow jersey with a 29-second lead over Tadej Pogačar, while Lenny Martinez took the polka-dot jersey.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 14

Asgreen solos to Nova Gorica as a crash hits the favourites

Kasper Asgreen, Mirco Maestri and Martin Marcellusi led for much of a rainy stage before Asgreen went alone 6 km from the finish in Nova Gorica, Slovenia, and won by 16 seconds from Kaden Groves and Olav Kooij. A crash 22 km from the finish brought down or held up Juan Ayuso, Primož Roglič, Antonio Tiberi and Giulio Ciccone, who did not start the next day. Simon Yates moved up to second overall, 1:20 behind del Toro.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 15

Verona wins alone in Asiago, Roglič loses time

Carlos Verona attacked from the break on the climb of Dori, 43 km from the finish, and won alone in Asiago, 22 seconds ahead of Florian Stork and 23 ahead of Christian Scaroni. Primož Roglič finished almost two minutes behind the winner and dropped to tenth overall, 3:53 down. Del Toro kept the maglia rosa, 1:20 ahead of Simon Yates.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 16

Scaroni wins at San Valentino as Roglič quits and del Toro wobbles

On a stage that began in very difficult weather, Primož Roglič abandoned and Juan Ayuso was dropped on the climb of Santa Barbara. XDS Astana teammates Christian Scaroni and Lorenzo Fortunato rode clear on the final climb, and Scaroni won at San Valentino. Del Toro was dropped and lost 1:36 to Richard Carapaz but kept the maglia rosa by 26 seconds over Simon Yates and 31 over Carapaz.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 17

Del Toro rebounds to win in Bormio

After the Tonale and the Mortirolo, Richard Carapaz and maglia rosa Isaac del Toro caught Romain Bardet, the last of the break, before del Toro went clear alone to win in Bormio – his first Giro stage win. Bardet and Carapaz finished 4 seconds back; Carapaz moved up to second overall at 41 seconds, with Simon Yates third at 51 seconds.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 19

Prodhomme wins at Champoluc, del Toro answers Carapaz

Nicolas Prodhomme attacked from the break on the Col de Joux, 28 km from the finish, and won alone in Champoluc. Maglia rosa Isaac del Toro answered every attack from Richard Carapaz, and the pair finished 58 seconds behind the winner and 24 seconds ahead of the other favourites. Before the last mountain stage del Toro led Carapaz by 43 seconds and Simon Yates by 1:21.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 21

Kooij wins in Rome, Yates seals his Giro

The final stage started in the Vatican Gardens, where Pope Leo XIV welcomed the riders, and finished in Rome after eight laps of a local circuit. Olav Kooij won the sprint ahead of Kaden Groves and Matteo Moschetti, and his Visma–Lease a Bike teammate Simon Yates sealed overall victory, 3:56 ahead of Isaac del Toro.
