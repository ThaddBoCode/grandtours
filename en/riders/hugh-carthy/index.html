In the headlines

Vuelta 2020 · Stage 12

Carthy wins on the Angliru, Carapaz back in red

On the 109.4 km stage finishing atop the Alto de L'Angliru, Hugh Carthy beat Aleksandr Vlasov, Enric Mas and Richard Carapaz (all +0:16). Roglič finished 26 seconds behind Carthy, Carapaz took the red jersey with a 10-second lead, and Carthy rose to third overall.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 21

Sobrero wins in Verona, Hindley seals the Giro

Italian time trial champion Matteo Sobrero won the 17.4 km time trial in 22:24, 23 seconds ahead of Thymen Arensman and 40 ahead of Mathieu van der Poel, for his first Grand Tour stage win. Jai Hindley finished 15th to win the Giro by 1:18 over Carapaz, with Landa third; Hugh Carthy moved up to ninth, but Juan Pedro López kept the maglia bianca.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 7

Crashes and abandons: only 142 of 184 reach Santiago

142 riders finished the race, 42 fewer than started. On stage 7 Alejandro Valverde (fractured collarbone after a crash) and Hugh Carthy abandoned; after the stage 16 crash Giulio Ciccone, Rudy Molard and Sep Vanmarcke left the race, Louis Meintjes followed on stage 19 while tenth overall, and Miguel Ángel López on stage 20.

 Vuelta 2020

Roglič wins a second straight Vuelta by 24 seconds

Defending champion Primož Roglič (Jumbo–Visma) won the 2020 Vuelta in 72:46:12, 24 seconds ahead of Richard Carapaz, with Hugh Carthy third at 1:15. Roglič took four stages (1, 8, 10 and 13) and the green points jersey as well.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 13

Roglič turns the Vuelta around in the Ézaro mountain time trial

Before the 33.7 km mountain time trial Roglič sat 10 seconds behind race leader Carapaz. He won in 46:39 with Will Barta second (+0:01) and Carthy fourth (+0:25), and moved back into the lead 39 seconds ahead of Carapaz and 47 ahead of Carthy.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 6

Izagirre wins at Formigal, Carapaz takes red

Ion Izagirre (Astana) won the mountain stage to Aramón Formigal ahead of Michael Woods and Rui Costa (both +0:25). Because of French COVID-19 restrictions, the originally planned 136.6 km stage to the Col du Tourmalet had been replaced by a 146.4 km stage to Formigal. Carapaz took the red jersey, 18 seconds ahead of Carthy and 20 ahead of Dan Martin; Roglič was 30 seconds back.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 14

Wellens wins in Ourense for a second time

Tim Wellens (Lotto–Soudal) took his second stage win of this Vuelta in Ourense, ahead of Michael Woods and Zdeněk Štybar (both on the same time). The general classification was unchanged: Roglič led by 39 seconds over Carapaz and 47 over Carthy.

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 15

Philipsen wins the longest stage in Puebla de Sanabria

Jasper Philipsen (UAE Team Emirates) won the sprint in Puebla de Sanabria after 230.8 km, the longest stage of this Vuelta, ahead of Pascal Ackermann and Jannik Steimle. The top of the general classification did not change: Roglič ahead of Carapaz (+0:39) and Carthy (+0:47).

 Vuelta 2020 · Stage 16

Cort wins in Ciudad Rodrigo, Roglič second

Magnus Cort (EF Pro Cycling) won in Ciudad Rodrigo ahead of Roglič and Dion Smith (all on the same time). Roglič extended his general classification lead to 45 seconds over Carapaz and 53 over Carthy.
