In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 20

Debutants del Toro and Seixas fight for the podium

Two Tour debutants fought over third place: Isaac del Toro finished third overall and won the white jersey, with Pogačar riding for him on stage 20. Paul Seixas, 19 and riding his first Grand Tour, finished fourth – with Lenny Martinez fifth, two French riders made the top five for the first time since 2014.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 19

Pogačar breaks Pantani's Alpe d'Huez record

Pogačar went clear alone on the climb to Alpe d'Huez, about 12 km from the finish, picked off the breakaway riders one by one, caught Carapaz and Lenny Martinez 1.7 km from the line and won solo – his fifth stage win of the race. His time beat the record for the climb set by Marco Pantani in 1995. Carapaz took the polka-dot jersey from Pogačar by one point, and del Toro stayed third overall, 24 seconds ahead of Seixas.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 10

Yates wins on Bastille Day, Healy takes the yellow jersey

On the first mountain stage, through the Massif Central, Simon Yates attacked twice on the Puy de Sancy and won from a 29-rider breakaway, 9 seconds ahead of Thymen Arensman. Ben Healy, third from the same break, took the yellow jersey with a 29-second lead over Tadej Pogačar, while Lenny Martinez took the polka-dot jersey.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 18

O'Connor wins solo on the Col de la Loze, Pogačar drops Vingegaard

On the queen stage over the Glandon and the Madeleine, Ben O'Connor attacked in the valley before the Col de la Loze and won alone, 1:45 ahead of Tadej Pogačar, who dropped Jonas Vingegaard inside the final kilometre. Pogačar's lead grew to 4:26, while Oscar Onley gained more than a minute and a half on Florian Lipowitz to close within 22 seconds of third place. Lenny Martinez was docked 8 mountains points for taking sticky bottles.
