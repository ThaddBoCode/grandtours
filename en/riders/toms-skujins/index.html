In the headlines

Giro 2023 · Stage 4

Paret-Peintre wins at Lago Laceno, Leknessund takes pink

Aurélien Paret-Peintre won at Lago Laceno two seconds ahead of Andreas Leknessund, with Toms Skujiņš third at 57 seconds. Leknessund took the maglia rosa, 28 seconds ahead of Remco Evenepoel, and also led the young rider classification.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 12

Denz wins the breakaway sprint in Rivoli

Nico Denz won the breakaway sprint in Rivoli ahead of Toms Skujiņš, with Sebastian Berwick third at three seconds. Geraint Thomas kept the maglia rosa, two seconds ahead of Primož Roglič.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 8

Peters wins in Loudenvielle as Pinot's hopes fade

Nans Peters dropped Ilnur Zakarin on the descent of the Port de Balès and held on over the Peyresourde to win from a 13-man break, 47 seconds ahead of Toms Skujiņš and Carlos Verona. Behind, Tadej Pogačar attacked on the Peyresourde and gained around 40 seconds on the yellow-jersey group. Thibaut Pinot, suffering from back pain, lost almost 19 minutes, while Adam Yates kept yellow by 3 seconds.
