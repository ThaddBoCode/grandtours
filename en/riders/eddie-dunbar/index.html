In the headlines

Vuelta 2026 · Stage 19

Dunbar wins at Peñas Blancas, Mas stays in control

Eddie Dunbar (Pinarello–Q36.5) won from the breakaway at Peñas Blancas in 5:00:54, passing Santiago Buitrago about 500 m from the line. Buitrago was second at 0:14 and Thomas Gloag third at 0:23. The general classification was unchanged: Mas led Roglič (1:37) and Gall (3:01).

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 11

Dunbar springs a surprise 500 m out – O'Connor loses 37 seconds

Eddie Dunbar (Jayco–AlUla) won on the Padrón loop from a leading group of 14 by attacking 500 metres from the line; Quinten Hermans was second (+0:02). In the peloton Roglič and Mas dropped red jersey Ben O'Connor, who lost 37 seconds. His lead over Roglič shrank to 3:16; Carlos Rodríguez took the white jersey.

 Vuelta 2024 · Stage 20

Dunbar wins for the second time, Vine takes the polka-dot jersey

On the penultimate stage Eddie Dunbar left the red jersey group less than five kilometres from the top of the Picón Blanco, passed Pavel Sivakov and won ahead of Enric Mas (+0:07) and Roglič (+0:10). Roglič now led O'Connor by 2:02 and Mas by 2:11. Jay Vine took the polka-dot jersey from Marc Soler.

 Vuelta 2026

Breakaways decide five of the hardest stages

Andreas Leknessund (stage 7), Jakob Omrzel (12), Marco Brenner (14), Eddie Dunbar (19) and Mikel Landa (20) all won tough stages from the breakaway. Landa won the queen stage solo at the Collado del Alguacil.

 Giro 2023 · Stage 16

Almeida outduels Thomas on Monte Bondone, Thomas back in pink

João Almeida won atop Monte Bondone ahead of Geraint Thomas on the same time, with Primož Roglič and Eddie Dunbar 25 seconds behind. Thomas took back the maglia rosa, 18 seconds ahead of Almeida and 29 ahead of Roglič, and Ben Healy took the maglia azzurra.
