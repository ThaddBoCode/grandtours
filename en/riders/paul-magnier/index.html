In the headlines

Giro 2026

Magnier takes three stages and the maglia ciclamino

France's Paul Magnier won at Burgas, Sofia and Pieve di Soligo and briefly wore the maglia rosa after stage 1. From stage 14 he duelled for the points lead with three-time stage winner Jhonatan Narváez, the jersey changing hands four times; when Narváez abandoned on stage 19, the maglia ciclamino was all but Magnier's.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 1

Magnier sprints to the first maglia rosa in Burgas

The almost flat opening stage from Nessebar to Burgas crossed Cape Agalina twice. Paul Magnier (Soudal–Quick-Step) won the sprint of an eleven-man group ahead of Tobias Lund Andresen and Ethan Vernon, taking the maglia rosa, maglia ciclamino and maglia bianca with a 4-second lead over Lund Andresen. Diego Pablo Sevilla took the first maglia azzurra.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 3

Magnier again: sprint victory in Sofia

The last Bulgarian stage crossed the Borovets Pass, its only climb, on the way to the capital. Paul Magnier won his second sprint ahead of Jonathan Milan and Dylan Groenewegen, strengthening his hold on the maglia ciclamino, while Guillermo Thomas Silva stayed in pink. Adam Yates and Andrea Vendrame did not start after the previous day's crash.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 18

Magnier wins in Pieve di Soligo and regains ciclamino

The sprinters survived the day's only climb, a very steep one, and fought out the win among themselves. Paul Magnier took his third stage victory ahead of Edoardo Zambanini and Jonathan Milan, winning back the maglia ciclamino from Jhonatan Narváez, while Jonas Vingegaard stayed 4:03 ahead of Felix Gall.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 6

Last-corner crash in Naples hands Ballerini the win

A few hundred metres from the finish in Naples, the Unibet Rose Rockets lead-out crashed on wet cobbles approaching the final corner, disrupting the sprint. Only Davide Ballerini and Jasper Stuyven, on the inside of the bend, were unaffected – Ballerini won, with Paul Magnier recovering to third.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 15

Breakaway survives: Dversnes surprises in Milan

The flat stage to Milan looked made for the sprinters, but a four-man breakaway held on: Fredrik Dversnes (Uno-X Mobility) won ahead of Mirco Maestri, Martin Marcellusi and Mattia Bais, with the bunch 57 seconds behind. Paul Magnier, fifth, took back the maglia ciclamino, and Jonas Vingegaard stayed in pink.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 21

Milan sprints to victory in Rome as Vingegaard celebrates

Jonathan Milan (Lidl–Trek) won the bunch sprint on the final stage in Rome ahead of Giovanni Lonardi and Paul Penhoët. Jonas Vingegaard won the Giro by 5:22 over Felix Gall and 6:25 over Jai Hindley, with Paul Magnier taking the maglia ciclamino, Giulio Ciccone the maglia azzurra and Afonso Eulálio the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 6

Groves wins in Naples after a crash and neutralisation

The 227 km stage to Naples, the longest of the race, was neutralised between km 156 and 166.5 after a mass crash; Jai Hindley, Juri Hollmann and Dion Smith abandoned. No points or bonus seconds were awarded at the last intermediate sprint and the finish, and the time gaps did not count for the general classification. Kaden Groves won the sprint ahead of Milan Fretin and Paul Magnier.
