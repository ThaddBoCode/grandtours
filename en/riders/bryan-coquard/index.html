In the headlines

Vuelta 2026 · Stage 8

Pogačar crashes out, Coquard wins, Mas takes the red jersey

Tadej Pogačar crashed about 30 km from the finish, could not continue because of his injuries and left the race. Bryan Coquard won the bunch sprint in 3:47:06 ahead of Mads Pedersen and Jordi Meeus, taking his first Grand Tour stage win. Enric Mas inherited the red jersey, leading Roglič by 1:00 and Gall by 1:11.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 11

Brennan wins his third stage in Lorca

Matthew Brennan won the sprint in Lorca in 3:17:18 ahead of Bryan Coquard and Jordi Meeus, led out by Wout van Aert. It was his third stage win of this Vuelta. The top of the general classification was unchanged: Mas led Roglič (1:18) and Gall (1:39).

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 16

Brennan takes his fourth stage in Palos de la Frontera

Matthew Brennan won the sprint in Palos de la Frontera in 3:56:33 ahead of Bryan Coquard and Vito Braet. It was his fourth stage win of this Vuelta. Mas stayed in the red jersey, with Gall second at 2:06.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 13

Pedersen wins in Montilla

Mads Pedersen passed Pascal Ackermann, who had launched an early sprint on the uphill drag to Montilla, and took his first stage win of this Vuelta, ahead of Bryan Coquard and Ackermann. Juan Ayuso had tested positive for COVID-19 before the stage but stayed in the race after a PCR test showed a low risk of infectivity. Remco Evenepoel kept the red jersey.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 9

O'Connor triumphs at Tignes as Roglič and van der Poel quit

Primož Roglič and Mathieu van der Poel did not start. Ben O'Connor went clear from a 41-rider breakaway on the final climb to Tignes and won 5:07 ahead of Mattia Cattaneo, while Tadej Pogačar dropped his rivals near the top and gained 32 seconds. O'Connor moved up to second overall, 2:01 behind Pogačar, Nairo Quintana took the polka-dot jersey, and seven riders, including Arnaud Démare and Bryan Coquard, missed the time cut.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 7

Van Aert wins again as crosswinds catch out Pogačar

Bora–Hansgrohe set a fierce pace from the start to drop Sam Bennett and other sprinters, and later INEOS Grenadiers used the crosswinds to split the peloton. Wout van Aert won his second stage in three days ahead of Edvald Boasson Hagen and Bryan Coquard. Tadej Pogačar, Mikel Landa, Richie Porte and Bauke Mollema were caught behind and lost 1:21; Peter Sagan took back green and Egan Bernal the white jersey.
