In the headlines

Giro 2025 · Stage 2

Tarling wins the Tirana time trial, Roglič takes pink by a second

Joshua Tarling won the 13.7 km time trial in Tirana by one second from Primož Roglič, with Jay Vine third at 3 seconds. Roglič took the maglia rosa, one second ahead of Mads Pedersen, and Mathias Vacek moved into the maglia bianca.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 3

Pedersen sprints to victory in Vlorë and reclaims the maglia rosa

Lorenzo Fortunato and Pello Bilbao crossed the Llogara pass together in front but were caught 17 km from the finish in Vlorë. Led out by Giulio Ciccone and Mathias Vacek, Mads Pedersen won the sprint ahead of Corbin Strong and Orluis Aular and took back the maglia rosa, 9 seconds ahead of Roglič. Fortunato moved into the maglia azzurra.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 13

Pedersen holds off van Aert uphill in Vicenza for his fourth win

The finish in Vicenza climbed to the sanctuary of Monte Berico, with ramps of up to 12 %. Romain Bardet and Mathias Vacek were caught 500 m from the line, and maglia ciclamino Mads Pedersen launched his sprint from distance to hold off Wout van Aert for his fourth stage win, with del Toro third, 2 seconds back. Del Toro's lead over Ayuso grew to 38 seconds.
