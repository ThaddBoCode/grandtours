In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 20

Carapaz wins the queen stage after Kuss crashes twice

On the queen stage over the Croix de Fer, Télégraphe, Galibier and Sarenne, Sepp Kuss led alone over the Col de Sarenne but crashed twice in the finale, and Carapaz passed him to win at Alpe d'Huez ahead of Evenepoel and Kuss, securing the polka-dot jersey. Pogačar rode for del Toro, who dropped Seixas to defend third overall and the white jersey; Seixas lost 1:50 but held on to fourth. Pedersen made the green jersey mathematically safe at the intermediate sprint.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 19

Kuss wins the queen stage, Hindley moves onto the podium

On the Dolomites stage over six climbs, including the Passo Giau as the roof of the race, Sepp Kuss (Visma–Lease a Bike) won from the breakaway at Alleghe, 13 seconds ahead of Derek Gee-West and 36 ahead of Giulio Ciccone, who took the maglia azzurra. Thymen Arensman cracked and lost third place to Jai Hindley, and Jhonatan Narváez abandoned.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 18

Pogačar crashes, Vingegaard waits – then wins at Hautacam

On the last mountain stage Pogačar attacked repeatedly on the Col de Spandelles but could not drop Vingegaard. On the descent Pogačar crashed and the yellow jersey waited for him; on Hautacam, Sepp Kuss and Wout van Aert set the pace before Vingegaard rode away about 4 km from the top. He won by 1:04, extended his lead to 3:26 and took the polka-dot jersey.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 15

Kuss wins in Andorra, Poels back in polka dots

From a 32-rider breakaway, Sepp Kuss attacked 5 km from the top of the Col de Beixalis and won his first Tour stage in Andorra la Vella, 23 seconds ahead of Alejandro Valverde. Nairo Quintana was first over the Port d'Envalira, the highest point of the race, and Wout Poels took back the polka-dot jersey. Pogačar stayed in yellow 5:18 ahead of Rigoberto Urán, while Guillaume Martin lost almost four minutes and dropped to ninth.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 9

Roglič and Jumbo-Visma rule the race – until the time trial

Primož Roglič took the yellow jersey in Laruns on stage 9, the first Slovenian to wear it, and kept it for eleven stages. Jumbo-Visma controlled the mountains with Wout van Aert, Sepp Kuss, George Bennett and Tom Dumoulin setting the pace, and after the Col de la Loze Roglič led Pogačar by 57 seconds – before losing everything in the final time trial.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 4

Roglič wins the first summit finish at Orcières-Merlette

Jumbo-Visma controlled the race's first summit finish, with Sepp Kuss setting a fierce tempo in the closing kilometres. Primož Roglič chased down Guillaume Martin's late attack and won ahead of Tadej Pogačar and Martin. Julian Alaphilippe finished fifth to keep yellow, and Pogačar took the white jersey.
