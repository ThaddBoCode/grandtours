In the headlines

Giro 2026 · Stage 2

Thomas Silva: first Uruguayan to win a stage and wear pink

Guillermo Thomas Silva (XDS Astana) won the reduced sprint at Veliko Tarnovo ahead of Florian Stork and Giulio Ciccone, after Vingegaard had countered an attack by Christian Scaroni on the final climb. He became the first Uruguayan to win a Giro stage and wear the maglia rosa, which he kept for two days.

 Giro 2026 · Stage 14

Vingegaard wins at Pila and takes the maglia rosa

On the short mountain stage around Aosta, Visma–Lease a Bike set up Jonas Vingegaard, who won solo at Pila for his third stage victory, 49 seconds ahead of Felix Gall and 58 ahead of Jai Hindley. Afonso Eulálio resisted for a long time but had to surrender the maglia rosa and now trailed by 2:26. Jhonatan Narváez took the maglia ciclamino, and Christian Scaroni abandoned.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 16

Fortunato takes the maglia azzurra and the combativity award

Lorenzo Fortunato (XDS Astana Team) led the mountains classification from stage 3 to Rome and won it with 355 points ahead of teammate Christian Scaroni (201); he was also named the race's most combative rider. On stage 16 the two rode clear together to San Valentino, where Fortunato left the win to Scaroni.

 Giro 2025 · Stage 15

Verona wins alone in Asiago, Roglič loses time

Carlos Verona attacked from the break on the climb of Dori, 43 km from the finish, and won alone in Asiago, 22 seconds ahead of Florian Stork and 23 ahead of Christian Scaroni. Primož Roglič finished almost two minutes behind the winner and dropped to tenth overall, 3:53 down. Del Toro kept the maglia rosa, 1:20 ahead of Simon Yates.
