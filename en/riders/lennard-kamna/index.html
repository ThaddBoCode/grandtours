In the headlines

Vuelta 2023 · Stage 9

Kämna wins from the break, GC times taken 2.05 km out

Eight escapees still had around five minutes on the final climb in rain and wind. Because of mud on the road the GC time was taken 2.05 km from the finish; Lennard Kämna shook off his companions and won ahead of Matteo Sobrero and Chris Hamilton. The favourites finished together and Kuss stayed in red, 43 seconds ahead of Marc Soler.

 Vuelta 2023 · Stage 15

Rui Costa passes Kämna on the line in Lekunberri

Ten riders, including Remco Evenepoel, formed the break. Rui Costa and Santiago Buitrago attacked 14 km from the finish and Lennard Kämna rejoined them despite crashing on the descent. In Lekunberri Costa edged Kämna and Buitrago on the line, with the red-jersey group arriving 2:52 behind; Kuss stayed leader.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 10

Cort wins a photo finish in Megève, Kämna comes close to yellow

Climate protesters on the road forced the race to stop, after which it was restarted with the previous gaps. From a 25-rider breakaway, Magnus Cort beat Nick Schultz in a photo finish at the Megève altiport. Lennard Kämna, who was also in the move, rose to second overall, just 11 seconds behind Pogačar.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 16

Kämna makes up for Puy Mary with a solo win at Villard-de-Lans

A breakaway of more than 20 riders built a lead of over 12 minutes, and Richard Carapaz's repeated accelerations on the climb to Saint-Nizier-du-Moucherotte broke it apart. Lennard Kämna attacked just before the summit and won alone, 1:27 ahead of Carapaz and 1:56 ahead of Sébastien Reichenbach. Apart from Tom Dumoulin and Nairo Quintana, the top ten finished together and the first eight places overall were unchanged.

 Vuelta 2023 · Stage 3

Evenepoel wins in Arinsal and takes the red jersey

Eleven riders formed the day’s break, but Lennard Kämna was caught shortly after going solo on the final climb to Arinsal. From a group of favourites Remco Evenepoel attacked 250 m from the line and won one second ahead of Jonas Vingegaard and Juan Ayuso. Evenepoel took the red jersey, 5 seconds ahead of Enric Mas, and also led the young rider classification.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 7

Pogačar edges Vingegaard on La Super Planche des Belles Filles

On the first summit finish, Vingegaard attacked on the gravel in the final 200 m and only Pogačar could follow; the race leader came back past him to win, after both had overtaken the last breakaway rider, Lennard Kämna, close to the line. Roglič was third at 12 seconds, and Pogačar's lead grew to 35 seconds over Vingegaard, with Geraint Thomas third overall at 1:10.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 16

Houle solos to his first Grand Tour stage win in Foix

Hugo Houle attacked from the breakaway on the descent of the Port de Lers, 38 km from the finish, and won alone, 1:10 ahead of Valentin Madouas and his teammate Michael Woods. Pogačar attacked three times around the top of the Port de Lers but could not shake off Vingegaard, who kept his lead of 2:22. Lennard Kämna and Jakob Fuglsang did not start.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 20

Hindley breaks Carapaz on the Marmolada

Carapaz and Hindley were separated by just three seconds after stage 16 and stayed inseparable until the penultimate stage. On the steep final kilometres of the Passo Fedaia, Hindley attacked, linked up with teammate Lennard Kämna and dropped Carapaz inside the last 3 km, taking the maglia rosa with a lead of 1:25 that he defended in the Verona time trial.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 4

Juan Pedro López wears pink for ten days

Juan Pedro López attacked from the breakaway on Etna and lost the two-man sprint to Lennard Kämna, but took the maglia rosa. The Trek–Segafredo rider held it for ten days, saved it on the Blockhaus by just 12 seconds, lost it in Turin and finished tenth overall – winning the maglia bianca as best young rider.
