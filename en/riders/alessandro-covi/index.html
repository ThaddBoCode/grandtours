In the headlines

Giro 2022 · Stage 20

Covi wins on the Fedaia, Hindley takes the maglia rosa

Alessandro Covi attacked from the breakaway on the Passo Pordoi, took the Cima Coppi and held off Domen Novak by 32 seconds on the Passo Fedaia for his first Grand Tour stage win. Behind, Jai Hindley attacked inside the final 5 km and, with Lennard Kämna setting the pace, dropped Carapaz within the last 3 km; Hindley took the maglia rosa with a lead of 1:25.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 11

Schmid wins on the gravel to Montalcino

On a stage with four gravel sectors, Mauro Schmid beat Alessandro Covi in a two-up sprint for his first professional win. Behind them Egan Bernal attacked to finish ahead of all the favourites, while Remco Evenepoel struggled on the gravel and lost about two minutes; Aleksandr Vlasov rose to second overall at 45 seconds.
