In the headlines

Vuelta 2021 · Stage 3

Taaramäe wins at Picón Blanco and takes red

Rein Taaramäe dropped his companions from the breakaway on the first summit finish and won 21 seconds ahead of Joe Dombrowski. He took both the red jersey and the mountains lead, with Kenny Elissonde third at 36".

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 4

Jakobsen wins and takes green; Taaramäe survives crash

Fabio Jakobsen outsprinted Arnaud Démare for the win and took the green jersey from Philipsen. Red jersey Taaramäe crashed 2.2 km from the line but, inside the final 3 km, was credited with the peloton's time and kept the lead.

 Vuelta 2022 · Stage 8

Vine wins again at Colláu Fancuaya, Pedersen takes green

Jay Vine won his second stage from the break after dropping his companions 6.2 km out; Marc Soler was second at 43 seconds, level with Rein Taaramäe, and Thibaut Pinot fourth at 47 seconds. Vine took the polka-dot jersey, and Mads Pedersen the green jersey at the intermediate sprint. Evenepoel, Mas and Roglič finished 1:20 behind Vine, and the overall order stayed the same.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 5

Philipsen wins in Albacete; Elissonde takes red

Jasper Philipsen won the sprint ahead of Jakobsen and Alberto Dainese, after a big crash 11 km from the finish had caught Romain Bardet and Taaramäe among others. Kenny Elissonde took the red jersey, five seconds ahead of Roglič.
