In the headlines

Tour 2027

Pogačar chases a sixth win – it would be a record

With five wins he is level with Anquetil, Merckx, Hinault and Induráin. A sixth would put him alone at the top of Tour history.

 Giro 2027

Pogačar, Vingegaard, Seixas: who will ride?

Pogačar has named the Tour, the Worlds and the Vuelta as his 2027 goals, though he says he would rather ride the Giro; defending champion Vingegaard's programme is still open. Paul Seixas is considering a Giro debut, and Bahrain have Antonio Tiberi on their Giro programme for now. Several decisions are due at the teams' December training camps.

 Tour 2026

Pogačar wins his fifth Tour, equalling the record

Tadej Pogačar won the Tour for the fifth time and the third year in a row, joining Jacques Anquetil, Eddy Merckx, Bernard Hinault and Miguel Induráin as the only five-time winners. He won five stages and finished 6:26 ahead of Remco Evenepoel.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 6

Pogačar's solo on the Tourmalet puts him in yellow

On stage 6 Pogačar went clear alone on the Col du Tourmalet, 43 km from the finish, and won at Gavarnie-Gèdre by 2:38 over Jonas Vingegaard – his largest stage-winning margin at the Tour. He kept the yellow jersey all the way to Paris, extending his lead with further wins at Le Lioran, Le Markstein and Alpe d'Huez.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 2

Del Toro wins on Montjuïc with Pogačar's help

UAE Team Emirates XRG whittled down the peloton with a fast pace on three laps of the Montjuïc circuit, and in the uphill sprint Tadej Pogačar made sure his teammate Isaac del Toro won – the 22-year-old's first Tour stage victory. Remco Evenepoel was third ahead of Vingegaard, who kept yellow but saw his lead over Pogačar cut to 6 seconds. Del Toro also took the green jersey and kept the white one.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 3

Pogačar wins at Les Angles and takes yellow

Because of wildfires in the Pyrénées-Orientales, spectators were discouraged from attending in France and there were none at the finish. On the final climb del Toro attacked with 300 metres to go to set up Pogačar, who won 2 seconds ahead of Vingegaard, Richard Carapaz and Paul Seixas. Pogačar took the yellow jersey from Vingegaard, level on time, while Alex Baudin took the polka-dot jersey.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 10

Pogačar strikes again on Bastille Day

Richard Carapaz attacked on the Pas de Peyrol, but on the Col du Pertus Pogačar attacked about 15 km from the finish, passed Carapaz before the summit and rode alone to Le Lioran for his third stage win. His lead over Vingegaard grew to 3:36, and Evenepoel's sprint for the line, 32 seconds down, lifted him to third overall. Del Toro struggled on the Pertus, dropped from third to seventh and lost the white jersey to Juan Ayuso.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 14

Pogačar attacks on the Col du Haag for a fourth win

Pogačar attacked 1.5 km from the top of the Col du Haag, the last climb, and extended his lead on the plateau to the finish at Le Markstein for his fourth stage win. Del Toro and Seixas finished 38 seconds behind and Vingegaard, who had worked with Seixas to limit the losses, 44 seconds back, leaving Pogačar 4:30 ahead overall. Seixas took the white jersey from Ayuso and moved up to fourth.

 Tour 2026 · Stage 19

Pogačar breaks Pantani's Alpe d'Huez record

Pogačar went clear alone on the climb to Alpe d'Huez, about 12 km from the finish, picked off the breakaway riders one by one, caught Carapaz and Lenny Martinez 1.7 km from the line and won solo – his fifth stage win of the race. His time beat the record for the climb set by Marco Pantani in 1995. Carapaz took the polka-dot jersey from Pogačar by one point, and del Toro stayed third overall, 24 seconds ahead of Seixas.
