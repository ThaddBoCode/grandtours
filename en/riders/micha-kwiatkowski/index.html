In the headlines

Tour 2023 · Stage 13

Kwiatkowski conquers the Grand Colombier on Bastille Day

On the Bastille Day summit finish Michał Kwiatkowski went clear from the breakaway 11 km from the finish and won 47 seconds ahead of Maxim Van Gils. Pogačar was third, four seconds ahead of Vingegaard, and with the bonus seconds cut the gap to 9 seconds. Caleb Ewan abandoned during the stage.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 18

Kwiatkowski and Carapaz cross the line together for INEOS

Michał Kwiatkowski and Richard Carapaz went clear from a large breakaway and rode in together, Carapaz leaving the stage win to his teammate – Kwiatkowski's first individual Grand Tour stage win – while taking the polka-dot jersey himself. Marc Hirschi crashed on a descent but was named the day's most combative rider. Mikel Landa attacked on the Plateau des Glières and Richie Porte punctured on the gravel section that followed, but the top four overall were unchanged.

 Tour 2024 · Stage 18

Campenaerts outsprints his breakaway rivals in Barcelonnette

A breakaway of more than 30 riders went clear early, and on the last climb Michał Kwiatkowski attacked, later joined by Mattéo Vercher and Victor Campenaerts. Campenaerts won the three-man sprint in Barcelonnette ahead of Vercher and Kwiatkowski for his first Tour stage win. The yellow jersey group finished more than 13 minutes down, with no change in the overall standings.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 15

Defending champion Bernal cracks and leaves the race

Egan Bernal was second overall after stage 9, but on the Grand Colombier he was dropped early and lost 7:20, ending INEOS Grenadiers' hopes of a sixth consecutive Tour win; he did not start stage 17. INEOS responded on stage 18 with a one-two for Michał Kwiatkowski and Richard Carapaz at La Roche-sur-Foron.
