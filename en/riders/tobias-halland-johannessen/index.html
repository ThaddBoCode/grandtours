In the headlines

Tour 2026 · Stage 9

Van der Poel wins the shortened heatwave stage to Ussel

Because of a red heatwave warning for the Corrèze department, the stage was shortened by 30.9 km and raced in temperatures of up to 40 °C. Mathieu van der Poel attacked from an eight-man breakaway on Mont Bessou, with only Tobias Halland Johannessen and Tom Pidcock able to follow and Alex Baudin joining on the descent. Van der Poel won the sprint six seconds ahead of the peloton – in the Limousin, home region of his grandfather Raymond Poulidor – and the GC was unchanged.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 7

Leknessund wins solo from the breakaway at Valdelinares

Andreas Leknessund, in the breakaway from kilometre zero, won solo at the Aramón Valdelinares summit in 3:49:47, 0:34 ahead of Tobias Halland Johannessen. It was the first stage win of this Vuelta for a breakaway rider. Pogačar kept the red jersey and Mas stayed second overall at 3:50.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 20

Landa wins the queen stage, Mas defends the red jersey

Mikel Landa won solo at the Collado del Alguacil in 4:58:01 after dropping the other escapees, 0:47 ahead of Tobias Halland Johannessen. Felix Gall attacked on the final climb and took back time, but the podium stayed the same: Mas ahead of Roglič (2:15) and Gall (2:44). Sepp Kuss moved up to fifth overall.

 Vuelta 2026 · Stage 21

Johannessen wins in Granada, Mas is crowned Vuelta winner

Tobias Halland Johannessen won the short final stage in Granada in 2:54:08, beating Alessandro Romele in a two-man sprint. Times were frozen 10 km from the finish, so the general classification leaders did not contest the stage. Enric Mas won the Vuelta by 2:15 over Roglič and 2:44 over Gall.

 Tour 2025 · Stage 11

Abrahamsen outsprints Schmid in Toulouse, Pogačar crashes unhurt

Jonas Abrahamsen and Mauro Schmid held off the chasing Mathieu van der Poel by 7 seconds, and Abrahamsen won the two-up sprint for his and Uno-X Mobility's first Grand Tour stage. Tadej Pogačar crashed in the finale after contact with Tobias Halland Johannessen, but the GC group waited and Ben Healy kept the yellow jersey.
