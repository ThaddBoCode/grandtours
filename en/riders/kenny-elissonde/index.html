In the headlines

Vuelta 2021 · Stage 5

Philipsen wins in Albacete; Elissonde takes red

Jasper Philipsen won the sprint ahead of Jakobsen and Alberto Dainese, after a big crash 11 km from the finish had caught Romain Bardet and Taaramäe among others. Kenny Elissonde took the red jersey, five seconds ahead of Roglič.

 Tour 2021 · Stage 11

Van Aert conquers the double Ventoux

On the first Tour stage to climb Mont Ventoux twice, Wout van Aert went clear from the breakaway on the second ascent and soloed to Malaucène, 1:14 ahead of Kenny Elissonde and Bauke Mollema. Behind, Jonas Vingegaard dropped Tadej Pogačar near the summit, but Pogačar, Richard Carapaz and Rigoberto Urán caught him near the flamme rouge. Urán moved up to second, 5:18 down, while Ben O'Connor lost about four minutes and David Gaudu more than 25.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 3

Taaramäe wins at Picón Blanco and takes red

Rein Taaramäe dropped his companions from the breakaway on the first summit finish and won 21 seconds ahead of Joe Dombrowski. He took both the red jersey and the mountains lead, with Kenny Elissonde third at 36".

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 6

Cort holds off Roglič; Roglič retakes red

Magnus Cort dropped his breakaway companions inside the final kilometre and held on up the ramp to Cullera, with Roglič only bridging up at the line for the same time in second. Elissonde lost four and a half minutes, so Roglič took the red jersey back.
