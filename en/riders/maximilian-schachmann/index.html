In the headlines

Giro 2024 · Stage 1

Narváez beats Pogačar on the opening day in Turin

Pogačar attacked on the final climb as early as stage 1, but Jhonatan Narváez and Maximilian Schachmann went with him, and Narváez won the three-man sprint in Turin. The Ecuadorian thus wore the first maglia rosa of the race before Pogačar took it the next day.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 13

Martínez wins on Puy Mary as the Slovenians break clear

From a 17-rider breakaway, Daniel Martínez beat Lennard Kämna by 4 seconds on the steep climb to Puy Mary, with Maximilian Schachmann third. Behind, Tadej Pogačar and Primož Roglič broke clear of the favourites on the Pas de Peyrol; Pogačar moved up to second overall, 44 seconds down, and retook the white jersey, while Egan Bernal lost 38 seconds and fell to third. Bauke Mollema abandoned after a crash, and Romain Bardet, concussed in a fall, did not start the next day.
