In the headlines

Giro 2024 · Stage 10

Paret-Peintre passes Tratnik to win at Cusano Mutri

Jan Tratnik went clear alone from a 26-rider breakaway and started the 17.9 km final climb to Bocca della Selva with a 50-second lead. Valentin Paret-Peintre caught and dropped him to win by 29 seconds over Romain Bardet, with Tratnik third. Nothing changed at the top of the standings; Olav Kooij did not start.

 Giro 2020 · Stage 16

Tratnik wins in San Daniele del Friuli

A 28-rider break went clear from the start, including mountains rivals Giovanni Visconti and Ruben Guerreiro. Jan Tratnik dropped Ben O'Connor on the climb to the finish and won by seven seconds, while João Almeida attacked in the last few hundred metres to gain two seconds on his rivals.

 Giro 2022 · Stage 3

Cavendish takes his 16th Giro stage win in Balatonfüred

Bais and Tagliani were in the breakaway again, this time with Samuele Rivi, while Jan Tratnik became the first rider to abandon after his crash on stage 1. Led out by Michael Mørkøv, Mark Cavendish opened his sprint 300 m out and held off Arnaud Démare and Fernando Gaviria; van der Poel stayed in pink ahead of the first rest day and Rick Zabel took the maglia azzurra.

 Giro 2021 · Stage 14

Fortunato conquers the Zoncolan

Lorenzo Fortunato (Eolo–Kometa) bridged across to Jan Tratnik on Monte Zoncolan, dropped him on the steep section and won by 26 seconds for his first professional victory. Egan Bernal followed Simon Yates' attack and then dropped him in the final kilometre to lead Yates by 1:33, while Remco Evenepoel lost more than a minute and a half.

 Vuelta 2021 · Stage 1

Roglič opens in red with the Burgos time trial

Defending champion Primož Roglič won the 7.1 km time trial in Burgos in 8' 32" as the last rider off the ramp and took the first red jersey. Alex Aranburu, who sat in the hot seat for most of the day, was second at 6", Jan Tratnik third at 8".
