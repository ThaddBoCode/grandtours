In the headlines

Tour 2023 · Stage 20

Ciccone takes the polka-dot jersey, Italy's first since 1992

After Neilson Powless, Felix Gall and Vingegaard had all led the mountains classification, Giulio Ciccone took over on stage 15 and sealed it in the Vosges on stage 20, finishing with 106 points to Gall's 92. He was the first Italian to win the classification since Claudio Chiappucci in 1992.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 1

Yates twins one-two in Bilbao, Adam takes yellow

Adam Yates won the opening stage around Bilbao four seconds ahead of his twin brother Simon after the pair broke away from the favourites in the finale. Pogačar led the chasers home 12 seconds down; Adam Yates took the yellow and green jerseys and Neilson Powless the polka-dot jersey. Enric Mas abandoned after a crash.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 3

Philipsen opens his account in Bayonne

After a two-man breakaway by Neilson Powless and Laurent Pichon, Jasper Philipsen won the sprint into Bayonne ahead of Phil Bauhaus and Caleb Ewan following a strong lead-out by Mathieu van der Poel. The commissaires reviewed his sprint duel with Wout van Aert but let the result stand. Adam Yates kept yellow and Powless added to his mountains lead.

 Tour 2023 · Stage 6

Pogačar wins at Cauterets, Vingegaard takes yellow

Jumbo–Visma's pace on the Col du Tourmalet left only Pogačar with Vingegaard, and Wout van Aert dropped back from the breakaway to help his leader. On the final climb to Cambasque Pogačar attacked less than 3 km from the finish and won by 24 seconds. Vingegaard took yellow with a 25-second lead, Hindley dropped to third at 1:34 and Powless regained the polka-dot jersey.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 5

Clarke wins on the cobbles, Pogačar profits as Roglič crashes

Simon Clarke narrowly beat Taco van der Hoorn in the sprint of the breakaway at Arenberg. Behind, Vingegaard was slowed by a mechanical, Roglič crashed and dislocated his shoulder and Jack Haig abandoned, while Pogačar attacked with Jasper Stuyven and gained time on all his rivals. Van Aert kept yellow, 13 seconds ahead of Neilson Powless.

 Tour 2022 · Stage 6

Pogačar wins the uphill sprint in Longwy and takes yellow

On the longest stage of the race, van Aert spent much of the day in the breakaway wearing yellow but was caught 11.1 km from the finish and dropped. Pogačar won the uphill sprint in Longwy ahead of Michael Matthews and David Gaudu and took the yellow jersey, 4 seconds ahead of Neilson Powless and 31 ahead of Vingegaard.

 Tour 2020 · Stage 6

Lutsenko solos to victory on Mont Aigoual

An eight-man breakaway was allowed to fight out the stage, and Kazakh champion Alexey Lutsenko dropped Neilson Powless on the Col de la Lusette, a climb new to the Tour, to win alone. Jesús Herrada was second at 55 seconds and Greg Van Avermaet third at 2:15. The favourites finished together almost three minutes down, and Adam Yates kept yellow by 3 seconds over Primož Roglič.
